# Public Corrections Policy — Historical Newspaper Archives

**Project:** Historical Newspaper Archives  
**Research path:** `D:\news`  
**Status:** Adopted  

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how corrections, review history, and source evidence are handled on the Historical Newspaper Archives website. It exists so that readers can trust what they see, know when something changes, and have a safe route to report a problem.

---

## 2. What is shown on story pages

Every public story page displays:

- The **most recent reviewed date** for that story.
- A distinction between **newly added evidence** and **corrections to prior interpretations**.
- The **evidence tier** (A/B/C or early P/I/N) for each source, and whether a curated inconsistency flag (V/R/P/M/F/Q) applies.
- Honest source/viewer links and research states when article scans are not available — never fabricated clippings.

---

## 3. Corrections and review history

- When an interpretation changes, the prior published interpretation is **preserved for audit** and not silently overwritten.
- Corrections are dated and describe what changed and why.
- Source evidence (original URLs, batch-report pointers, acquisition status) is preserved verbatim; original source files under `D:\news` are never modified.
- Private research notes are **never** exposed in public builds.

---

## 4. Corrections / contact route

A visible, safe route for corrections and questions is provided on the Methodology page. Reports are reviewed against the primary source before any change is published.

---

## 5. What is not corrected

- The original newspaper text is historical evidence; it is reproduced as found (including dated language, outdated figures, or unconfirmed claims) and is **not** edited to match later understanding.
- A newspaper report records that a publication made a claim; it does **not** establish the claim as fact. Corrections address errors in our presentation or indexing, not disagreements with historical reporting.
- Source URLs are preserved exactly as observed in the seed queue, even if a remote URL later breaks; redirects are normalized only in a separate canonical-link field.

---

## 6. Deployment

No live deployment has been authorized. A later possible path (`darkstarconsultinggroup.com/newspaper-archives`) is not an established deployment target. Corrections policy applies before any publication.
