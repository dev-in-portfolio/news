## Counting and confidence rules

- **Grade A:** An independent period *newspaper article, editorial or column* was opened and its substantive body read. The source's relevance and any limitation are recorded below.
- **Grade B:** A dated period newspaper title or article was located, but its newspaper-hosted body could not be directly examined. Search excerpts and later reproductions do not make it Grade A.
- Each distinct **newspaper title** counts at most once **per seed**, even if multiple articles from that paper were read. The original Indiana seed is never counted as independent. News agencies, later retrospectives, magazines, organizational reports, news broadcasts, and official releases are excluded from the numerical leads.
- A **context-only** lead can establish the wider event or contemporary environment without corroborating a particular named person, local gathering or allegation. Such sources are explicitly tagged and must not be presented as proof of that particular claim.
- Dates and numbers reflect what the *period sources reported at the time*, not a finalized modern estimate. Different estimates are not merged. Assertions and allegations are attributed.
- Direct opening of the original Indiana-hosted seed pages **timed out** in this session. Titles, dates and seed IDs below are those already present in the tracker, not newly verified page transcriptions.

### HN-878 — FBI false Madrid fingerprint identification

**Finding:** The misidentification was publicly corrected by May 2004. The June seed is not the date of the underlying error.
