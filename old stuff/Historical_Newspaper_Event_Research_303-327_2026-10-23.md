## What the evidence codes mean

- **A — identifiable contemporary newspaper article with text:** newspaper title and exact issue date, with report-bearing page OCR, issue PDF, contemporaneous transcription, or substantial original article text visible. A source may OCR imperfectly.
- **B — identifiable contemporary newspaper article, text still to inspect:** issue title/date and story-specific headline or reference are identifiable, but the article text was not checked in full.
- **C — uncounted lead:** wrong incident, ambiguous relation, retrospective-only source, unsupported headline or date. Leads in this class do **not** increase counts.
- Count each distinct **newspaper title + date** only once per packet, even if more than one page appears. Separate issue dates of the same newspaper can count. An official document, book or specialized professional journal is supplemental context, **not** an additional newspaper by default.

This is an **initial finding pass**, not a complete list of all publications, an event synthesis, or a finding that competing early reports were correct. Accident dates and newspaper issue dates differ; sensational assertions, suspected guilt and preliminary death tolls remain attributed to their reporting.

## Publication-by-publication discoveries

### EV-HN-303 — H. H. Holmes says Benjamin Pitezel killed himself (seed: August 9, 1895)

**No second issue verified for this precise August assertion.** There are abundant later and earlier Holmes/Pitezel newspaper articles, but identifying the same *particular statement* requires matching its wording and date, not simply finding another Holmes murder story. **C, excluded:** the [Library of Congress Holmes newspaper chronology](https://guides.loc.gov/chronicling-america-h-h-holmes) and [contemporary-item collection](https://asthepaperstoldit.com/h-h-holmes/archive/). Holmes's claim is an accused man's account, not proof of suicide.
