# Historical newspaper event research — article-finding pass EV-HN-353–377

**Date:** October 23, 2026  
**Scope:** 25 seeds in the existing 1,056-item research tracker, EV-HN-353 through EV-HN-377.  
**Purpose:** Find distinct contemporaneous newspaper appearances from different publications about each seed's event or specific evolving news angle. This is **discovery**, not a determination that the events occurred exactly as described in early dispatches, not a theory of concealment, and not an exhaustive completion claim.

## How to read this pass

- **A — readable primary appearance:** dated original newspaper page with inspectable OCR, scan/PDF, or substantial faithful transcription of its original article.
- **B — specific issue/article lead:** named newspaper and date with a relevant story identified by a catalog, newspaper index, periodical seller, or source guide, but its original page/article was not successfully inspected in this pass. **B is not a verified full-text recovery.**
- **Not counted:** the original Indiana seed; duplicate URLs to the same newspaper issue; general historical essays, unverified claims, and articles actually about a different event. A newspaper issue containing multiple related articles counts once as a publication/date appearance in these counts; full article-level enumeration is still open.
- Links lead as directly as possible to the archived issue or article. Some Library of Congress links are article-specific issue pointers, not evidence that full OCR was successfully opened; those remain **B**.
- The event date, first reported fatality estimates, accusations, and speculative causes are **contemporary assertions**, not findings we independently affirm.

## Tally and limitations

Each line above is a **new candidate appearance relative to the Indiana seed**; these were not compared against all previous discovery batches for duplicate *newspaper appearances* across different event IDs, because some entries intentionally describe different phases of the same saga. Within an event, count at most one appearance per named newspaper + issue date.

For B-stage LOC issue pointers, the article date, title and paper are documented by LOC's curated finding aid, but OCR/full-page opening was blocked. Those links are valuable **future retrieval targets**, not finished reviews.

Unconfirmed mastheads and misattributed issue links have been explicitly excluded, including the unverified *News* records under EV-HN-367 and EV-HN-370 and a mismatched *New York Times* pointer under EV-HN-375.

**Current conservative tally:** **75 A** readable newspaper appearances; **37 B** specifically identifiable issue/article leads; **112 total** across these 25 seeds. These numbers do not include the 25 original Indiana seeds.

This batch is an initial widening pass, **not the end of searching all newspapers**. Next seeds in source sequence: **EV-HN-378–402**.
