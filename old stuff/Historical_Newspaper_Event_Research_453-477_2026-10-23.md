# Historical Newspaper Event Research — HN-453 through HN-477

**Finding pass dated:** 2026-10-23  
**Events covered:** 25, May 1927–May 1932  
**Purpose:** Locate additional contemporaneous newspaper publication appearances for events already identified in the 1,056-discovery master. This is a *discovery/retrieval ledger*, not a historical conclusion, a comprehensive bibliography, or a claim to have recovered every article.

## Counting and source standard

- **A — article/page found:** An original newspaper issue/page or OCR/facsimile is independently linked and its connection to the event is identifiable in the archived page, headline, or indexed text. Multiple pages in the same issue/newspaper for the same event count as **one publication appearance** here.
- **B — retrieval lead:** A specific period newspaper issue/article is identified through a catalog, retrospective citation, dealer listing, or collection inventory, but the event-bearing source page has not been independently examined. B does **not** count as a retrieved article.
- **Context:** An archived newspaper article that addresses a nearby stage of a developing story but not the precise discovery's claim. These are flagged separately and not counted as an A match to that specific claim.
- The original Indiana newspaper references already recorded as the master *seed* for each HN number are **excluded from new-find counts**, even where original scans are available.
- Contemporary casualty figures, allegations, accusations, and press descriptions are preserved as *what a paper said at the time*, **not** automatically accepted as settled facts. Historical newspaper terminology may be racist or otherwise misleading.
- URL accessibility and OCR quality are uneven. Repeated wire-service text printed in **different newspaper publications** is a different publication appearance, not an independent eyewitness account.
