# Historical newspaper research — article-finding pass HN-403–427

**Date:** October 23, 2026  
**Scope:** 25 source rows, HN-403 through HN-427, from the unchanged 1,056-discovery master `Historical_Newspaper_Discoveries_1777-2026.md` (v26).

## Method and counting

This is an **initial, non-exhaustive finding pass**, seeking contemporary coverage in other publications and on different issue dates. It is not event synthesis, claim validation, or a claim that every newspaper was searched.

- **A — original-issue appearance located:** a dated original page, OCR, digitized issue/PDF, or a clear transcription of its contemporary article with the event visible in the accessible text or index extract. A does **not** certify a human transcription of the full article against page images.
- **B — specific publication lead, not read:** a newspaper, issue date, and subject identified from an index/bibliography but no inspectable text of the original article in this pass. **Do not count B as recovered articles.**
- One newspaper/issue is counted only once **within each HN row**; a reprinted wire story does not prove independent reporting. Original Indiana discovery seed links are **not counted**. Source rows that follow the same incident at different dates stay separate without asserting distinct events.
- Dated original-issue pages from search indexes still merit image/OCR double-check before article-level finalization. “A” here means found/readable appearance, not final verified transcription or exhaustive attribution.
- Early casualty totals, claims of sabotage, accusations, and racially prejudicial descriptions remain *attributed press claims*, not accepted findings.

**Working total: 61 A issue appearances and 3 B publication leads across 25 rows.** Counts describe entries below, not unique real-world events, and are subject to further inspection. **All 25 source rows remain open.**
