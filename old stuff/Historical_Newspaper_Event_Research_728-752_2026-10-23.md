# Historical newspaper publication discovery — HN-728–HN-752

**Discovery ledger | October 23, 2026 | 25 locked events**

## Method and status

This continues the fixed 1,056-event master and its classification v1.0, without creating or merging events. The Hoosier State Chronicles anchor for each HN entry is **not counted** as a new lead. The purpose is to discover as many contemporaneous newspaper treatments as possible across titles, dates, cities, and editorial angles. These are **findings so far**, not a completeness claim.

- **A:** contemporary original newspaper page text/OCR or publisher-archive article inspected at the linked page. For historical scan OCR, consult the page image before quoting exact words.
- **B:** an issue/article indexed or specifically identified but not independently inspected in full. NewspaperArchive results are often OCR previews behind an access wall. An original article cited in another newspaper also stays B until obtained.
- **Hold:** contextual, unclear match, or a different phase of reporting; excluded from totals.
- One count per **new newspaper title per HN**, even if multiple pieces from that title are linked. The same title can independently recur for different HN events. Syndicated copies in different publications are publication finds, not evidence of distinct original reporting.
- Early death counts and suspected causes are attributed as *contemporary newspaper claims*, never silently corrected to final historical conclusions.
