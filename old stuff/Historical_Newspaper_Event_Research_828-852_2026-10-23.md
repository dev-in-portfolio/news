## Reading this ledger

- **A — inspected original:** contemporary newspaper text opened and examined on the newspaper's own archive, an identifiable newspaper's republished archive, or an original newspaper PDF/OCR. A does **not** guarantee that the independent newspaper verified *every local detail* in the seed.
- **B — catalog/title only:** a named, dated newspaper article verified in a catalog or publisher archive, but its actual article text was **not** available for inspection. Do not cite its purported content beyond what the title/catalog establishes.
- **Angle:** **Direct** covers the event and seed's defining detail; **event** covers the same story without proving the seed's distinctive point; **parallel** documents comparable experiences elsewhere, not the seed's specific locality; **counterpoint** documents the contemporary debate, not agreement with a seed editorial.
- Count a newspaper **once per seed row**, even when multiple articles of that newspaper were found. The Hoosier source article is excluded. Different seed rows may cover overlapping world events, but a repeated article is not used twice. Stand-alone wire dispatches, contemporary non-newspaper records, and retrospective accounts are not counted.
- Dates below refer to the source articles; the Hoosier date is the **seed issue date**, not necessarily the historical event date. Specific alleged actions and evolving death totals are attributed to sources rather than silently asserted as settled facts.
