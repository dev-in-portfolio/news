## Method and count discipline

The `Seed queue` of `Historical_Newspaper_Event_Research_Tracker_v0.1.xlsx` was read without editing. Its last four identifiers are HN-1053 through HN-1056. This report is an additional-source ledger, **not a certification that all 1,056 rows have been researched in the current running series**. The previous recorded cumulative pass had handled 375 seed rows by HN-1052; this pass adds four, making 379 confirmed rows in that running total. Reaching the *last ID* must not be mistaken for proof of complete row-by-row coverage.

- **A** — independently produced newspaper reporting, with the publisher's substantive article body opened and read. A publisher outlet is counted once per bounded event, even if it published multiple updates.
- **B** — an identifiable original newspaper article, with publisher/headline/URL confirmed, but its complete article body not accessible. B is a research lead, **not** a body-read source.
- **Excluded / held** — the Guardian seed; AP/AFP/Reuters/Bloomberg/PA/dpa/APA reprints; news aggregations copying another publication; broadcaster-only, trade or science-magazine-only and official/institutional releases; duplicate stories or different event stages; purported newspaper items without adequate evidence of original editorial authorship.
- The seed date and publication date are distinct fields. Later dated reporting may describe the same event. Preliminary details, threats and causal claims are attributed rather than converted into established facts.

**Batch result: 9 A + 2 B = 11 additional qualifying newspaper article leads across four tracker seeds.** In HN-1053 the one Guardian seed actually bundles **two independent disasters**. The ledger keeps one seed row, proposes two subordinate event identities, and does not combine their source counts or edit the fixed tracker.

## Source evidence and packet notes

### HN-1053 — Hurricane Trudy and Greek flooding, *separate event identities* (seed 19 October)

**Original seed (not counted):** [The Guardian, 19 October](https://www.theguardian.com/news/2026/oct/19/hurricane-trudy-mexico-pacific-coast-greece-floods). Its headline joins a Pacific hurricane near Mexico to severe weather on Greek islands roughly halfway around the world. These are meteorologically and geographically **unrelated**. Do not let the joint headline drive a merged event identity.

**Proposed subordinate identity `EV-HN-1053-MX` — Mexico, Hurricane Trudy (roughly 17–19 October):**

- **A1 — Milenio, 18 October:** [Huracán Trudy: trayectoria del ciclón dejará lluvias fuertes en estos 3 estados](https://www.milenio.com/el-clima/huracan-trudy-trayectoria-dejara-lluvias-mexico-18-octubre). Original report by **Blanca Valadez**; publisher text opened and read. It describes Category 2 status as of the *18 October morning advisory*, movement parallel to Jalisco's coast, forecast very heavy rain in southern/coastal Jalisco, heavy rain in Colima and southern Nayarit, plus dangerous surf. This is a dated snapshot, **not Trudy's constant intensity**.
- **A2 — El Universal (San Luis Potosí edition), 17 October:** [Huracán Trudy: Sheinbaum pide extremar precauciones](https://sanluis.eluniversal.com.mx/nacion/huracan-trudy-sheinbaum-pide-extremar-precauciones-por-paso-del-huracan-ante-pronostico-de-lluvias-en-nueve-estados/). By **Sharon Mercado**, original newspaper article fully opened and read. Reports federal warnings and then-current Category 2 conditions. President Sheinbaum's warning refers to Trudy **and** an unrelated Gulf cold front; the nine states named in her weather warning must **not** be attributed exclusively to the hurricane.

**Proposed subordinate identity `EV-HN-1053-GR` — Greece, 13–15 October storms and Rhodes flood fatality:**

- **A3 — To Vima, 14 October:** [Deadly Storm Hits Rhodes as Floodwaters Sweep Cars Into the Sea](https://www.tovima.com/society/deadly-storm-hits-rhodes-as-floodwaters-sweep-cars-into-the-sea/). Original **To Vima Newsroom** synthesis, publisher body opened and read. Discusses a reported death near Stegna on Rhodes, vehicles carried out to sea, transportation disruption and flooding on Evia. The story explicitly attributes details to the Athens-Macedonian News Agency and local authorities, so it is recorded as **newspaper newsroom synthesis with named underlying sources**, not independent eyewitness investigation. A separate same-outlet [To Vima cross-country storm story](https://www.tovima.com/society/storm-batters-greece-as-rivers-overflow-and-roads-flood/) was also read for regional context, but is **not counted again** for the same Greek event/outlet.

2. An early 14 October To Vima/agency story identifies the Rhodes victim as **25**, while later eKathimerini indexing reports a **62-year-old** worker. The identity/age conflict is **unresolved** in this pass; state only that a fatality was reported, and obtain later definitive identification before using an age.
3. eKathimerini's specifically identified [storm/death article dated 14 October](https://www.ekathimerini.com/news/1318192/storm-kills-man-on-rhodes-floods-parts-of-greece-for-second-day/) returned access errors; because original authorship could not be confirmed, it is **held outside A/B**, rather than presenting a snippet as a reviewed original report.

### HN-1054 — Westminster Abbey Roman sarcophagus, new identification hypothesis (21 October)

**Original seed (not counted):** [The Guardian, 21 October](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/oct/21/mystery-roman-sarcophagus-westminster-abbey-solved-at-last-expert-says).

- **A1 — The Standard / Evening Standard, 21 October:** [Mystery of Westminster Abbey skeleton 'solved'](https://www.standard.co.uk/news/london/westminster-abbey-skeleton-roman-sarcophagus-abbot-archaeology-b1301771.html). Original article credited to **Tom Place, News Reporter**. Opened the publisher's substantive text, including the carbon-dating discussion, first discovery/rediscovery and competing abbot candidates. A photograph credit to PA Archive **does not** turn this bylined article into a PA-syndicated story.
- **B1 — The Times, 21 October:** [Has riddle of Westminster Abbey skeleton been solved at last?](https://www.thetimes.com/uk/history/article/mystery-roman-coffin-westminster-abbey-medieval-monks-hxk2wr6d5). Identified as an original newspaper history article and included on the **Kaya Burgess** Times author profile. The actual publisher article opened only to a subscription gate after its headline and deck. Although external indexing exposed passages, the **full publisher body was not available**, so **B**, not A.

**Packet note:** A fourth-century Roman-inscribed coffin, originally associated with **Valerius Amandinus**, apparently was reused for a **much later** Christian burial. Prof Warwick Rodwell favors a high-ranking medieval abbot, with **Ælfwig** (abbot roughly 993–1020) favored over **Wulfnoth** (successor through roughly 1049), but **the occupant is not conclusively identified**. Radiocarbon dating places the man within the broad late-tenth/early-eleventh-century interval. The [archaeological paper](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00681288.2026.2675788) and [Westminster Abbey announcement](https://www.westminster-abbey.org/abbey-news/mystery-of-abbeys-roman-sarcophagus-solved) were used as background cross-checks, **not counted** as newspapers. A New York Post retelling explicitly reliant on Live Science/Daily Mail was not passed as independent first-hand newspaper reporting; magazines likewise excluded.

- **A3 — Washington Blade, 21 October:** [Supreme Court blocks White House anti-trans prison policy](https://www.washingtonblade.com/2026/10/21/supreme-court-blocks-white-house-anti-trans-prison-policy/). **Joe Reberkenny** original newspaper piece; full publisher body read, with reporting and response requested from the White House. Qualifies as an independent LGBTQ community newspaper, not merely a rehosted wire.
- **B1 — The Wall Street Journal, 21 October:** [Supreme Court Blocks Trump's Ban on Care for Transgender Prisoners](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/supreme-court-maintains-access-to-transgender-medical-care-in-federal-prisons-9b1d5137). ### HN-1056 — Iran's *conditional* Gulf infrastructure threats (seed 22 October)

**Original seed (not counted):** [The Guardian, 22 October](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/22/iran-threatens-seven-gulf-countries-us-israel-renew-attacks).

- **A1 — Arab Times, 19 October:** [Iranian Regime Threatens to Cut Undersea Internet Cables, Strike Gulf Data Centres and AI Facilities](https://www.arabtimesonline.com/news/iranian-regime-threatens-to-cut-undersea-internet-cables-strike-gulf-data-centres-and-ai-facilities/). This **Kuwaiti daily newspaper** published a substantive newsroom report well before the Guardian seed, discussing Mohsen Rezaei's stated threats, hard-line Iranian newspaper responses and possible spillovers for regional digital systems. Full publisher article read. Its emphasis is on cables/data/AI; do **not** retroactively claim the 19 October report specifies exactly seven threatened countries.
- **A2 — El País, 23 October:** [Irán amenaza con atacar infraestructuras petroleras y de inteligencia artificial en el Golfo si EE UU e Israel reanudan los bombardeos](https://elpais.com/internacional/2026-10-23/iran-amenaza-con-atacar-la-infraestructura-petrolera-y-de-inteligencia-artificial-del-golfo-si-ee-uu-e-israel-reanudan-los-bombardeos.html). Original Spanish-language reporting by **Trinidad Deiros Bronte**; substantive article body read. Covers the later reiterated warning of strikes on oil, subsea cables, data centres and AI installations in **as many as seven Gulf states** if new US/Israeli attacks occur, placing it in contemporaneous diplomacy and tensions.

**Packet note:** This was an announced **contingency/threat, not proof of a fresh assault on the named Gulf digital infrastructure**. Critically, the underlying target language was circulating **before 22 October**: an [Al Jazeera report dated 13 October](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/13/iran-threatens-gulf-data-centres-how-exposed-is-the-regions-internet) had already discussed Rezaei's data-centre/undersea-cable/AI warning, while Arab Times published on 19 October and [The National, 21 October](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/gulf/2026/10/21/araghchi-says-strait-of-hormuz-will-stay-closed-until-us-agrees-to-irans-terms/) alluded to the same broad threat in a different, chiefly diplomatic article. The seed should therefore be indexed as **22 October coverage/escalation of a continuing warning** rather than the first appearance of a wholly new policy or actual attack. The 13 October broadcaster story and brief The National contextual mention are **not counted as separate qualifying newspaper reports**. Ajker Patrika's 23 October article attributed its narrative to **The Guardian** and mirrored it; excluded as derivative. Reuters' 23 October follow-up is independent wire reporting on possible plans but **not an added newspaper original**. An independently written 23 October La Voce di New York summary remains held because substantial sourcing and structure appeared derivative of the seed; no A/B increment.

## Exclusion, conflicts and quality-control trail

1. **No Guardian increments.** The four seed URLs provided the event identities only.
2. **HN-1053 split:** two events: Trudy off Mexico's Pacific coast and floods in Greece. One Guardian wrapper, **not one disaster**. `EV-HN-1053-MX` and `EV-HN-1053-GR` are suggested downstream IDs only.
3. **No double count within outlet/event:** Two To Vima reports helped frame Greek flood chronology, but only one is counted. Later coverage by the same newspaper does not create a separate independent outlet.
4. **Early inconsistent victim age:** Rhodes sources indexed both 25 and 62 years. This report deliberately declines to resolve without stronger verification and retains only the existence of a reported fatality.
5. **Paywalls and access errors:** The Times and WSJ are **B**, not full-read A. eKathimerini publisher errors and bne IntelliNews 403 leave sources **uncounted** rather than guessed.
6. **Originality checks:** El Universal and Milenio had named newspaper reporters and full publisher bodies; To Vima had its own newsroom synthesis (while attributing parts to ANA-MPA); The Standard's PA credit was to an illustration, not the reporter; Washington Post's separate AP syndication was excluded.
7. **Iran warning predates seed:** independent media reported this same *conditional class of targets* on 13 and 19 October. Do not reframe 22 October as the first-ever issuance of the threat, or merge 23 October Reuters operational allegations with a verified attack.
8. **Scope exclusions:** NHC advisories, the Rodwell research paper, Westminster Abbey, broadcasters, AP/Reuters reprints and content-laundered Guardian summaries are not independent original newspaper articles.
