# Historical newspaper coverage finding register — EV-HN-203–227

Research pass: October 22, 2026 · **Coverage finding only** · All 25 original event packets remain open

## Rules and scope

This follows the established 1,056 initial discoveries and expansion passes through EV-HN-202. This pass covers **EV-HN-203–227**, retaining each original newspaper seed. The goal is to locate dated appearances across *different period newspapers and issue dates*: original dispatches, follow-ups, contemporary reprints, investigative reporting, court reporting and illustrated newspapers. Do **not** treat added appearances as newly discovered events. Do **not** treat an initial source's claims (especially casualty counts, charges, or alleged poisoning) as independently proven facts.

- **A** — original dated issue text or image accessible directly, or contemporary newspaper text reproduced legibly with publication/date identified.
- **B** — specifically dated newspaper/issue attested through a bibliographic citation, period index, historic catalogue, or a later quotation. Original issue/article text not necessarily inspected.
- **C** — plausible search lead or source whose precise issue, title, or same-incident identity cannot yet be established. Keep outside the A/B count.

Each line in the A/B table is an **additional issue-level lead beyond the existing Indiana seed**. Multiple relevant stories in the *same newspaper issue* appear once. An article may relate to **two distinct existing research packets** (especially the two *Polaris* threads); overlapping issues are cross-linked but must not inflate any eventual global count of unique newspaper issues.
