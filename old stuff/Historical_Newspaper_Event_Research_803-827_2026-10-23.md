## Counting rules

- A lead is **one *additional* contemporary newspaper title associated with one HN seed row**. Multiple articles from the same title under one row are counted once. A title can occur against different HN rows, which are separately researched events or interpretive angles. The listed Hoosier State Chronicles seed paper is *not* counted as an additional lead.
- **A (article inspected):** Article text, original OCR, or the contemporary newspaper's digitized article was opened and read to establish relevance. Republishing an authentic contemporaneous article on its publisher's archival site qualifies. A wire-service story actually printed by an identified paper may count as that paper's title. **B (index only):** Original newspaper/issue identifiable but specific article not opened; no B counted in this block.
- **Angle labels:** `DIRECT` confirms the distinguishing seed angle; `EVENT` confirms the common event but not the seed's distinctive person, organization, or claim; `PARALLEL` establishes a materially relevant analogous local or faith-community response while expressly *not* corroborating the exact seed claim; `CORRECTIVE` supplies contemporaneous checks on a provisional or incorrect assertion.
- Do not turn questions, claims, projected outcomes, disputed casualty figures, preliminary theories, or hearsay into settled facts. An incident appearing in a later issue may predate the issue. Never conflate the September 9 Somalia action with other 1993 Mogadishu battles.
- Retrospective histories, institutional records, standalone newswires, modern republications not proven contemporary, and tangential news remain **uncounted**. Primary source links are included in each counted entry.
