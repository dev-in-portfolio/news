## Counting method and pass result

- **25 seed packets examined** using title/date/event-specific web searching. All **25 remain open** for more issue-level searching.

The exact issue copies were **not directly read** for these five, except that the contemporaneous *Daily National Intelligencer* March 26, 1836 issue has a directly visible, issue-bound indexed OCR transcription (counted below as one directly text-inspected newspaper issue).

They concern Charles Gibbs's claimed pirate confession. **Provisional** until the Indiana May 21 seed's name and text are reconciled against Gibbs; do not silently count them as proven copies of that seed.
- **Two relevant contemporary non-newspaper periodical appearances** for packet 104, tracked separately.
- **Two contemporaneous newspapers supplied event-bearing original-issue indexed OCR:** *Rhode-Island Republican* April 14, 1831 (provisional seed match), and *Daily National Intelligencer* March 26, 1836 (strong match). This does not mean full page scan inspection or complete article boundaries were verified.
- **No asserted total for all newspapers nationally**; these are additional leads, not a saturation claim. Later books, bibliographies, duplicate web listings, and the already recorded Indiana seed issue are excluded from additional-newspaper counts.

**Packet 111 identity caution:** A pamphlet entitled *Mutiny and Murder: Confession of Charles Gibbs* also appeared in Providence in 1831; Rhode Island Historical Society and the Library of Congress catalogue it. The pamphlet **is not a newspaper issue**, and its sometimes extraordinary claimed tally of murders must not be presented as verified fact. The original Indiana *Palladium* seed description says only “published pirate confession recounts multiple mutinies,” so identifying its pirate as Gibbs is plausible but not yet verified. [RIHS record](https://navigator.rihs.org/rhode-island-a-bibliography-of-its-history/mutiny-and-murder-confession-of-charles-gibbs-a-native-of-rhode-island-who-with-thomas-j-wansley-was-doomed-to-be-hung-in-new-york-on-the-22d-of-april-last-for-the-murder-of-the-captain-and-mat/); [LOC law collection](https://stuff.coffeecode.net/www.loc.gov/law/help/piracy/piracy_trials.php).

**Packet 126 date caution:** Contemporary newspaper accounts call the blast **March 12, 1836**, whereas several retrospective casualty compilations date the *Ben Franklin* disaster **March 13**. Keep the disagreement; do not merge the dates uncritically or infer distinct explosions without further records. The fatality count also varies by source and timing of deaths. [Contemporary issue](https://lib-dspace1.ttu.edu/bitstream/handle/20.500.12255/85080/1836_03_26_Daily%20National%20Intelligencer.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=3); [comparison of sources](https://www.usdeadlyevents.com/1836-march-13-steamboat-ben-franklin-boiler-explosion-wharf-mobile-al-13-30/).
