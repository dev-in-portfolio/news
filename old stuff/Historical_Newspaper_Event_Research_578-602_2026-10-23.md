# Newspaper Archives Online — Additional Publication Discovery, HN-578–HN-602

**Research checkpoint:** 2026-10-23  
**Fixed inventory:** HN-001–HN-1056 (1,056 seeded discoveries). **This pass:** HN-578 through HN-602, 25 distinct inventory events, 1950–1955.  
**Phase:** Article discovery only. The objective is to find as many independently published *contemporary newspaper articles* about each existing event as possible. No event expansion, conclusion-building, claim of exhaustive coverage, or historical verification project is implied.

## Reading the findings

- **A — original-page find:** A dated newspaper page, scan, or OCR was accessible and its relevant reporting was identified. An OCR abstract/index attached to an original issue is specified when the whole page text was not personally inspected.
- **B — publication identified:** A named, dated original newspaper report was confirmed by an institutional catalog, periodical listing, or a faithful historic front-page reproduction, but original article text was not independently read here.
- **Related / hold:** Relevant but the wrong event stage, wrong date, incomplete newspaper attribution, or too weak a match to count. These are leads, not finds.
- The initial Indiana newspaper *seed issue* supplied by our inventory is **excluded** from the additional-publication counts. Repeated links to the same newspaper about the same event count once per event in this checkpoint; separate consecutive inventory events remain separate.
- Counts represent publications **found so far in this pass**, not all publications that printed the story. An A or B grade attaches to source access, not a declaration that every fact or headline was true. URLs describe primary archive pages or the catalog/reproduction that specifically establishes a newspaper edition.
