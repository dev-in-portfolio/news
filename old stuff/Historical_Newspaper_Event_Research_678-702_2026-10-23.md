# Historical Newspaper Event Research — HN-678–HN-702

**Discovery pass:** 23 October 2026. **Scope:** original newspaper coverage from publication titles other than the existing Indiana seed title. These 25 locked events have **not** been replaced, renumbered, or expanded into new event IDs.

This is a **finding ledger, not an exhaustive index or an assertion that every newspaper printed below has been read in full**. One title is counted once per event, even if several articles appeared in that newspaper; syndicated text in separate newspapers remains a separate publication instance. Relevant earlier/later contemporaneous reporting is identified as such. Original contemporary allegations are recorded as *allegations*, not independently established facts.

**A** = original contemporary article text/page inspected (including publisher archive or facsimile with legible OCR). **B** = dated, identifiable original newspaper story/issue corroborated in a newspaper archive, university collection, historical bibliography, contemporaneous reprint or newspaper collector, but the relevant original full page not independently read. **Hold** = interesting leads or documentary background which do not justify a counted separate newspaper publication in this pass. The original seed titles (*Indianapolis Recorder*, *Mechanicsburg Journal*, *Jewish Post*, *BG*, *NAN*) are not counted.

## Individual discoveries and source trails

### HN-678 — Black newspaper insists Jackson State belongs beside Kent State
*Seed: Indianapolis Recorder, 23 May 1970.*
- **A — *The Washington Post*, 18 May 1970**, editorial “Jackson State”: sustained comparison with Kent State, including the differences in race and police/Guard response. Original editorial reproduced in the 19 May *Congressional Record*: [Bates College Muskie archive](https://abacus.bates.edu/muskie-archives/ajcr/1970/Jackson%20State.html).
- **B — *Indiana Daily Student*, 18 May 1970**: contemporary editorial making the comparison; identification discussed with surviving contemporary editorial context: [The Nation retrospective](https://www.thenation.com/article/politics/jackson-state-shootings-fifty/).
- **B — *Lompoc Record*, 23 May 1970**: archived report of a protest/march considering Kent State and Jackson State in parallel. This is related coverage, not a matching editorial: [issue/page record](https://newspaperarchive.com/lompoc-record-may-23-1970-p-1/).

### HN-679 — Police sniper claim accompanies photograph of Jackson State dormitory
*Seed: Indianapolis Recorder, 23 May 1970.*

## Unresolved and next-stage targets

The discovery pass produced **79 original-paper publication/event matches (A 16, B 63)** across 21 of 25 seeds. Four event rows have **no independently qualified outside newspaper found in this pass**: **HN-684, HN-685, HN-686, and HN-690**. Zero does **not** mean no such articles exist.

The narrower elements **still unconfirmed**, even where broader coverage was found, are: the exact student unofficial-inquiry testimony (HN-680), a *photo* matching the sniper/dorm report (HN-679), the original *local Indiana church* aid network (HN-686), reform rabbis' October 1972 congressional-investigation demand (HN-690), a precise 9–10 April tornado follow-up beyond *Straits Times* (HN-698), and a newspaper repeating the particular Khmer Rouge territory/secrecy assertion as of **March 1975** (HN-702).

For any later full-text retrieval, preserve the distinction among **issue located**, **article located**, and **exact wording/page inspected**. The B items remain candidates for that next retrieval pass, not certified verbatim originals. Avoid adding these 79 counts to prior batches unless cross-batch events/publications are deduplicated on `(locked event ID, newspaper title)`.
