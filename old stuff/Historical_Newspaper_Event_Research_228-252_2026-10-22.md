# Historical newspaper coverage finding register — EV-HN-228–252

Research pass: October 22, 2026 (Eastern) · **Coverage finding only** · All 25 event packets remain open

## Rules and scope

This continues the established catalogue of **1,056 initial discoveries** and the newspaper-appearance expansion through EV-HN-227. The 25 original Indiana newspaper seeds below are retained as anchors, not counted as newly found appearances. The task at this stage is **finding all traceable contemporary newspaper appearances**: immediate reports, reprints, conflicting witness statements, follow-ups, inquests, trials and illustrations. These are *issue appearances*, not new incidents, and not necessarily independent reporting. This pass is **not exhaustive**.

- **A:** dated contemporary newspaper article/issue text, scan or legible transcription actually identified and accessible.
- **B:** original newspaper, precise issue date and connection to the incident attested in a catalogue, index or identified historical citation, without full original text inspected.
- **C:** plausible, disputed or insufficiently identified candidate. Do not count.
- Count **one appearance per newspaper and issue date** even if many articles, pages or catalogue entries refer to it. Keep an original incident date separate from later publication dates. The source itself may contain false assertions; counting it does not endorse them.
- Historical illustrated *newspapers* are included; other periodical writing is noted separately when useful. The borderline weekly news-publication *Spectator* is segregated as supplemental rather than silently included in the newspaper-only subtotal.

## Supplemental period press and noncounted context

1. **EV-HN-233 — *The Spectator*, December 18, 1875:** The [contemporaneous London political-news weekly](https://archive.spectator.co.uk/article/18th-december-1875/1/a-crime-the-like-of-which-the-world-has-probably) gives a substantial *Mosel* narrative. It is not in the **newspaper-only count** above because of the weekly news-magazine boundary. This is a discoverable additional period publication, not a newly discovered event.
2. **EV-HN-242 — experiments versus actual mill blast:** [*Scientific American*, August 10, 1878](https://www.scientificamerican.com/issue/sa/1878/08-10/) and *Popular Science Monthly* (December 1878) contain contemporaneous technical discussion but are **scientific periodicals**, not newly counted newspaper issues. The May 1878 Washburn A mill explosion and later dust-explosion investigations require separate chronological identification.
3. **EV-HN-236 — Kansas lynching:** Kansas Historical Society contextual material identifies a horse thief **John Callaham/Calleham** and a stranger named **Cole** as victims near Dodge City, reportedly around **April 8, 1876**. Neither a newspaper issue title nor issue date was independently secured; context is not a count. [Kansas historical research](https://www.kshs.org/).
4. **EV-HN-239 — Mary Ann Brown Patten:** Reliable maritime biography identifies her actual death in **March 1861**; a “second obituary” printed in 1877 is a *historical newspaper error*, not evidence she died a second time. [Mariners' Museum collections](https://www.marinersmuseum.org/).
5. **EV-HN-248 — false Garfield letter:** The *Morey letter* is a well-documented political forgery; the exact authorship was never legally proved. Contemporary papers' claims of authenticity, responsibility, motive and election impact must be read as attributed claims, not automatically confirmed. [National Park Service account](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/an-1880-october-surprise.htm).
6. **EV-HN-231 — other uncounted Schiller leads:** *Newfoundlander* complete newspaper PDFs of **May 14** and **May 21, 1875** are accessible, but the available OCR did not confirm a specific *Schiller* article, so they are *not yet counted*. [May 14 issue](https://dai.mun.ca/pdfs/newfoundland/Newfoundlander18750514.pdf), [May 21 issue](https://dai.mun.ca/pdfs/newfoundland/Newfoundlander18750521.pdf). Also pending: *Waterford Mail*, *Bendigo Advertiser*, *Holland City News* and other possible *Schiller* contemporary press until article identification is checked.
