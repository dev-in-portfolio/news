# Historical Newspaper Event Research — HN-503 through HN-527

**Finding pass:** October 23, 2026  
**Range:** August 1936–September 1941; 25 existing master event IDs  
**Purpose:** Locate further reporting in other period newspapers about already-discovered events. **This is a finding ledger**, not a full article transcription, a finished bibliography, or an exhaustive count of every publication.

## Counting and evidence rules

- **A — located newspaper appearance:** Original digitized newspaper page, issue, or article/facsimile with event-specific headline/OCR/text was found. One title counted once per HN event, regardless of pages. Original newspaper scans behind an online OCR transcription still count when title, date and relevant content are identifiable.
- **B — publication-specific lead:** A concrete title/date and topical story are identified by a catalog, book, archive index, publisher retrospective, or dealer description, but the original relevant printed page has **not** been directly inspected. A B is not a retrieved article.
- **Context (uncounted):** Syndicated news-agency bulletin without a separately identified recipient newspaper, non-news primary records, later scholarship, or adjacent-stage press articles that do not match the precise event. An original **Jewish Telegraphic Agency Daily Bulletin** is a useful contemporary publication, but is not silently recast as a separate newspaper here.
- **Seed exclusion:** Every Indiana newspaper page that defined HN-503–527 in master version 26 is **excluded from new-finding totals**. Distinct papers publishing the same AP/UP wire are publication appearances, not additional independent eyewitnesses.
- **Reading discipline:** Scans/OCR frequently contain printing and recognition mistakes. Contested responsibility, casualty totals and rumors remain **dated claims of their newspaper**. Direct URLs below point to the archival item, not necessarily a single isolated clipping.

## Findings by event

### HN-503 — August 14, 1936 — Fear of post-Olympic Nazi persecution

- **A0 / B0** additional independently located newspaper appearances for the **specific post-Olympics persecution concern**. The *Jewish Post* August 14 original is already the master seed.
- **Context — [Jewish Telegraphic Agency, July 10, 1936](https://www.jta.org/archive/nazis-prepare-renewed-anti-jewish-drive-after-olympics).** Original-period Daily Bulletin anticipated a new anti-Jewish drive following the Games, but no additional newspaper title is identified in this link.

The page's contemporary text was indexed; this is a press-service report, not a verified recipient newspaper.
- **Context — [*American Jewish Outlook* (Pittsburgh), August 21, 1936, original PDF](https://iiif.library.cmu.edu/file/OUT_1936_004_011_08211936/OUT_1936_004_011_08211936.pdf).** Contains post-Games debate about fair treatment of athletes; **not counted** because that is different from the master event's reported fear of renewed persecution in Germany.
- **Follow-up target:** Search regional Jewish weeklies' August 14–28 issues for the JTA story or an independently written treatment; do not assume wire pickup without finding it.

### HN-525 — March 11, 1941 — Congressional passage and signing of Lend-Lease

- **A1 — [*Oregon Statesman* (Salem), March 12, p. 1](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn85042470/1941-03-12/ed-1/seq-1/).** Original reporting that Congress sent Lend-Lease to Roosevelt and describing the aid mechanism.
