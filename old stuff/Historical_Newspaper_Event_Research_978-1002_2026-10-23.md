## Scope, grading, and safeguards

- **A — body checked:** A contemporaneous, independently reported article from a named newspaper or news-specific digital newspaper. Its article body was opened and read. An article with the same byline or syndicated origin appearing in multiple newspapers is counted only for its original publication; two articles from one outlet for the same seed never count as two independent leads.
- **B — indexed only:** An identifiable, dated newspaper issue/page on an archive, where the underlying story body or its specific headline cannot be inspected. **B does not assert that the proposed student article's content was verified.**
- The Guardian links in the fixed seed tracker identify the inquiry but **are not counted as extra independent newspaper finds**. A wire-service story republished by another publication is excluded as an independent newspaper find.
- Reporting dates matter. Subsequent casualty counts, final investigative findings and election certification are not projected backward into first-day news.
- The B archive publication's actual name, confirmed in Hoosier State Chronicles metadata, is **Jefferson Booster** (Lafayette, Indiana)—not “Jewish Front Star.”
- HN-991 and HN-992 share the **same event packet EV-HN-991**, the **February 3, 2023 East Palestine derailment**, while tracking different reporting stages. They are **two search seeds, not two separate derailments**.

### HN-978 — The Ever Given blocks the Suez Canal (March 24, 2021)

- **A1 — Washington Post**, Tim Elfrink, March 24: [One of the world's biggest ships got stuck sideways in the Suez Canal](https://www.washingtonpost.com/nation/2021/03/24/suez-canal-ever-given-stuck/). Original article body recounts the grounded vessel, canal obstruction and queued shipping.
- **A2 — Los Angeles Times**, Nabih Bulos, March 24: [Suez Canal blocked by grounded mega-ship](https://www.latimes.com/world-nation/story/2021-03-24/egypt-suez-canal-blocked-megaship). Distinct staff reporting describes the ship, location, traffic and authorities' initial wind/sandstorm explanation.
- **Caution:** An early official explanation of cause is not a completed accident determination; the stranding began March 23, and the March 24 reports captured the continuing closure.
