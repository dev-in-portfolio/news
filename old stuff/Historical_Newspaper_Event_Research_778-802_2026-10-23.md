# Historical Newspaper Event Research — HN-778–HN-802

Research date: 2026-10-23  
Master: 1,056 HN-row locked event tracker; classification v1.0 (13 main / 100 sub / 361 third-level).  
Scope: **additional contemporary newspaper coverage**, beyond the original Hoosier State Chronicles seed. No event inventory or classification changes.

## Method and count

- **A** = contemporaneous article text at its newspaper publisher, or original-issue OCR, **opened and inspected** in this pass.
- **B** = contemporaneous newspaper title/article or issue indexed, but its relevant article/image OCR **not directly inspected**.
- Exactly **one counted lead per newspaper title per HN row**. Different articles by the same title are context, not extra leads; the same paper may contribute to different HN rows.
- Later retrospectives, wire-service-only pages, non-newspaper materials, and generic stories missing the identified event are not counted. Related **parallel locality** reports are identified explicitly; they do not establish that an Indianapolis person or incident appeared elsewhere.
- Links go to the accessible record rather than invented page scans. Historical uncertainty and reporting-time death estimates are retained, not retroactively overwritten.

**Batch result: 43 additional leads — 41 A, 2 B; 23 of 25 HN rows with at least one lead.** Two no-match rows: **HN-792, HN-798**.  
Prior HN-678–777 subtotal: 295 (127 A / 168 B). **Running HN-678–802 subtotal: 338 leads (168 A / 170 B) across 125 rows.** This subtotal counts sourced leads, not unique newspaper titles or distinct historical events.
