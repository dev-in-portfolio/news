# Historical Newspaper Event Research — HN-628–HN-652

**Date:** 2026-10-23  
**Pass:** Discover contemporary reports in *other named publications* for the fixed 1,056-event inventory.  
**Scope:** 25 pre-existing event IDs, HN-628 through HN-652. No new events assigned; the seed publications are not counted as additional finds.

## Reading the findings

- **A — direct original:** A named contemporary newspaper page, its OCR, or the contemporary article itself can be read at the link. The article must concern the indexed event or its explicitly marked immediate stage.
- **B — identified original:** A specific dated newspaper and pertinent report/front page are identified by an archival reproduction, newspaper catalog, historical institutional bibliography, or original-front-page collection. We have **not** read the complete original page at that link. These need image/article inspection in the deeper retrieval pass.
- **Hold:** Context, later interpretations, unnamed images, magazines rather than newspapers, or an apparent match to the *wrong stage*. Holds do not count.
- **Counting unit:** One distinct **newspaper title** per event, even where it has multiple editions, dates, related articles, or pages. Each identified article/date is still recorded under that title. Thus these totals are not counts of all independent articles.
- **Historical wording:** Early casualty estimates and contemporaneous accusations describe what was reported *at the time*. They are not substituted for later established figures or findings. In this discovery phase, no retrospective fact-check or interpretation of the event is undertaken.

The linked source itself states the publication/date where possible. An **A** link often takes the reader to a newspaper page with other unrelated stories; its relevant article is named in the annotation.
