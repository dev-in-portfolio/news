# Newspaper Archives — Additional Publication Finding Ledger

**Research pass:** HN-528 through HN-552 (25 existing events; 8 December 1941–9 August 1945)  
**Date recorded:** 23 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Locate further contemporary newspaper appearances for already inventoried events, beyond each event's original seed newspaper. This is a *finding* ledger, not an interpretation or a claim of exhaustive publication coverage.

## Recording rules

- **A — Located newspaper page:** An issue/page image, PDF, or OCR record from a newspaper archive identifies the relevant coverage. OCR can be imperfect; quoted figures below are provisional readings until the scans are inspected closely.
- **B — Identified publication lead:** A named paper, issue/date, and relevant article is documented by a museum, dealer, finding aid, or catalog, but its original page has not been independently examined here.
- **Related / hold (not counted):** Distinct stages, articles that discuss only the broader episode, uncertain dates, photography, magazines, retrospectives, or links lacking enough article identification. These are valuable follow-up leads, not additional confirmed matches.
- **Deduplication:** Count each additional *newspaper title once per HN event*; exclude the event's original seed title. The same newspaper can appear under another event when that event is a separately inventoried report or changed information.
- **Caution:** Absence of a listed match is a search gap, not evidence that other newspapers did not print the story. The listed dates can differ from the seed's publication date; differences are noted.

- **A1 — Medford Mail Tribune** (Medford, Oregon), 8 Dec 1941, p. 1. Early reported estimate of roughly 3,000 **dead and wounded combined**, not 3,000 confirmed dead. [Original page](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn97071090/1941-12-08/ed-1/seq-1/).
