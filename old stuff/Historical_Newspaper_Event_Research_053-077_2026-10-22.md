## EV-HN-061 — St Nicholas church-tower collapse, Liverpool (11 February 1810)

- **I — Liverpool Saturday Advertiser, 17 February 1810**, page pending. A Historical Society of Lancashire and Cheshire study cites this contemporaneous issue. This is an additional dated publication lead beyond the *Western Sun* May 12, 1810 seed. [Source citation to paper](https://www.hslc.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/65-3-Peet.pdf).
- **C — The Annual Register**, volume 52 for 1810, Chronicle p. 456. A later historical work cites its account. This is a period annual survey; original passage has not been inspected, and it **must not be counted as a second newspaper title**. [Historical citation](https://dl.tufts.edu/teiviewer/parent/k0698j802/chapter/c20s3); [digitized volume listing](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:The_Annual_Register).
- Date caution: the collapse is consistently identified in other records as **11 February 1810**, although one later transcription mistakenly gives January. Do not propagate the January date without a primary issue check.

- Names in an 1818 article might vary (Chehaw, Cheha, Chehaws, Aumuculle, Aumuculee); use variants, not separate event counts.
