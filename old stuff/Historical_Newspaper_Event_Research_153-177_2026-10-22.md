# Historical newspaper event discovery: EV-HN-153–177

Research checkpoint — October 22, 2026

## Scope and evidence rules

This is the **finding stage**, continuing the existing 1,056-discovery historical newspaper project and the event packets in *Historical Newspaper Event Research Tracker v0.1*. The objective is to collect **every discoverable separate dated newspaper appearance**, including reproductions, distant reprints, disputes and later developments, across publications. A separate appearance is **not** a newly discovered event. This is an open register, not a claim that searching or verification is complete.

- **A — period newspaper text accessible:** searchable text/transcription or a legible contemporary newspaper facsimile was encountered. Still evaluate whether the text is actually the same event.
- **B — specifically identified dated newspaper:** original issue/index, contemporary reference, catalog entry, bibliographic source or archival caption identifies a newspaper and issue, but the article text has **not** been fully inspected here.
- **C — unresolved lead:** possible issue/reprint or mismatch, with newspaper title/date, relation to the seed, or article presence not independently established. **Do not count C as confirmed matching appearances**.

Dates supplied in original event seeds are usually **seed article publication dates**. They are not necessarily incident dates. A and B represent retrieval confidence, **not** independent confirmation that every factual allegation printed was true.
