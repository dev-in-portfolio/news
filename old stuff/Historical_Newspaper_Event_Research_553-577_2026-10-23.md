# Historical newspaper research: HN-553–HN-577

**Research date:** October 23, 2026  
**Coverage:** 25 existing master-inventory events, August 1945–January 1950.  
**Purpose:** Finding step—locate additional contemporary newspaper publications reporting each recorded event. Do not add events to, or renumber, the 1,056-event master inventory.

## Counting and evidence rules

- The master-inventory *Indianapolis Times*, *Daily Banner*, *Decatur Daily Democrat*, *Jewish Post*, or *National Jewish Post* seed is **excluded** for its own event.
- **A** = a located original newspaper page, OCR, or issue scan with relevant coverage. An A can describe an earlier/later stage of the *same evolving story*; its note specifies the difference. It is **not** a claim that the newspaper replicated the seed's exact words or number.
- **B** = a named and dated contemporary newspaper edition identified through a catalog, archive index, historical reproduction, or bibliographic citation, but its complete relevant newspaper page was **not independently read** in this pass.
- **Related / hold** = context, unconfirmed claim, or distinctly different development. **Not counted.** Wire services alone and later historical articles are not counted as separate original newspapers.
- **Each publication counts once per HN event**, even when multiple issues of it are linked. Numbers and accounts in breaking stories should be kept as contemporary *reports*, not necessarily later-established totals.
- This is an **additional-publication finding pass**, not a claim of every edition or exhaustive coverage. The original seed remains in the master record.

- **A2 — *Bend Bulletin*, Aug. 14, 1945, p. 1.** Tokyo radio/Potsdam surrender reports amid uncertainty about a formal reply. [Original issue](https://oregonnews.uoregon.edu/lccn/sn90051770/1945-08-14/ed-1/seq-1/).
