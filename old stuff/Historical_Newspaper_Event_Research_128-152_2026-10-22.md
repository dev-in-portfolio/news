## What this pass is (and is not)

This is the **finding stage**: locate as many separate, dated historical newspaper appearances as practical for each existing event seed. The 25 seeds below come from the existing Historical Newspaper Event Research Tracker v0.1. A record is a *newspaper issue/article lead*, not a new event. Later reports, commentary, and contradictory accounts stay connected to their seed event; they are not automatically duplicates or confirmations. This is an **open collection**, not a complete census of publications or articles.

Evidence labels:
- **A — original article readable**: period newspaper text/transcription is accessible and matches the incident.
- **B — dated issue corroborated**: scholarly/historical citation, reputable index, or image listing identifies newspaper and issue; item image/article still needs inspection.
- **C — possible lead**: contemporary paper is identified, but exact link, article content, timing, or relation to the seed needs resolution. Not counted as a confirmed match.
