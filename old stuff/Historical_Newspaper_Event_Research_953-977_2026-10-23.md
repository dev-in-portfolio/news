# Historical Newspaper Event Research — HN-953–HN-977

**Research date:** October 23, 2026  
**Sequence:** 25 contiguous event seeds, June 24, 2016–January 6, 2021  
**Work type:** Discovery and corroboration only; locked master inventory and classification unchanged.

## Evidence and counting method

- **A — opened original:** the contemporary newspaper's own original reporting, column, or student-newspaper story was opened and substantive article text inspected. A photograph-only account is identified as such. A newspaper title contributes at most one A per seed; multiple stories in the same newspaper do not inflate the count.
- **B — indexed lead:** a specific contemporaneous newspaper story is discoverable and attributable but its article body could not be opened. A B is **not** equivalent to an inspected account. The seed's original citation is background, not an automatic additional independent A/B.
- Wire-service and newspaper-to-newspaper reprints, broadcast television, magazines, later retrospectives, institutional releases, and mere search snippets are excluded from A. An original newspaper opinion column is identified as commentary rather than neutral reporting.
- **Crucial local limitation:** the original *Jewish Front Star* Indiana pages for HN-959, HN-961, HN-974, and HN-975 are linked as seed documents, but their story bodies were **not** readable in this pass. Other newspapers establish the wider event or Indiana context; they do **not** confirm the precise wording, school, date error, or positions printed in those four student stories.
- Citations below are direct article links. Event dates and contemporary publication dates are kept distinct. Early provisional numbers are never silently replaced with later totals.

1. **A · *The Washington Post*, June 24:** [“In stunning decision, Britain votes to leave the E.U.”](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/britons-head-to-the-polls-for-historic-vote-on-eu/2016/06/23/0d466fb0-34a7-11e6-ab9d-1da2b0f24f93_story.html).
