# Historical Newspaper Event Research — HN-928–HN-952

**Research date:** October 23, 2026  
**Queue:** 25 seeds, November 2012–June 2016  
**Seed tracker:** `Historical_Newspaper_Event_Research_Tracker_v0.1.xlsx`, worksheet `Seed queue`  
**Status:** Independent contemporary-newspaper pass completed for this batch; exact-source and attribution holds remain specified below.

## Method

- **A:** The substantive body of a contemporary independent newspaper article, editorial, or column was opened and read. A contextual report counts only as evidence about the larger event, not the original student's precise wording, local campaign, or allegation.
- **B:** A contemporary, independently relevant newspaper item was identified, but its substantive body could not be read. None were retained in this batch.
- Count each distinct **newspaper title once per seed**, not once per URL. Exclude the seed publication itself, agency-only copy and newspaper-hosted wire republications, television/radio pieces, magazines, government and NGO publications, and retrospective articles outside the event period. Later articles may clarify a contemporary error but do not retroactively change what was known on publication day.
- Most Indiana archive issue links render bibliographic/issue pages without the article body. The original dates, descriptions and issue links below come from the seed tracker; this review does **not** claim to have transcribed or read those original seed articles. The initial *Guardian* article for HN-952 is also the original seed, so it is not counted as an independent second title.
- **Context-only**, **disputed**, **preliminary** and **not claim-specific** labels are substantive limitations. A source's account of a public controversy does not independently establish every factual allegation inside it.
