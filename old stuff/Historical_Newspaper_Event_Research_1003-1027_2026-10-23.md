# Historical Newspaper Event Research — HN-1003–HN-1027

Research continuation | 23 October 2026 | **25 event seeds** | Separate event packets `EV-HN-1003` through `EV-HN-1027`

## Method and count discipline

The established tracker identifies a Guardian story as an **event seed**, not a newly recovered independent source. This batch checks for *additional* original reporting in contemporary newspapers/newsrooms. **Grade A** means a contemporaneous, independently reported newspaper article was opened and substantive article-body material was read. **Grade B** means a specifically identified original newspaper article was located, but its text could not be read; it is **not** represented as verified body evidence. Syndicated Associated Press/Reuters/Bloomberg reprints, science/trade websites, broadcasting sites, official NASA statements, repeat links and repeated articles from one outlet are not counted as independent newspaper finds. Links below go to the publisher, not to archive mirrors. The candidate source date may differ by a day from the event date if an original follow-up covered the same event.

**Result: 47 Grade A + 3 Grade B = 50 additional source leads.** All 25 tracker seeds were addressed. One seed (HN-1023) has **no additional qualifying independent newspaper source**, even though the event is well supported by NASA and original specialist reporting; that gap is explicitly preserved.

## Cross-packet constraints retained

1. **Two Minneapolis deaths:** Renée Good (7 January, packet HN-1012) and Alex Pretti (24 January, HN-1015) must remain two distinct individuals/incidents; the newer investigation must not overwrite the earlier case.
2. **One Artemis II crewed flight, four milestones:** HN-1016 fuel-test postponement, HN-1023 second rollout, HN-1024 1 April launch, HN-1026 10 April return. Treat as four dated milestones of the **same mission**. Firefly's robotic Moon landing HN-1006 is different.
3. **One continuing Iran war, separate developments:** HN-1019 initiation of US–Israeli strikes, HN-1020 shipping impact, HN-1025 provisional pause. HN-1013 January Iranian protests are chronologically and substantively distinct.
4. **Contemporaneous estimates remain estimates:** Wildfire acreage, measles cases, crash/flood casualty counts, Iran protest deaths and Hungarian seat projections evolved. A report written earlier is not silently updated with later totals.
5. **No syndication inflation:** The repeatedly republished AP accounts for the Potomac collision and Artemis rollout are not multiple independent reporting sources. Paywalled FT articles remain B even when search snippets sound definitive.
6. **No claim of exhaustive archive coverage:** These are research results for this batch, not proof that no other original newspaper articles exist. In particular, HN-1023 remains a newspaper-discovery gap.
