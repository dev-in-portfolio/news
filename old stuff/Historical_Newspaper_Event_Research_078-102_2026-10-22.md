## Method and status

This pass looks for distinct *newspaper issue/article appearances* about the same event across different publications. The first seed paper listed in the tracker is **not** counted as a new discovery. A direct issue contains inspectable contemporaneous newspaper article text or a period page; a dated issue lead comes only from a later transcription, bibliographic citation, or restricted-access index and awaits inspection. A dated foreign dispatch or a dated letter **is not** automatically a dated newspaper issue. One issue can contain more than one article, but we do not assume that an auto-extracted multi-page listing proves separate articles. All article identity and claims are provisional.
