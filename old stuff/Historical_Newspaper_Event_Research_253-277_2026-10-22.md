# Historical newspaper coverage finding register — EV-HN-253–277

Research pass: October 22, 2026 (Eastern) · **Coverage finding only** · All 25 packets remain open

## Scope, evidence and counting rules

This is the next **25-seed batch** in the 1,056-discovery historical newspaper project. The Indiana original for each packet is an **anchor, not an additional publication**. We are collecting contemporary reporting from as many different papers and issue dates as can actually be identified, including follow-ups, inquests and conflicting early dispatches. A shared telegraph dispatch printed by ten papers is ten **newspaper appearances**, not ten independent investigations or ten incidents. This pass is **not exhaustive**.

- **A** — The actual dated original newspaper issue text, image/OCR, or an identifiable, readable reproduction of its contemporary words is available. An accurately attributed excerpt of a newspaper is A with a *reproduction* qualifier, not proof the full original page was examined.
- **B** — A specific newspaper **and issue date** are tied to the event in an issue index, catalogue or bibliographic citation, but the article text has not been inspected.
- **C** — Name, issue date, event connection or attribution remains uncertain. These are **not counted**.
- One count for **newspaper title + dated issue** per packet, even if multiple articles or pages. No cross-packet global deduplication is claimed. No original Indiana seed is counted. News-oriented illustrated weeklies are included; scholarly and commercial journals are identified separately below.
- Contemporary accusations, headline casualty totals and explanatory theories remain *claims made at the time*, never independently established facts merely because they were printed.
