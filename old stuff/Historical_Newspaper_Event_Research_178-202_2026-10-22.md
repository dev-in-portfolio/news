# Historical newspaper coverage finding register — EV-HN-178–202

Research pass: October 22, 2026 · Stage: **find appearances**, not event adjudication or category assignment

## Scope and counting

This pass follows the previously established 1,056-event discovery set and the event-by-event coverage expansion. It addresses existing packets **EV-HN-178 through EV-HN-202** (25 incidents), preserving the original seed article for each. The tables below list **additional dated publication appearances or issue-level leads**, not 25 new events. Newspaper stories, reprints, follow-up legal reporting, and contemporary newspaper illustrations are eligible; each different publication/issue is separately discoverable. Multiple articles in the same issue count as **one issue-level appearance** here, with individual articles noted where known.

Evidence marks are deliberately conservative:

- **A — period issue text/image directly locatable** in an archive or direct contemporary transcription. Original OCR may contain errors; article-to-incident match must still be read carefully.
- **B — dated issue attested** by an archive index, contemporary reprint attribution, catalogue, historical source quoting the period article, or sourced bibliography. The underlying page/article has not been fully read in this pass.
- **C — search lead only**: a date, newspaper identity, precise event match, or article relevance remains unresolved. These are excluded from the firm A/B list and count.

**Important:** B is not a claim that an original scan was independently inspected. The seed locator itself is not repeated as a newly found article. Event dates and print dates differ; contradictory death counts and early mistaken reports are retained as claims made by the press, not accepted as established fact. All 25 incidents remain **open** for further publication finding.
