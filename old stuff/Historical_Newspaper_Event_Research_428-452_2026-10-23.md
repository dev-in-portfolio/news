# Historical Newspaper Event Research — HN-428–HN-452

**Research date:** October 23, 2026  
**Scope:** 25 existing discovery IDs, from the July 1921 Sacco–Vanzetti verdict to initial May 1927 Bath School bombing reports.  
**Purpose:** Find **additional newspaper appearances from different publications** for each already-known event. This is a finding pass, not a rewritten historical narrative or an exhaustive claim.

## Result and method

- **25 discovery IDs examined:** HN-428–HN-452 inclusive.
- **59 category-A appearances** recorded on retrievable contemporary newspaper pages/issues (or their original archived OCR/PDF).
- **7 category-B article/issue leads** for later manual retrieval or page-level inspection.
- **25 original Indiana newspaper seeds excluded from the new-appearance count.** No event has been closed or called exhaustively researched.
- Counts represent **publication appearances, not 59 distinct events or necessarily 59 distinct wire-service reports**. Separately dated updates by a given publication can also differ substantively; only one appearance per title is counted in each event row below.

**A** = original contemporary newspaper issue/page or primary archive text with the event appearance located. A does **not** mean every column, caption, and continuation on that page has been transcribed.  
**B** = indexed/bibliographically documented dated article or issue where the original relevant newspaper page was not sufficiently inspected; not upgraded to A.  
**Seed** = the article identified in the discovery master, included here to anchor event identity but **not** counted as a new discovery.
