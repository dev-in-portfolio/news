# Historical newspaper publication discovery — HN-703–HN-727

**Discovery ledger | October 23, 2026 | Batch of 25 locked events**

## Scope and reading key

This is an **article-finding pass**, not a new event inventory, historical-conclusion audit, or claim that all newspaper pages have been read. HN numbers and anchor-event wording remain locked. We looked for **additional newspaper titles** reporting each event; the Indiana anchor is linked separately and **is not included in the new-lead counts**. A newspaper title is counted at most once per HN event even when two of its articles are linked. The same title may legitimately appear for different events.

- **A — original contemporary article/page text inspected** in a digitized historical newspaper or newspaper-publisher archive. Inspect the page in its original layout before citing exact words.
- **B — contemporary newspaper article/issue identified**, but the article text/page has not been completely inspected here. A NewspaperArchive result may be an indexed preview; a modern history citing a 1970s story is a *retrieval lead*, not the original article itself.
- **Context / hold — not counted:** related framing, uncertain original article match, unspecified date, or a paper issue found without its specific article confirmed. These remain candidates to retrieve, not discoveries presented as checked.

**Counts are distinct newspaper–event leads, not unique articles, unique newspaper titles across the full batch, or reviewed complete texts.** B entries and contextual leads require follow-up retrieval. Coverage surrounding the seed event is included where clearly labeled, including adjacent days; that does not imply matching its precise editorial angle.
