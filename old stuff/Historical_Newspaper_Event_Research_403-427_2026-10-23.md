# Historical newspaper research — article-finding pass HN-403–427

**Date:** October 23, 2026  
**Source:** The established 1,056-row discovery tracker, *Seed queue*, HN-403 through HN-427. The original workbook and event classification are unchanged.

**Result:** 63 inspected contemporary newspaper appearances (**A**) and eight dated publication leads pending full original inspection (**B**) across 25 tracker rows. These are appearances *within source rows*, not 71 unique historical events.

## Method and boundaries

The work here is to **find contemporary articles from different publications**, not to decide what ultimately happened, repeat modern histories, or claim that all newspapers have been found.

- **A — original newspaper appearance inspected:** The linked original archive page, legible OCR, PDF, or original article transcription contains relevant contemporary reporting. Each entry is one newspaper on one issue date. An issue is counted only once within a source row.
- **B — specific dated newspaper lead, not inspected:** A newspaper, date, and relevant headline or excerpt are identified by a documentary index, restricted catalog, or secondary reproduction, but the original article was not independently read. These are **not counted as recovered articles**.
- **Indiana seeds are excluded from the totals**, even when the original seed is linked for orientation.
- Coverage is not exhaustive. Reports from successive days can contradict one another. Estimates, speculation, allegations, and discriminatory framing are recorded as **claims made by the press at that time**. A syndicated story reproduced in several papers is evidence of circulation, not multiple independent eyewitness accounts.
- HN-415/416 concern different reporting stages of the **same Chicago racial violence**; HN-419/420/425 concern phases of the **Newberry prosecution**; HN-421/422 concern distinct **ratification and certification** stages; HN-423/424 concern evolving reports of **one Wall Street explosion**; HN-426/427 concern different editions and estimates of **one Tulsa attack**. They are not independent events simply because the seed list assigns separate HN IDs.
