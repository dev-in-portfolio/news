# Historical Newspaper Event Research — HN-653–HN-677

**Discovery pass | 23 October 2026 | 25 fixed events (1965–1970)**

## Scope and reading key

This continues the locked 1,056-event inventory, with **no new event IDs**. The Indiana source newspaper that supplied each inventory item is excluded *for that item*; discoveries below are additional, independently titled contemporary newspapers. A = a dated, readable original newspaper page/article or its archived original text; B = a dated, specifically identified original newspaper report attested by a newspaper archive, library, collector, or documented reproduction, without sufficient original-page inspection to qualify as A. **HOLD** = contextual, under-specified, mismatched reporting stage, or non-newspaper material; not counted. The count is by distinct newspaper **title for each event**, not by article or wire agency. Shared AP/UPI reporting in two independently printed newspapers can count twice; a paper's multiple articles or editions count once. Original historical casualty figures are retained as *contemporary claims*, not adopted as settled totals. This is a finding ledger, **not** a full-text acquisition or an assertion that every newspaper was found.
