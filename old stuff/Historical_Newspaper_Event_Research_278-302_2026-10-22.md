The source of packet order and original headlines is the existing `Historical_Newspaper_Event_Research_Tracker_v0.1.xlsx`, **Seed queue**, rows HN-278–HN-302. The tracker was read but **not edited**. The Hoosier State Chronicles newspaper attached to each original packet is a **seed**, excluded from additional-appearance counts.

## Evidence rule and count

- **A — text-bearing contemporary newspaper evidence:** date and newspaper title plus digitized issue/page OCR, issue text, or a substantial contemporaneous article transcription tied to that dated issue. An archived clipping or issue image indexed with the precise article also qualifies if its issue is identifiable.
- **B — dated issue identification only:** the newspaper title, issue date and event-specific article are identified in an index, historical citation or original-issue sale record, but the article's text is not independently inspected.
- **C — possible lead, not counted:** title/date or identity uncertain, only a retrospective source, different incident, or an unverified date.
- Each distinct **newspaper title + date** is one additional appearance within one packet. Subsequent reports from the same paper on **different dates** may be counted; the same paper's page duplicates cannot. Newspaper publication dates are not incident dates. Periodicals and government reports are noted as **supplemental** unless the item is clearly a contemporary illustrated newspaper.
