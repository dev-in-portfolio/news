# Historical Newspaper Event Research — HN-853–877

Research ledger | 23 October 2026 | Source period: 2000–2004

## Method and summary

This is the next 25-seed installment after HN-828–852. The seeds are cataloged articles in the Indiana historical-newspaper collection, not counted as independent corroborating titles. Each credited lead is a **distinct contemporary newspaper title for that seed**; multiple articles in one title count only once per row. An article can corroborate the *underlying event* while leaving the precise Indiana interview, individual's participation, particular editorial conclusion, or causal theory unverified. **A** = original period newspaper report/editorial text opened and examined; **B** = dated/indexed contemporary newspaper citation whose body was not inspected; **Hold** = specific claim or source not independently substantiated and therefore **not counted**. An archived, faithfully republished 2001 article is identified as such. Wire-service-only items and later retrospective reporting are excluded from counts.

**This installment: 25 seed records; 49 A / 0 B = 49 counted newspaper-title leads; 2 seeds without a count.** Prior confirmed cumulative HN-678–852: 175 seeds, 248 A, 173 B, 421 leads. **New cumulative HN-678–877: 200 seeds; 297 A, 173 B, 470 counted leads.** These are lead counts *by seed*, not globally unique newspaper titles; the same newspaper may independently report several different events.
