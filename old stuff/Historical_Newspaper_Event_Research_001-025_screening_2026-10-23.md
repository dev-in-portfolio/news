## Scope and evidence rules

The authoritative `Historical_Newspaper_Event_Research_Tracker_v0.1.xlsx` remains unchanged. These are 25 **source rows**, not 25 independently established occurrences. The original Library of Congress newspaper seed for each row is excluded from additional-publication counts. Allegations in the original press remain allegations. A modern historical summary, a research bibliography, and the existence of an entire newspaper run are not qualifying additional newspaper articles.

For this early historical series:

- **P:** A specific additional original-period newspaper issue and the relevant article text/page were inspected directly, including archive transcription. This proves a publication appearance, not original editorial authorship or that the story was true.
- **I:** A specific additional newspaper and issue/date are identified in the existing tracker or a reliable period-source index, but its event-specific article has not been inspected.
- **N:** No new eligible additional newspaper appearance verified during the limited public-index screening. This is an open evidence gap, not proof none exists.

This checkpoint reused previously recorded pilot findings and sampled targeted web/archival searches for all 25 subjects. The initial public-content reader could not access the Library of Congress links; a subsequent direct inspection of the Library of Congress **Image w/Text** pages recovered the original seed OCR for HN-002–006. Those seed reports refine identities and claims but **do not count as additional newspaper appearances**. Search results heavily favored modern histories and unrelated matches; these were not promoted to historical newspaper leads.
