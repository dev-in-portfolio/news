# Historical newspaper articles: HN-603–HN-627

**Discovery pass — 23 October 2026**  
**Master inventory:** *Historical Newspaper Discoveries 1777–2026*, fixed event IDs HN-001–HN-1056.  
**Coverage:** 25 existing seed events, 1955–1959. This is **article-finding**, not event creation, exhaustive certification, or an assessment of what really happened.

## Counting and handling

- **A — located page:** a dated, named contemporary newspaper's original page or readable OCR is available and contains reporting on the specified event. The source link should lead to the relevant page or its archive.
- **B — edition identified:** a specific contemporary newspaper and issue are supported by a collector catalog, archival bibliography, or reproduced clipping, but the relevant article's original page was not fully inspected here.
- **Hold/related:** source material that concerns another stage, a later retrospective, a magazine rather than a newspaper, or an inadequately identified issue. **Excluded from A/B counts.**
- Count **unique newspaper publications per event**, not photographs, scans, clippings, wire-service copies, links, or multiple articles within one issue. An individual newspaper may appear against more than one independently numbered seed event if its coverage applies to both. Each seed's Indiana publication is deliberately excluded from the *additional-newspaper* count.
- Exact dated reports and provisional casualty totals retain their original moment in the unfolding event; do not overwrite them with modern final totals. Links sometimes point to a collection with the dated page listed rather than the image itself.
