# Historical Newspaper Event Research — HN-753–777

**Research date:** October 23, 2026  
**Master inventory:** locked 1,056 HN seed rows; category classification v1.0 unchanged (13 main / 100 sub / 361 third-level).  
**Pass scope:** HN-753 through HN-777, 25 seed rows. HN-758 and HN-759 already share master event-packet ID `EV-HN-758`; this pass does **not** silently convert them into separate historical incidents.  
**Original anchor excluded:** Hoosier State Chronicles (`newspapers.library.in.gov`) seed pages are used only to define the research targets, never counted as new findings.

## Evidence rules and result

- **A — inspected contemporary newspaper evidence:** original publisher's historical article text, or an original newspaper issue/page with extracted OCR that was opened and inspected.
- **B — indexed contemporary newspaper lead:** the publication and pertinent article/issue were identified by an index, search excerpt, or repository result, but the underlying article/page was not inspected in full; **B does not mean verified full text**.
- One counted item per **newspaper title per HN seed**. The same title can legitimately recur for different HN chronology rows; wire-service syndication does not make newspapers different original reporting organizations.
- Newspaper dates follow displayed publication/issue dates when possible. Wire dispatch dates, article-page URLs, and times of breaking-news updates can differ. Figures and proposed explanations are attributed **as contemporary reports**, not treated as final historical outcomes.
- Context-only items, later retrospectives, wire services without a newspaper issue, magazines, and non-matching Black-press coverage were not counted.

**This pass: 65 additional newspaper-title leads — 58 A / 7 B — across 24 of 25 seed rows.** HN-768 has no defensible additional match. This is a discovery ledger, **not an exhaustive search or a complete article-text collection**.

### HN-753 — Mexico City earthquake: first-day reports (September 20, 1985)

**Master seed:** magnitude initially described as 7.8; early 230-confirmed-deaths report. **New sources: 4 A.**

1. **[A] Los Angeles Times — Los Angeles, California — September 20, 1985.** “Mexico City Buildings Topple as 7.8 Quake Hits Wide Area: Death Toll High; Rubble Traps Many.” Early structural losses, communications disruptions, and rescue efforts; no settled casualty figure. [Contemporary article](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1985-09-20-mn-6296-story.html).
2. **[A] Washington Post — Washington, D.C. — September 20 dispatch / September 21 archive.** “Victims Describe Terror, Tragedy.” Eyewitness reports, especially collapse and rescue at Juárez Hospital. [Contemporary article](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1985/09/21/victims-describe-terror-tragedy/09d38e6d-2470-4467-9999-c2044f913e94/).
3. **[A] Lawton Morning Press — Lawton, Oklahoma — September 20, 1985, p. 1.** “Major Earthquake Hits Mexico”; original issue OCR reports magnitude 7.8 and an early police figure of at least **170** dead. [Original issue/OCR](https://gateway.okhistory.org/ark:/67531/metadc2568378/m1/1/).
4. **[A] Muskogee Daily Phoenix and Times-Democrat — Muskogee, Oklahoma — September 20, 1985, p. 1.** “Quake jolts Mexico City”; original OCR includes a contemporaneous television-station estimate of at least **250** deaths, while also acknowledging other, lower partial reports. [Original issue/OCR](https://gateway.okhistory.org/ark:/67531/metadc2641966/m1/1/).

**Chronology caution:** 170, 230, and 250 reflect different early accounts, not contradictory modern final tallies.
