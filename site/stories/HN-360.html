Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Semi-Weekly Republican, p. 2 — original page; NIOSH historical mining research.
Report: The newspaper said 400 miners had been entombed by a December 19 blast at the Darr Mine near Jacobs Creek, Pennsylvania, while smoke, damaged passages, and fire hindered rescue. It noted the catastrophe followed Monongah and another severe mine incident in a matter of weeks.
Assessment: Federal mining research records 239 deaths at Darr, not 400. The article said the source of the blast was then uncertain; later research classifies it as a gas-and-dust explosion. The original identifies an early number feared trapped, not the definitive death register.
