Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2, Barnwell and Charleston, South Carolina dispatches — original page.
Report: A disguised crowd overwhelmed jail staff in Barnwell, South Carolina, seized eight Black men, and shot them dead outside town. The account referred to a previous killing as the pretext for collective vengeance; its own Charleston follow-up stated the broader community denounced the violence and feared further reprisals.
Assessment: Reported mass lynching rather than the lawful disposition of charges. The newspaper's categorical declaration of the prisoners' guilt is not a trial record; the eight individuals' identities require further work. Follow-up open.
