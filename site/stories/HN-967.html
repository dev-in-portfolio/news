Discovery narrative

Source: The Guardian, April 15 — original Paris fire coverage.
Report: Fire devastated the roof of Notre-Dame Cathedral, destroyed its spire and threatened the bell towers; firefighters later said the two towers had been saved.
Assessment: Roof and spire destruction did not mean the entire stone structure collapsed. The fire's precise origin was still under investigation, and images of the burning roof were not proof of an intentional attack.
