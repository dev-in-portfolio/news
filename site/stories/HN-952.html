Recorded finding

Pulse June2016 early count '50 dead' versus later '49 victims' clarified that 50 includes attacker and 49 excludes him.

Discovery narrative

Source: The Guardian, June 12 — contemporaneous eyewitness reconstruction.
Report: Survivors described fleeing gunfire at Orlando's Pulse nightclub while families waited for word about people inside. Early updates referred to around 50 fatalities.
Assessment: The final victim count is 49, with the gunman separately killed. Some first-day totals included the attacker, and initial witness timelines were incomplete. Do not read preliminary totals as conflicting victim lists.

Assessment: LA Times June12 vs Washington Post June12/13. Different denominators, not disappearance of a victim.
