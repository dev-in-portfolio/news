Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 1 — original report; Columbia Accident Investigation Board report.
Report: A student investigation summary described Columbia at high altitude, its return expected within minutes, and raised the possibility that carrying many experiments meant the shuttle's payload might have been too heavy.
Assessment: The payload conjecture is not the established accident cause. The later Columbia board traced fatal thermal-protection damage to foam striking the left-wing leading edge during launch, followed by hot-gas penetration at reentry; it also identified institutional safety failures.
