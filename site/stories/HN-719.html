Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 1 — original page; NASA retrospective.
Report: The first-day Associated Press account put the falling laboratory's debris in the Indian Ocean southwest of Australia and stated that no pieces had been reported on land.
Assessment: An initial tracking report revised by observations from Australia. NASA confirms Skylab reentered July 11 and that, although most wreckage fell in the Indian Ocean, some material landed in sparsely populated Western Australia. The reporter's phrase captured what was known at that moment, not the final debris map.
