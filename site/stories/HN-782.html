Report: East Germans passed through newly opened crossings, people celebrated atop the Wall, and the paper reported a momentous reversal of the border controls that had divided Berlin since 1961.
Assessment: The November 9 border opening and the later German state reunification are different milestones. Formal unification occurred October 3, 1990, following political and treaty negotiations; the newspaper's jubilant “Wall goneâ€ headline was a description of a symbolic change, not yet the physical removal of all barriers.
