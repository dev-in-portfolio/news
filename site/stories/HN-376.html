Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page; Smithsonian National Museum of American History.
Report: A short account said the fire at New York's Triangle Waist Company killed 141 garment workers, many young women, in the upper floors of a factory building. It blamed a discarded cigarette near accumulated textile waste and inadequate escape measures, including insufficient exterior fire access.
Assessment: The Smithsonian records 146 deaths, not the dispatch's 141. The article's smoking-material explanation is an early proposed ignition mechanism, not a judgment that any specific worker was criminally responsible. The verified disaster helped bring sustained attention to factory fire safety and labor conditions.
