Report: Cyrus Vance, the U.N. envoy who had worked on Croatia's cease-fire, arrived in Sarajevo to discuss the rapidly expanding fighting among Bosnian communities. The same page described continuing violence and hopes for negotiations.
Assessment: Peace diplomacy during an ongoing war, not a cease-fire already achieved. The contemporaneous article's ethnic and military categories reflect its reporting context; questions of criminal responsibility or the complete chronology of Bosnia's atrocities require later judicial and archival records.
