Discovery narrative

Source: Daily Wabash Express, p. 2, sharply partisan editorial reproducing its own February 28, 1866 dispatch — original page.
Report: The editor attacked Thomas A. Hendricks for publicly citing a $100,000 gift to Ulysses S. Grant as if it had been presented in 1869, after Grant's election. To refute that chronology, the newspaper reprinted its 1866 Associated Press notice of the gift and named contributors, arguing that the later date made an ordinary gift appear like an attempt to influence a president.
Assessment: A documentable date dispute embedded in political invective. The old dispatch supports the 1866 date; the paper's assertions about Hendricks's intent and personal responsibility for a “forgeryâ€ are accusations, not independently established findings.
