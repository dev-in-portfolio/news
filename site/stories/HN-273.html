Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2, Boston special — original page.
Report: A Boston & Providence commuter train plunged through the Bussey Bridge between Forest Hills and Roslindale. Several packed coaches dropped into the roadway; neighbors and firemen hurried to rescue passengers. The headline declared 30 dead, while the article's body counted at least 25 and acknowledged that the injuries and fatalities could not yet be settled. It assigned possible responsibility to defects in ironwork before describing a completed structural investigation.
Assessment: Contemporaneous collapse and rescue reporting with conflicting early totals and a preliminary cause theory. Final findings and the roster need the official investigation. Follow-up open.
