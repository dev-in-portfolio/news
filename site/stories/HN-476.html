Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; FBI case history.
Report: The newspaper announced Charles Lindbergh's willingness to pay ransom after 20-month-old Charles Lindbergh Jr. was taken from the family home. Its illustrated scene of the abduction was explicitly an artist's conception, not a photograph or eyewitness reconstruction.
