Recorded finding

1898 'plague discovered in San Francisco' newspaper rumor vs contemporaneous federal finding of no evidence.

Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page; CDC historical investigation; CDC epidemiological study.
Report: The paper asserted that “Asiatic cholera, or the bubonic plagueâ€ had appeared in San Francisco's Chinatown and claimed quarantine officials and local newspapers were suppressing information. It gave no case confirmation in this short report and conflated two distinct infectious diseases.
Assessment: Do not repeat the claimed 1898 outbreak as fact: CDC historical accounts identify March 1900 as the first documented local human plague case. This 1898 item is a contemporary scare or unverified rumor, though later San Francisco plague cases were real. The paper's suppression allegation is not independently substantiated for 1898.
