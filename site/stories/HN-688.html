Source: Jewish Post, p. 3 — original page.
Report: A follow-up page described religious memorial services in American cities for the murdered Israeli delegation and debates over public sympathy, political responsibility, and proposed responses.
Assessment: A valuable community-press perspective. A memorial service, a religious leader's quoted position, and a proposed security response are distinct activities, and this newspaper does not establish that all such proposals became law or that any single constituency spoke with one voice.
