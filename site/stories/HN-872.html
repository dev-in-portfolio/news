Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 11 — original opinion page; U.S. Department of Energy blackout account.
Report: After the August 14 outage across the Northeast and Ontario, an opinion writer weighed proposed causes including aging infrastructure, demand, and deregulation, with 50 million people reported affected.
Assessment: These were explanations under discussion, not all proven causes of the outage. The joint U.S.–Canada task force investigated a sequence of technical and operational failures; the article should be read as a period debate rather than the final causal report.
