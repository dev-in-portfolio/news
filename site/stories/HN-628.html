Discovery narrative

Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page; NASA, early spaceflight chronology.
Report: A story on proposed U.S. moon launches noted that the Soviet Union had just launched Lunik III, frustrating hopes that an American spacecraft would make the next lunar breakthrough.
Assessment: Luna 3 later returned the first photographs of the Moon's far side. That later achievement should not be backdated to October 5 as if this paper had already seen and verified the images; it reported a launch and plans, not the completed photographic result.
