Recorded finding

1905 Vladivostok mutiny foreign wires gave competing accounts of severity, fire and loss of life.

Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Semi-Weekly Republican, p. 2 — original page.
Report: Dispatches from Vladivostok during Russia's revolutionary unrest claimed that mutinous soldiers and sailors had fought loyalist troops and that much of the city was burning. The newspaper's headline said more than 800 of the tsar's soldiers killed, but the article body gave a different figure of more than 500, drawing on reports whose communication and geographic details were unclear.
Assessment: The contradiction is inside the original article. Neither casualty figure nor the claim that most of the city had burned can be treated as verified on this evidence. Follow-up open in Russian and international contemporary records.
