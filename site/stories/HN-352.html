Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Semi-Weekly Republican, p. 2 — original page; Pas-de-Calais departmental archives.
Report: In the aftermath of the March 10 Courrières mining disaster, the paper reported roughly 1,100 victims in the Pas-de-Calais coalfield, an earlier underground fire, blocked passages, and rescue efforts eventually abandoned because of heat and smoke. It asserted that galleries had collapsed and the trapped miners were doomed.
Assessment: The departmental archives document 1,099 fatalities and identify a coal-dust ignition. The death estimate was remarkably close, but the newspaper's declaration that rescue was impossible should not be treated as a final account of all survivors or the completed inquiry's explanation.
