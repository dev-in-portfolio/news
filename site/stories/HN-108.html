Report: A short item claimed a family returned from one child's funeral to find another daughter apparently dead. It alleged she had been placed in a coffin, then suddenly moved, rose, and called for her mother while relatives kept watch.
Assessment: Unattributed, secondhand extraordinary claim, printed without names, medical records, or a verifiable witness. It is not established as fact. Follow-up open.
