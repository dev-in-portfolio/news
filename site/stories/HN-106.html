Discovery narrative

Source: Indiana Palladium, p. 2, printing an account attributed to Indian agent Col. Pepper — original page.
Report: The paper detailed the Fort Wayne trial of Nowelinggua (spelling uncertain), a Miami man charged with killing a woman named Wishmah. Counsel argued about Miami custom, a treaty's reservation of hunting rights, and whether Indiana criminal law applied; the judge rejected that argument. The jury convicted him of manslaughter, reportedly imposing two years' imprisonment and a one-cent fine, while recommending clemency.
Assessment: Detailed but culturally biased contemporary courtroom report, including offensive characterizations of Indigenous people and disputed descriptions of Wishmah's status. The outcome of the clemency request has not been established. Follow-up open.
