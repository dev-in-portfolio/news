Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page.
Report: Officers reported 61 prisoners in the investigation of masked night-rider violence around Reelfoot Lake, Tennessee, after the killing of Captain Rankin. The newspaper included a woman's stated intention to testify and fresh accounts of armed masked men demanding food or stopping travelers.
Assessment: These are reports of arrests, witness claims, and intimidation, not 61 proven convictions or a verified membership list. The unsigned threats quoted in the dispatch cannot identify their author. The causes and subsequent court outcomes need Tennessee judicial records. Follow-up open.
