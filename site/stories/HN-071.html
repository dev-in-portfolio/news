Discovery narrative

Source: Western Sun (Vincennes), p. 2, reprinting a letter from Danbury, Connecticut — original page.
Report: The letter alleged that Abel H. Platt killed his wife and two children in New Fairfield, injured his mother when she tried to intervene, and then died by suicide. It described earlier erratic behavior and reported that an inquest returned a verdict of insanity.
Assessment: Detailed but reprinted eyewitness-style letter and reported inquest, not a medical finding established by this research. Original inquest papers and corrections have not been inspected. Follow-up open.
