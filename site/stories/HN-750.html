Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; FBI account identifying the victim.
Report: The Associated Press reported a hijacked TWA jet returning from Beirut to Algiers, the release of an American man and his daughter, and the hijackers' claim that a man killed in Beirut was a U.S. Marine.
Assessment: An important identity correction. The man murdered in Beirut was Robert Stethem, a U.S. Navy diver, not a Marine as the hijackers reportedly claimed. The FBI's later history confirms his identity and killing. The unfolding hostage reports, identity claims, and eventual release chronology should not be flattened into one contemporaneous certainty.
