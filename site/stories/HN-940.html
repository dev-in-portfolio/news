Report: The paper discusses Thomas Eric Duncan, who arrived from Liberia, was initially discharged from a Texas hospital, received an Ebola diagnosis, and died on October 8, while considering medical response and public fear.
Assessment: The central clinical chronology is supported by CDC, but allegations of widespread domestic transmission are unwarranted. CDC identifies two nurses infected while caring for a patient in the U.S.; separate imported cases do not imply uncontrolled national community spread.
