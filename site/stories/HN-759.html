Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; Rogers Commission technical findings.
Report: Investigators examined large fuselage pieces, considered whether a blowtorch-like flame from a booster rocket caused the loss, and wondered whether the crew cabin might have remained intact after the vehicle broke apart.
Assessment: An evolving investigative hypothesis, not a recovered-cabin determination. The subsequently documented joint-seal failure explains the plume, but the January 31 article's possibility that a particular module survived was still conjecture based on incomplete wreckage information.
