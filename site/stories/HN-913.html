Discovery narrative

Source: Muncie Times, p. 1 — original front-page report; CDC pandemic burden estimates.
Report: A dispatch describes international spread of the new swine-origin influenza A(H1N1) and quotes an early CDC tally of 403 U.S. cases and one death, while expecting more cases and hospitalizations.
Assessment: An early surveillance snapshot, not the eventual epidemic total. CDC later estimated approximately 60.8 million U.S. illnesses, 274,304 hospitalizations and 12,469 deaths for April 2009–April 2010, with ranges reflecting statistical uncertainty.
