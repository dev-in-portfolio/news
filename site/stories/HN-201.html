Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Daily Herald, p. 1, telegraphic news summary — original page.
Report: New reports from Memphis concerned the explosion of the steamboat Miami on the Arkansas River. The dispatch estimated that the death toll might reach 150, said only seven crew members were known to have survived, and described bodies recovered and seriously injured people hospitalized at Helena.
Assessment: Early disaster estimate, not a verified final casualty list. The archive page reports the event and figures but does not provide an accident investigation. Follow-up open.
