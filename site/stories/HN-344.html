Discovery narrative

Source: Richmond Palladium (Daily), p. 1 — original page.
Report: Rev. Elwood O. Ellis, pastor of a Friends congregation on South Eighth Street in Richmond, Indiana, had disappeared. Police and friends searched waterways, while relatives speculated that he might have become ill or distressed. The newspaper reports contradictory sightings and says his bicycle was still at home. His wife and mother were awaiting news, with no confirmed location or fate.
Assessment: A missing-person search; suicide, mental illness, and criminal acts were unconfirmed theories. The newspaper's OCR header misreads the year as 1994, but the issue date and 1904 political news establish the publication is from 1904. Follow-up open.
