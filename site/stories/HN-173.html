Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper Banner, p. 9, reprinting a Sentinel report — original page; the July Stocking trial coverage also uses Driscoll, whereas the January 1856 account spells the name Driskill.
Report: The newspaper said that once the Supreme Court records arrived for Abraham Rice, Timothy Driscoll (Driskill in later coverage), and David M. Stocking, Governor Joseph A. Wright delayed their executions because the court could not hear the matters until its November term. The newly listed date was January 11, 1856.
Assessment: Contemporary follow-up materially qualifying HN-170. This September article reports a postponement, but a January 1856 report (HN-174) subsequently describes the executions as having occurred. The appellate history has not been reconstructed from court records.
