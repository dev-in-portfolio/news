Discovery narrative

Source: The New-York Packet, p. 3 — original page.
Report: A short item named Elisha Fobes of Southwick and Joseph French of Chester, Massachusetts, as jailed in Litchfield, Connecticut, pending trial for allegedly counterfeiting and circulating that state's paper currency. Directly afterward the page reported an additional six-week reprieve, according to rumor, for four Massachusetts prisoners sentenced to death for treason.
Assessment: Specific arrests and a separately reported reprieve, with no guilt or sentence confirmed for the counterfeit defendants. Court and pardon records remain to be checked. Follow-up open.
