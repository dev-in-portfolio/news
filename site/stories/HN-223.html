Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Journal, p. 1, New Bedford telegram — original page.
Report: The schooner Abbie Bradford reportedly brought word of crews from two New Bedford whaling vessels, the Ossay Taft and Ansel Gibbs, stranded near Marble Island, Hudson Bay. Men were said to have lived ashore for many months, and fourteen reportedly died of scurvy and exposure before survivors were retrieved.
Assessment: A specific maritime survival report, but the digitized OCR displays a wreck year later than its 1873 publication, an impossibility requiring page-image review. This entry therefore does not assert the day or year of the actual wrecks. The casualty number also awaits corroboration from logs. Follow-up open.
