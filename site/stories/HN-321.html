Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page; West Virginia mine-safety disaster register; National Park Service history.
Report: A dispatch said an explosion at the Red Ash mine near Thurmond, West Virginia, had killed nearly 125 workers, with none expected to survive. It described wrecked workings and cars hurled by the blast. The article speculated about “firedampâ€ without a surviving eyewitness account of ignition.
Assessment: The early death toll was dramatically wrong: the West Virginia government's historical register and the NPS document 46 deaths on March 6, 1900. Do not preserve the printed 125 as the true toll or treat the paper's proposed ignition as verified.
