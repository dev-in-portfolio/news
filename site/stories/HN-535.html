Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; NFPA, nightclub and assembly occupancy fire history.
Report: A photograph of a crowded exit vestibule accompanied the latest death toll, 478, after the November 28 fire in Boston's Cocoanut Grove nightclub. Investigators were beginning to examine possible prosecutions and fire-safety failures.
Assessment: NFPA's retrospective account uses 492 fatalities. First reports could not yet reconcile all bodies and hospital deaths; the highlighted exits illustrate lethal evacuation problems, not conclusive proof of the precise ignition source or any single individual's criminal intent.
