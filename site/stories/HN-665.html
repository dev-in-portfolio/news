Report: Under “Worst riots launched by whites in Detroit and N.Y. in 1863,â€ a news-service essay argued that descriptions of 1967 Detroit as the country's worst racial conflict overlooked anti-Black mob violence in Detroit and New York during the Civil War.
Assessment: A historically grounded challenge to a superlative, not a complete statistical comparison. Racial violence in 1863, particularly the New York draft riots, is documented, but severity depends on whether one compares deaths, displacement, duration, or property damage. This article helps recover historical memory obscured by the 1967 headline cycle; its exact 1863 figures need separate primary checking.
