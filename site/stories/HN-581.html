Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page.
Report: Officials ordered an investigation after two Long Island Rail Road electric commuter trains collided near Kew Gardens, Queens. The newspaper reported 78 dead and almost 300 hurt, describing a following train striking a stalled rush-hour local.
Assessment: This was a documented catastrophic rear-end crash on November 22; the November 23 date is publication. The article's quoted speed estimate is eyewitness description, not an instrument reading or completed technical investigation. Detailed cause allocation is follow-up open.
