Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page; later verdict report HN-395.
Report: A special grand jury indicted new Terre Haute mayor Donn M. Roberts for alleged bribery, fraudulent voter registration, and ballot-box manipulation. The newspaper said investigators alleged payments to precinct workers for fictitious voter names; Roberts posted bond and declined comment.
Assessment: These are indictments and alleged particulars, not a January conviction. The follow-up in 1915 reports an actual election-fraud verdict (HN-395); the intervening courtroom record would be necessary to assign responsibility for each charged count.
