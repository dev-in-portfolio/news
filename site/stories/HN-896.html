Discovery narrative

Source: Muncie Times, p. 9 — original report; GAO examination.
Report: A report on the Children's Defense Fund's Katrina's Children called for mental-health treatment, mobile health facilities, school-based clinics, and easier Medicaid access for displaced and traumatized children.
Assessment: A documented advocacy program and unmet-needs warning, not proof every measure was enacted. Subsequent GAO examinations show why schooling and child welfare posed substantial logistical problems, but intervention outcomes require separate data.
