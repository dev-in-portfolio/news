Recorded finding

1891 Mammoth mine: Evening Capital Journal 28 January says 107 bodies recovered; later histories cite 109 deaths.

Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2 — original page; NIOSH technical history, p. 6.
Report: After the January 27 Mammoth No. 1 mine explosion in Pennsylvania, the newspaper's headline counted 130 deaths, its first body text stated 110, and a later passage asserted 151. It described lethal afterdamp, recovery efforts, and competing claims about inspection and ventilation. A federal mine-safety technical review citing historical Bureau of Mines research records 109 miners killed and associates the gas ignition with a roof fall.
Assessment: Three inconsistent contemporary figures superseded by a better-documented federal historical count of 109, not proof of misreporting by any named worker. Corroborated count and historical mechanism; individual accountability not established.
