Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; Library of Congress Great Depression primary-source overview.
Report: The front page included a photograph of police and Bonus Army veterans fighting after officers began evicting demonstrators from federal property; its caption reported a veteran shot dead.
Assessment: Thousands of World War I veterans and families sought early payment of promised service bonuses during the Depression. The confrontation and forced clearance were real, but a caption's interpretation of who initiated each blow is not a complete account of the police and military operation.
