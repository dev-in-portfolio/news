Discovery narrative

Source: Daily Evansville Journal, p. 3, dispatch from the fighting near Richmond — original page; Library of Congress historical context.
Report: The article said Lowe's balloon observed a battle near Fair Oaks/Seven Pines from roughly 2,000 feet, with Parker Spring operating a telegraph wire to send observations about Confederate movements to General McClellan. The correspondent heralded this as a landmark wartime use of an airborne telegraph station.
Assessment: Period account of an early military observation-and-telegraph operation, supported in broad historical context by the Library of Congress; specific claims of technological priority remain the correspondent's. Follow-up partially present.
