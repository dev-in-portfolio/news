Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 7 — original page; U.S. State Department, Chile 1969–1973.
Report: “Chile's Ruling Military Junta Rounds Up Allende Supportersâ€ reported arrests and President Salvador Allende's death after the September 11 coup, while explaining the overthrow chiefly through unrest, strikes, and alleged political failures of Allende's government.
Assessment: A real coup, with a partial contemporary narrative. Later U.S. diplomatic records document covert U.S. involvement in Chilean politics and support for opposition activity, though the particulars of direct involvement in the September 11 operation require careful documentary distinction. The article's explanations of blame are not independent judgments settling responsibility; Allende's death was later established as suicide.
