Discovery narrative

Source: Jewish Post, p. 10 — original opinion page; NTSB investigation.
Report: Rabbi Elliot B. Gertel condemned attacks on Black churches, recalled earlier synagogue arsons, and expressed interfaith solidarity. While warning about hatred, he wrote conditionally that Flight 800 may have resulted from a bomb.
Assessment: A contemporary fear and speculative analogy, not proof of a bomb aboard the aircraft or of a connection to church arsons. The NTSB later attributed the disaster to a fuel-air explosion in the center-wing tank; it did not identify a terrorist bomb. The page demonstrates how unconfirmed disaster explanations entered wider moral commentary.
