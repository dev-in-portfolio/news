Discovery narrative

Source: Gazette of the United States, p. 4 — original page.
Report: A story described a woman frightened from her home by an alleged black-robed apparition and extraordinary noises. Crowds gathered. A man agreed to watch overnight with a dog and loaded pistols; he reported hearing sounds but seeing no ghost, and the apparent haunting ceased.
Assessment: Period ghost-and-skepticism anecdote, not evidence of a supernatural event. The newspaper does not establish who made the noises. Follow-up open.
