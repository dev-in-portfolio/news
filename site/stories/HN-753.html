Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; USGS event record.
Report: Rescuers searched damaged buildings after the September 19 earthquake. The paper cited a magnitude of 7.8, at least 230 confirmed deaths, and roughly 1,000 people believed trapped.
