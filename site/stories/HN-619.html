Discovery narrative

Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page; NASA, animals in space history.
Report: The page reported amid uncertainty about the fate of the dog aboard Sputnik 2, launched November 3, as the Soviet space mission generated contradictory or incomplete information.
Assessment: Laika did not return; the spacecraft had no recovery system, and later evidence showed she died within hours from heat stress, much earlier than the public Soviet presentation suggested. A newspaper's uncertainty is genuine evidence of information asymmetry, not proof that the animal remained alive or that the flight was uncrewed.
