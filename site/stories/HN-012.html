Discovery narrative

Source: The Gazette of the State of South-Carolina, p. 2 — original page.
Report: The printed notice says Charleston's city council resolved to fund civic needs using a lottery after war-related financial distress. It outlines prizes, ticket prices, and deductions from winnings, explicitly describing the mechanism as a voluntary alternative to compulsory taxation.
Assessment: Primary-source ordinance/notice, not merely a rumor. Follow-up on how the lottery actually performed is open.
