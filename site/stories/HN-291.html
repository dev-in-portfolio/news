Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2 — original page.
Report: Mary Ann Armagost of David City, Nebraska, was arrested amid allegations that she poisoned a former husband, her current mother-in-law, and a stepdaughter. After initial testing on the stepdaughter was complicated by embalming fluid, investigators reportedly exhumed two older remains and found arsenic. The coroner's jury attributed two deaths to poisoning by Armagost; a preliminary criminal hearing was still to occur.
Assessment: Distinct stages: accusation, chemical testing, coroner's conclusion, pending criminal examination. No conviction or disposition is established by the page. Follow-up open.
