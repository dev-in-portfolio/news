Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2 — original page.
Report: The newspaper said roughly 30 federal prisoners working outside Fort Leavenworth broke toward a cornfield after a signal attributed to George East. Guards fired; East was reported mortally wounded and Sam Mills and S. Dove seriously injured. Their return to a penitentiary yard containing some 200 prisoners prompted a second, short-lived disturbance that Warden French reportedly quieted before another volley.
Assessment: A report of an attempted mass escape, not proof East died of his wounds or that every named prisoner played the role assigned by the correspondent. Follow-up open.
