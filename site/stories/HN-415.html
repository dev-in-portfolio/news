Discovery narrative

Source: Evening Republican, p. 2 — original page; Illinois State Archives coroner report.
Report: A dispatch on violent attacks and retaliation in Chicago said 27 people had died, described serious injuries, troops standing by, and disturbances in Black and other neighborhoods. Its headline portrayed the entire city as descending into disorder.
Assessment: The 1919 Chicago race riot followed the death of Eugene Williams, a Black teenager attacked at a segregated Lake Michigan swimming area. The later accepted total is 38 dead, with 23 Black and 15 white victims. Much of the violence targeted Black Chicagoans; the first report's 27 was provisional.
