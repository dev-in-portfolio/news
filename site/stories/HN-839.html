Discovery narrative

Source: Jewish Post, p. 8 — original page; NOAA hurricane report.
Report: A dispatch said the building of Honduras's Jewish congregation in Tegucigalpa had been destroyed in Hurricane Mitch shortly after its mortgage was paid, along with one Torah brought to Central America by refugees from Germany.
Assessment: A particular community's cultural and material loss, not the entire hurricane's impact or an independently verified inventory of sacred objects. NOAA documents catastrophic regional flood and landslide deaths; the wording “only synagogueâ€ is the newspaper's claim for that date.
