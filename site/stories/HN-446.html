Discovery narrative

Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page; PBS American Experience case history.
Report: The front page announced that evangelist Aimee Semple McPherson, missing for weeks, had been found alive near the Mexican border. Her mother recounted McPherson's telephone explanation that kidnappers had held her in Mexico before she escaped.
Assessment: Her return was real; the alleged kidnapping was a contested account, not a fact established by the headline. Investigators later alleged the disappearance was staged, but the prosecution did not prove that theory and dropped its case. Neither the kidnapping story nor its denial can be certified from this first report.
