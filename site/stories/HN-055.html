Discovery narrative

Source: The Statesman (London), p. 3, British Library digitized OCR — source collection and downloadable year file.
Report: An editorial accused rival newspapers and interested speculators of circulating false stories about negotiations between Britain and France, including diplomatic missions supposedly underway, with stock-market consequences. It contrasted its own earlier reporting with those rumors.
Assessment: A newspaper's adversarial editorial allegation, not verified proof of deliberate market manipulation. It is valuable as a contemporary account of information and speculation during wartime. Follow-up open.
