Discovery narrative

Source: The United States Oracle of the Day, supplement p. 3 — original page.
Report: A European dispatch about Bourg, France, described four condemned highway robbers obtaining cutting weapons, freeing themselves from irons, and resisting their jailer ahead of an October execution. Two reportedly died in the episode; two were taken alive toward punishment.
Assessment: Sensational foreign crime report, reprinted months after the alleged event. Names are uncertain in OCR, and the tribunal and casualty details have not been verified. Follow-up open.
