Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 1 — original page; National Park Service, Montgomery Bus Boycott.
Report: A photograph of Martin Luther King Jr. and Coretta Scott King accompanied news of his conviction for conspiring to boycott segregated buses. The caption said he was appealing a fine and hard-labor sentence, while other organizers sustained the campaign.
Assessment: This was a prosecution of the boycott's leadership, not a finding that segregated public transportation was lawful or just. The boycott continued; federal court action in Browder v. Gayle ultimately made Montgomery bus integration effective December 20, 1956. Compare HN-608–HN-609 and HN-615.
