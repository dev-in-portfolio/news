Assessment: Directly attributed witness allegation; exact quantity unverified here. Mass imprisonment, executions, torture, and disappearances under the junta are well documented, but this particular couple's observation and the apparent figure are not independently verified by the newspaper clipping. Do not convert the claim into a nationally verified casualty count.
