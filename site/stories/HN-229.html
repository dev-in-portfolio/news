Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper Weekly Courier, p. 6, reprinting a Sedalia, Missouri, eyewitness report — original page.
Report: A tornado struck Houstonia, Missouri, shattering homes and businesses, seriously injuring several residents, and reportedly blowing an entire stationary train off the tracks. The account described emergency physicians arriving by special train and first-person descriptions of the storm's course.
Assessment: Substantive eyewitness-attributed disaster report. The damage total and eventual deaths cannot be fixed from the immediate narrative. Follow-up open.
