Discovery narrative

Source: The Guardian, August 4 — first-day blast report.
Report: An enormous blast at Beirut's port shattered buildings and overwhelmed hospitals; early officials cited at least 78 dead and pointed to a large stockpile of ammonium nitrate.
Assessment: The provisional death count rose above 200. Ammonium nitrate storage and official warnings became central investigative questions; the initial report did not establish which individuals were criminally responsible.
