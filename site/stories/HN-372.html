Discovery narrative

Source: Evening Republican, p. 2 — original page.
Report: The newspaper reported 46 killed and more injured near Green Mountain, Iowa, when a Chicago, Rock Island & Pacific train, using Chicago Great Western track because of a blockage, derailed. It said rails spread, then two locomotives and passenger cars piled up, smashing a Pullman and a smoking car.
Assessment: This is a detailed initial disaster and railroad-explanation report, but not the completed accident inquiry. Its attribution to spreading rails came from early witnesses and officials; no later formal cause or final victim list was established in this pass. Follow-up open.
