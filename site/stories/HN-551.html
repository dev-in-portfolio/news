Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; National Archives, atomic bombings of Japan.
Report: A United Press dispatch conveyed Truman's announcement that the United States had used an atomic bomb against Hiroshima, with descriptions of a weapon vastly more powerful than conventional explosives and a threat of further strikes.
Assessment: The August 6 bombing is established. The newspaper's power comparison and early claims about the target are official wartime statements, not a complete independent account of a city, its civilian population, blast deaths, and subsequent radiation injuries. Hiroshima was not exclusively a military base.
