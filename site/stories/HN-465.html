Report: The story ran under “Stocks Crash Fails to Hurt Employment,â€ reporting early government assessments that October business and labor figures did not yet show broad damage from the selling panic.
Assessment: This records what officials could see in November 1929, not the eventual result. The Great Depression's employment collapse took time to unfold, so the headline is a useful example of lagging data and premature reassurance, not evidence that the downturn never affected workers.
