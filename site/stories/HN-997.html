Discovery narrative

Source: The Guardian, January 6 — original grounding announcement; NTSB accident investigation.
Report: A panel covering an unused emergency exit separated from Alaska Airlines Flight 1282 shortly after takeoff, leaving a hole in the Boeing 737 Max 9. The plane returned safely and U.S. regulators ordered temporary inspections and groundings.
Assessment: Confirmed structural separation, not an explosion or a passenger opening the door. The NTSB later identified manufacturing, training and oversight problems; the January report could not yet know the full missing-bolt sequence.
