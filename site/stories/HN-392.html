Discovery narrative

Source: Walkerton Independent, p. 2 — original page; contemporary front-page coverage; U.S. Navy's First World War context.
Report: The paper described the June 28 assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand and Duchess Sophie in Sarajevo: a failed bomb attack and subsequent pistol shots. It identified Gavrilo Princip among those arrested and asserted additional details about bulletproof clothing, advance planning, thrown-away bombs, and official knowledge that required more checking.
Assessment: The murders and their place in the prelude to World War I are established. The report's conjectures about each suspect's knowledge, every accomplice, and the purpose of each weapon are not proved by the breaking dispatch. Anti-Serb sentiment and the paper's generalizations must be distinguished from individual criminal evidence.
