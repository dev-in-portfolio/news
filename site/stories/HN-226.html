Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Union, p. 2, retrospective news account — original page.
Report: A Pennsylvania jury had convicted William E. Udderzook of murdering Winfield S. Goss. According to the account, Goss had first been falsely represented as having died in a workshop fire, enabling a disputed life-insurance claim; subsequently he lived under an alias before being killed after threatening to expose the arrangement. The article described contradictions in dental evidence offered after the supposed first death.
Assessment: A reported jury conviction in a highly unusual alleged insurance conspiracy. The paper's prediction that Udderzook would soon be hanged is not proof of any subsequent execution. Complete trial and insurance records remain to be checked. Follow-up open.
