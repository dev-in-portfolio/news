Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2, dispatches from Evansville — original page.
Report: An armed group from Posey County, Indiana, reportedly forced its way into the Evansville jail despite resistance from Sheriff Kerth and officers, then seized Hume Redman, accused of killing his wife at Mount Vernon. A gun battle followed; Redman was taken out and lynched, and David Murphy, identified as a relative of the deceased wife, was also killed. The report described attacks on doors, gunfire, alarms, and the officers' attempts to defend the prisoner.
Assessment: Detailed press account of mob violence against a jailed defendant, not a court judgment legitimizing the killing. The first account's descriptions of culpability, individual actors, and casualty sequence merit comparison with inquest and trial records. Follow-up open.
