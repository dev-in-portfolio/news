Discovery narrative

Source: Western Sun & General Advertiser, p. 3 — original page.
Report: An anti-counterfeiting notice reproduced a purported $30 Bank of Harmony note signed with names including Frederick Rapp. The article declared that there was no bank or banking association at Harmony and called the instrument fraudulent.
Assessment: Documented contemporary public warning about a purportedly fictitious issuer; no suspect, issuer records, or legal disposition were verified. Follow-up open.
