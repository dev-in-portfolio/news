Recorded finding

1908 claimed arrest of Belle Gunness in Syracuse was corrected: detainee identified as Belle Herron.

Discovery narrative

Source: Richmond Palladium (Daily), p. 1 — original page; LaPorte County Historical Society background.
Report: The front page reported that experts thought the headless woman's remains recovered from the burnt Gunness home were too small to be Belle, while the prosecutor maintained the remains were hers. In the same rush of speculation, police removed a woman from a train; she identified herself as Cora Belle Herron and established that she was not Gunness.
Assessment: This is a valuable example of contradictory expert and official views printed together. The traveler was wrongly suspected, not evidence that Gunness escaped. Neither a sensational escape claim nor an unequivocal identity finding is established by this page; the historical mystery must remain labeled as such.
