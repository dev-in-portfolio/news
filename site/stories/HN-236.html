Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Union and Jasper Republican, p. 2, reprinting the Kansas City Times — original page.
Report: A reported vigilante party near Fort Dodge, Kansas, seized and hanged a young buffalo-bone collector named Calahan alongside a suspected horse thief after the suspect had taken shelter at Calahan's dugout. Rev. Mr. Calahan of Topeka, the young man's father, insisted his son had nothing to do with the theft. The editorial condemned the killings and identified a suspected leader, whose name is unstable in OCR.
Assessment: Newspaper allegation of a possible wrongful lynching, with the innocence claim supported in this account principally by the bereaved father's investigation and statements. Do not treat that claim, the mob's membership, or guilt of either victim as independently adjudicated. Follow-up open.
