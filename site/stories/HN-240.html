Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2, reproducing a Mount Vernon, Illinois correspondent to the St. Louis Republican — original page.
Report: The Mount Vernon National Bank sued Adams Express Company over an $8,000 package bound for the Third National Bank of St. Louis. According to the article, bank officers had wrapped and sealed the money with unusual care before handing it to express agents in June; at the destination the package was opened to reveal paper cut into banknote-sized pieces, with some inner wrapping and twine altered but the outer addressing apparently intact.
Assessment: Reported lawsuit and alleged transit theft, not proof of which employee, stranger, or stage of custody was responsible. The reporter expressly said the culprit was unknown; the court judgment and any recovered money remain unverified. Follow-up open.
