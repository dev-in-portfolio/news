Discovery narrative

Source: Muncie Times, p. 15 — original Jan Resseger 'Witness for Justice' column; GAO review of aid for displaced students.
Report: Resseger criticized the post-Katrina reorganization of public schools and uncertainty created by school-by-school sponsorship, warning that the replacement of a centralized school system risked leaving vulnerable children without stable support.
Assessment: A contemporaneous policy critique, not a neutral measure of charter-school performance. Subsequent aid and enrollment figures need comparison with the article's specific predictions before drawing a causal conclusion.
