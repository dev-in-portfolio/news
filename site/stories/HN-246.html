Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2, reprinting a Chicago Inter Ocean special — original page.
Report: A massive blast destroyed Rosenthal & Co.'s flaxseed mill at Frankfort, Indiana, on March 11. The paper named ten workers killed and described ruins subsequently burning; several workers were absent and a late-arriving timekeeper escaped. The report referred to a boiler explosion, noting reused boiler and engine equipment, but admitted the complete loss of the workers prevented a definitive determination of what happened.
Assessment: Specific workplace catastrophe and provisional mechanism, not an independently verified final engineering inquiry. Casualty names are vulnerable to OCR misreading; check page image and local death records before reusing them. Follow-up open.
