Discovery narrative

Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page.
Report: The paper connected the prolonged United Steelworkers strike and factory changeovers to falling manufacturing wages, citing a reduced weekly take-home figure for a family worker.
Assessment: A current wage index and a national steel labor dispute are related but not proof that every factory worker lost the reported amount because of the strike. The article is useful for tracing the conflict's broader economic reporting, with attribution divided among strike layoffs and seasonal auto-model changes.
