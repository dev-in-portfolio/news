Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; State Department history of the Soviet collapse.
Report: The dispatch described Boris Yeltsin urging resistance, crowds protesting a hard-line committee that had removed Mikhail Gorbachev from power, and miners responding to a strike call.
Assessment: News from an unsettled coup attempt, not an enduring change of government. The coup failed within days; its defeat accelerated the political dissolution of the Soviet Union later that year.
