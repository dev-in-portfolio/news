Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis News, p. 1 — original page; Library of Congress fire-code history; Chicago Public Library history.
Report: The front page reported the December 30 Chicago Iroquois Theatre fire during a performance of Mr. Bluebeard, placing the immediate known dead near 580, with many more still listed missing. It described confusion over how the stage fire started, desperate searches for relatives, and makeshift emergency responses around the morgues.
Assessment: Later histories identify 602 deaths, not 580 plus every person initially missing; the preliminary categories overlap. Chicago and Library of Congress accounts document combustible stage materials, obstructed exits, and the ensuing changes to fire-safety practice.
