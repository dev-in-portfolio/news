Discovery narrative

Source: National Jewish Post, p. 1 — original page; UN General Assembly Resolution 181 (II).
Report: The newspaper described celebrations after the partition tally and reported escalating clashes in Palestine, alongside speculation about leadership of a future Jewish state.
Assessment: The November 29, 1947 UN General Assembly vote approved a partition plan, not the already completed creation of two states. The plan's reception was sharply contested, and violence and displacement followed. The selected Jewish press perspective is important primary evidence but cannot stand alone for all Palestinian and Arab experiences of the period.
