Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Union, p. 2, reprinting the Louisville Courier-Journal — original page.
Report: Two young men at the Louisville Medical College, allegedly rivals in a romance, arranged a pistol duel in a completely darkened room. After a first exchange missed both participants, a second left one slightly wounded in the arm. The paper said the pair reconciled afterward and the incident only later became known.
Assessment: A colorful reprinted incident, not a police or college disciplinary record. Participants are unnamed and independent confirmation is absent. Follow-up open.
