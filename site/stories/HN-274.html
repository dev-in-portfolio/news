Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2 — original page.
Report: Lawyers for California resident William K. Rogers, also identified as William Kissane, sought to quash a New York 1854 forgery indictment involving the Chemical Bank. The retrospective recalled his earlier, separate prosecution relating to the burning of the steamer Martha Washington, reporting that he had been acquitted. It also recycled an extraordinary, unverified allegation that agents had poisoned a private investigator years earlier.
Assessment: The 1887 news is the revived legal proceeding, not a new 1887 arson or poisoning. Previous acquittals cannot be converted into guilt, and the investigator-poisoning story is an allegation of doubtful provenance. Motion's disposition open.
