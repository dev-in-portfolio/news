Discovery narrative

Source: Indiana American, p. 1, reprinting Bennett's Evening Herald — original page.
Report: A second case on the same page described a letter purporting to come from Bank of Kentucky cashier G. C. Gwathmey, instructing New York engravers to print roughly $370,000 in notes using the bank's actual plates. The engravers became suspicious of a purported collector, consulted the bank's New York agent, and learned the order was forged; the article reported arrests and recovery of the impressions.
Assessment: Specific contemporary account of an attempted fraud involving genuine printing plates and forged authorization, with arrests reported but no trial disposition verified. Follow-up open.
