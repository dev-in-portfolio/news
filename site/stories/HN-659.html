Discovery narrative

Source: The Daily Banner, p. 1 — original page; Glamorgan Archives, Aberfan Tribunal.
Report: The headline “107 Known Dead in Welsh School Disasterâ€ reported a coal-waste landslide that struck Aberfan, including its school, the day before.
Assessment: Verified catastrophe; early count revised. The eventual recognized toll was 144 people, including 116 children. The public inquiry attributed responsibility to the National Coal Board and failures involving the unstable waste tip. The first-day figure reflected an incomplete rescue and recovery operation, not the final count.
