Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Gazette, p. 17, lengthy Republican partisan opinion — original page.
Report: A partisan writer accused a rival printer of preparing Republican-labeled election tickets with a concealed candidate substitution and distributing them in Jasper County, Indiana, to mislead voters. The article named local political opponents and made sweeping personal accusations.
Assessment: Documented published allegation of ballot deception, not a verified finding of forgery or proof against the named printer. The disputed tickets, election results, and any proceedings were not independently examined. Follow-up open.
