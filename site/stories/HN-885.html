Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 2 — original commentary; GAO later preparedness-review overview.
Report: A writer denounced what was presented as days of delay by federal agencies in sending troops and supplies to New Orleans, comparing the response with national urgency after September 11. The page suggested conditions improved swiftly after military arrivals.
Assessment: A forceful contemporary account and political judgment, not a verified hour-by-hour response log. Later government reviews documented multiple failures and strengths in preparedness and response; the article's timing and 'order restored' statements need operational corroboration.
