Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2 — original page.
Report: Armed robbers stopped a Lake Shore express near Kendallville, Indiana, shot and injured the engineer, blasted an express car and its safe, and escaped. The article cites sharply conflicting witness accounts: one rider guessed 20–25 robbers, whereas the express superintendent said no more than six. Rumored losses of $300,000 were disputed by the company's estimate of no more than $20,000. Passengers were not themselves robbed in the account.
Assessment: The robbery is reported; gang size and haul are explicitly contested. No identities or convictions established. Follow-up open.
