Discovery narrative

Source: Daily Evansville Journal, p. 2, local eyewitness-style coverage — original page.
Report: The paper described an explosion at a store at Eighth and Chestnut that shattered windows, walls, and merchandise. It attributed ignition to the proprietor allegedly weighing gunpowder while holding a lit pipe, and reported severe burns to him and a customer, with others injured.
Assessment: Local disaster report with a specific contemporary causal claim, not a completed official technical investigation or a confirmed final medical outcome. Follow-up open.
