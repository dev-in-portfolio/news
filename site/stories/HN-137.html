Discovery narrative

Source: Semi-Weekly Journal (Indianapolis), p. 1, reprinting St. Louis reports; separately reprinted by the Vincennes Gazette, August 7 — original page; second printing.
Report: After a robbery at the Jacksonville, Illinois, branch bank, the reports said teller H. D. Town confessed to Col. Mather that he had taken funds over several years, destroyed bank books to conceal the losses, and hidden stolen cash and gold in three locations. Town reportedly asserted a deficit under $4,000, while a crowd pursued Mather after he recovered the money; Town was committed and sought reduced bail.
Assessment: Detailed report of a purported confession, recovery, and preliminary court proceedings, carried in multiple newspapers; the second printing repeats rather than independently confirms the underlying St. Louis account. Follow-up open.
