Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Journal, p. 2 — original page; Iowa mine-inspection history; 1902 mine report.
Report: The newspaper said 50 coal miners had died near Oskaloosa, Iowa, in the Lost Creek explosion of January 24, and described a misfired shot, flammable gas and coal dust, shattered cages, and desperate rescue attempts.
Assessment: Incorrect preliminary figure. Iowa mine-inspection history identifies 20 killed and 14 injured. The contemporary proposed sequence of ignition should be checked against the full inspector's account; 50 is not the historically documented number of deaths.
