Recorded finding

Indira Gandhi assassination 1984 first dispatches differ on number/status of bodyguard attackers.

Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 3 — original page.
Report: The first dispatch said Prime Minister Indira Gandhi had been killed outside her home by reportedly three Sikh security personnel, and that Sikh extremists later claimed responsibility.
Assessment: First-day suspect-count reporting must be distinguished from established perpetrator identities. Subsequent accounts identified two Sikh bodyguards as the shooters; a reported claim of responsibility is not itself independent proof of an organization directing the attack.
