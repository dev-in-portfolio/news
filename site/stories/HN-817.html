Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 4 — original page; CDC analysis of Chicago's mortality.
Report: The paper reported that Center Township Trustee Julia Carson's office was distributing free fans and helping supply window air conditioners to low-income, elderly, and ill residents, explicitly prompted by Chicago's killer heat wave.
Assessment: Local preventive assistance, not an Indianapolis heat-death tally. CDC later distinguished 485 certified heat-related deaths from 739 excess deaths in Chicago's July 1995 event; the two measures describe different mortality concepts.
