Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2, Spokane Falls dispatch — original page.
Report: Fire swept Spokane Falls, Washington Territory, burning much of the business district to the river. Firefighters reportedly lacked adequate water and ordered a dozen buildings demolished with explosives to form firebreaks, without stopping the fire. The same printed report gave incompatible starting times and losses ranging from $11 million to conjectures of $30 million, then called the highest estimate exaggerated.
Assessment: Contemporary disaster report with explicitly unstable times, death reports, and property-loss estimates. The rebuilding and final damage audit need independent review. Follow-up open.
