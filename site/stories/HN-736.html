Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; NASA mission history.
Report: The launch-day story chronicled Sally Ride and four male astronauts lifting off on Challenger, a visible milestone followed by crowds wearing celebratory shirts and buttons.
Assessment: The first U.S. woman astronaut, not the first woman from any nation to reach space. NASA places Ride on STS-7, June 18; Soviet cosmonaut Valentina Tereshkova flew in 1963. The first's exact scope matters.
