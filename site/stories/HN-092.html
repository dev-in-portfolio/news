Discovery narrative

Source: Western Register and Terre-Haute Advertiser, p. 2 — original page.
Report: The newspaper said an Indigenous man was killed in his camp near the headwaters of Sugar Creek. It referred to the rescue or escape of a person arrested in connection with another killing and urged that the perpetrators be brought before the law.
Assessment: A local report with severely interleaved and damaged OCR. Whether the two crimes were connected, and who was responsible, cannot be inferred. Follow-up open.
