Discovery narrative

Source: Daily Tribune (Terre Haute), p. 1 — original page; U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum historical synthesis.
Report: A dispatch citing British newspaper correspondents and American missionaries described Ottoman deportations of Armenians, forced journeys toward arid regions, robbery, killings, deaths through hunger and thirst, and separation of families. The report recorded contemporaneous accusations of systematic destruction while specific refugee and casualty reports were difficult to verify in wartime.
Assessment: The Holocaust Memorial Museum confirms the Armenian genocide in 1915–1916, with estimates ranging from at least 664,000 to as many as 1.2 million deaths across the period. This independent historical finding establishes the overall systematic persecution; it does not automatically authenticate every individually narrated atrocity or exact 1915 newspaper figure. The event must not be mischaracterized as merely an unverified wartime rumor.
