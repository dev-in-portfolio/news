Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page; Wyoming State Mine Inspector's report, reproduced by local historians; Utah Archives inventory of the cross-state investigation.
Report: A June 30 explosion at Union Pacific's Hanna No. 1 mine, Wyoming, was initially feared to have killed 234 miners. The same dispatch describes 46 people brought out alive and repeats workers' speculative explanation involving opening an air chamber.
Assessment: Historical inspector material records 169 deaths. Inquest language attributed ignition to combustible gas but did not securely identify the triggering act; the newspaper's air-chamber hypothesis remains a theory.
