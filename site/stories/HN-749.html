Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; Coca-Cola company history.
Report: A wire article previewed Coca-Cola's announcement that it was changing its long-secret recipe, even as Pepsi portrayed the decision as a triumph for its own brand.
Assessment: An accurate product-change announcement but not evidence that consumers would accept the new formula. The company returned the original formula as Coca-Cola Classic on July 11, following backlash. This is a documented business decision and reversal, not an unsolved industrial conspiracy.
