Discovery narrative

Source: Evansville Daily Journal, p. 2, reprinting the Buffalo Commercial — original page.
Report: A dockside commission merchant and two nearby businesses were reportedly robbed of approximately $2,500, $190, and $390. In an unusual detail, the article claimed the safes or offices had been left apparently locked, delaying detection until the owners tried to retrieve the missing money.
Assessment: Reprinted local crime report, with its descriptions of entry and amounts not independently verified; no suspect or conviction identified. Follow-up open.
