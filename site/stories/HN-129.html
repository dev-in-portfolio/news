Discovery narrative

Source: Indiana American, p. 2, reprinting the Toledo Gazette — original page.
Report: The story said Elizabeth Swartwout, who had traveled with the Jenkins family from Chicago, fell ill and died during a stay at the Mansion House in Michigan City, Indiana. Suspicious relatives allegedly secured an exhumation; physicians reportedly found arsenic in her stomach, and a coroner's jury returned a verdict of willful murder. The report connected the inquiry to Mr. and Mrs. Jenkins but left the allegations and subsequent proceedings unresolved.
Assessment: Secondhand report of an inquest and forensic allegation; an inquest verdict is not a criminal conviction. Follow-up open.
