Discovery narrative

Source: Crawfordsville Weekly Journal, p. 1, reprinting a New York newspaper account — original page.
Report: Professor Lowe's former war balloon United States ascended near Central Park with Lowe, a passenger, and daredevil Harry Leslie aboard. The article describes Leslie descending on a rope and performing acrobatics in midair before the balloon eventually landed near Yonkers.
Assessment: Detailed contemporary spectacle report, without separate event records or accident log; a vivid later chapter in Lowe's aviation career, following HN-188 and HN-191. Follow-up open.
