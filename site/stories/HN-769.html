Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; U.S. Navy history of Iran Air Flight 655.
Report: Two days after USS Vincennes shot down an Iranian civilian airliner, the paper linked public scrutiny of the ship's Aegis system to a separate Pentagon defense-contract bribery investigation.
Assessment: Two news subjects appeared beside one another; the article does not establish that procurement bribery caused the shootdown. Navy history records 290 people killed aboard Iran Air Flight 655. The decision to fire and the unrelated procurement allegations require distinct evidence.
