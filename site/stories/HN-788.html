Report: On the day of reunification, the paper described President George H. W. Bush's support for a unified Germany alongside concern among other U.S. political figures about the country's historical legacy and future place in Europe.
Assessment: Formal reunification occurred on October 3, 1990, not when the Wall first opened in 1989. The article's dissenting views are contemporary political commentary, not evidence that the new Germany lacked legal recognition.
