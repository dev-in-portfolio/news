Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 3 — original page; National Archives, Roswell and Air Force reports.
Report: In a roundup of supposed flying-disc recoveries and sightings, the paper carried the Roswell military-base retraction, describing a foil-covered object associated with a weather-observation balloon.
Assessment: That account followed a brief, widely distributed military announcement suggesting a flying disc had been recovered. Later Air Force research identified Project Mogul balloon-related material as its explanation; this is the documented official conclusion, not blanket proof about unrelated sightings or all subsequent witness narratives.
