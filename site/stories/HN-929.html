Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 4 — original student post-election discussion; NOAA Sandy assessment.
Report: A post-election school article notes Obama's victory and reports speculation that Hurricane Sandy influenced public perceptions during the campaign's final days.
Assessment: A proposed electoral explanation, not a causal finding. NOAA documents Sandy's destructive physical impacts and 147 direct fatalities in the storm's broader affected region; storm consequences and voter behavior must not be conflated.
