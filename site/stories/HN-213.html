Discovery narrative

Source: Crawfordsville Weekly Journal, p. 7 — original page; Library of Congress historical context.
Report: An article described the court examination of William H. Mumler, who produced photographs allegedly showing deceased people beside living sitters. It reported his acquittal, recited accounts from fascinated witnesses, and printed Mumler's insistence that no trick was involved.
Assessment: A genuine fraud proceeding, not confirmation that spirits appeared in photographs. The Library of Congress likewise notes that Mumler was charged but not convicted; the newspaper's speculation about inexplicable photographs is the author's opinion. Legal result contextualized.
