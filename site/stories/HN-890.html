Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 1 — original front page; CDC's contemporary influenza update.
Report: A local feature asked whether Indianapolis could respond if H5N1 avian influenza began spreading widely among people, with many citizens doubting institutional preparedness after Katrina.
Assessment: A plausible preparedness question, not evidence that an H5N1 human pandemic was occurring in Indiana. The CDC reported no identified human H5N1 cases in the United States in its late-2005 assessment and no demonstrated sustained person-to-person transmission.
