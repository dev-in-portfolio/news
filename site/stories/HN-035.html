Discovery narrative

Source: The Kentucky Gazette (Lexington), p. 2 — original page.
Report: A reprinted account alleged that a crowd of children attacked and killed two small children identified as aristocrats during revolutionary violence in Paris, and followed this with other horrifying claims about the September massacres.
Assessment: Sensational, politically charged secondhand atrocity narrative; uncorroborated at the level of these specific details. The historical existence of revolutionary massacres does not independently establish this story. Follow-up open.
