Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page.
Report: The paper reported the Supreme Court had sentenced former Chattanooga sheriff Joseph F. Shipp to 90 days for contempt, along with jailer Jeremiah Gibson and four others receiving terms of 60 or 90 days. The proceedings stemmed from the 1906 lynching of Ed Johnson, despite a stay issued by the Supreme Court.
Assessment: This is a specific court disposition, not merely speculation about participants. It is crucial not to confuse the newspaper's 1909 sentencing date with the 1906 lynching date. The high court's contempt finding does not make Johnson's lynching lawful or prove the underlying accusation against him.
