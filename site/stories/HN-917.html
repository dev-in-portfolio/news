Recorded finding

Haiti quake on 12 January 2010, not 2009 as flagged in tracker.

Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 2 — original student report; USGS earthquake origin record.
Report: The student paper reports Haiti's devastating magnitude-7.0 earthquake, relief needs, aftershocks and an interim estimate of 200,000 deaths. In its main paragraph it accidentally dates the event January 12, 2009, even though the publication was in February 2010.
Assessment: A verifiable newspaper date error. USGS records the earthquake on January 12, 2010. The paper's 200,000 figure was an interim, attributed estimate rather than an individually verified death register.
