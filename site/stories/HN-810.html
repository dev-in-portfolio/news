Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 17 — original page; U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum genocide chronology.
Report: Paul Gillingham reported from Benaco camp in Tanzania, saying it held more than 330,000 Rwandan refugees in about ten square kilometers, with arrivals continuing and the UNHCR planning to divide the camp into smaller units.
Assessment: An observed refugee emergency at a particular time and location—not a national census or the death toll of the genocide. The museum describes the April–July killing of hundreds of thousands, mainly Tutsi people; refugee movement and perpetration of atrocities are separate questions.
