Discovery narrative

Source: Western Sun & General Advertiser, p. 2, an extended first-person public defense — original page.
Report: A long signed statement concerning John D. Woolverton, D. C. Johnson, and disputed Cottrell papers said the speaker had been removed from office amid suspicion of writing forged materials. He denied involvement, contested handwriting identifications, and publicly accused others of manufacturing a case against him.
Assessment: One side of a public political and personal dispute, not an impartial court finding. The author and opponents' claims need independent records and the opposite side's full reply. Follow-up open.
