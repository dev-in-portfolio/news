Discovery narrative

Source: People's Pilot, p. 2 — original page; Colorado mine inspector's contemporary report.
Report: A February 18 explosion at the Vulcan coal mine near Newcastle, Colorado, was said to have trapped and killed 55 miners. The blast wrecked ventilation equipment and buried parts of the passageway; a miner thrown near the entrance was gravely injured and died. Rescue workers tried alternate shafts and makeshift ventilation while the newspaper speculated that a gas pocket associated with underground fires was responsible.
Assessment: The casualty figure and suggested mechanism are the paper's initial account; the cited state inspector's report is a route to the official investigation, not evidence that the newspaper's theory was confirmed. Follow-up open.
