Discovery narrative

Source: People's Pilot, p. 2, Wausau, Wisconsin telegram — original page.
Report: Neighbors searching near Edgar, Wisconsin, reportedly found the body of missing farmer William Habeck in a shallow, disguised grave under brush and a fallen tree. The report attributed an ax killing to his 19-year-old son Herman, who was said to have confessed, with 17-year-old Otto allegedly helping conceal the body. Both were arrested.
Assessment: Report of recovery, an attributed confession, and arrests, not confirmation of a court-tested confession or final verdict. Follow-up open.
