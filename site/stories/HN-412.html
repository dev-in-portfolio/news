Discovery narrative

Source: Evening Republican, p. 2 — original page; CDC global 1918 pandemic background.
Report: Travelers reaching Laredo, Texas, described overwhelmed undertakers in Mexico and alleged 40,000 influenza cases in Mexico City by mid-October, with pneumonia and deaths among poor households. The headline called it the most fatal epidemic in Mexico's history.
Assessment: The pandemic and severe social disruption are credible in context. This Indiana paper reprinted secondhand traveler testimony, not a Mexican national mortality register. Its 40,000 figure counts alleged cases, not deaths, and the sweeping claim that this was the country's worst epidemic remains unverified by this source.
