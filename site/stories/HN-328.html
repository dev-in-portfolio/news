Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Journal, p. 2 — original page.
Report: Mary Belle Witwer, arrested at Dayton, Ohio, on suspicion of poisoning her sister, had become the subject of claims about 12 earlier deaths involving relatives and families where she had worked. The unusually cautious short report acknowledged that she denied wrongdoing and said no incriminating evidence had yet been found.
Assessment: A classic dangerous pattern of serial-killer insinuation without proof. The alleged additional deaths are not established homicides and Witwer is not presented as convicted. Follow-up open.
