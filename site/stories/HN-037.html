Discovery narrative

Source: The American Herald of Liberty, p. 1 — original page.
Report: A report attributed to commissioners at Rochefort asserted that papers on a vessel called L'Apollon showed an English-backed effort to deliver Rochefort, Brest, Lorient, Saint-Malo, Bordeaux, and other ports to enemy control. An engineer aboard was said to have killed himself when detected; the report also spoke of executions.
Assessment: Claim attributed to a belligerent revolutionary government, printed amid severe repression. The claimed papers, individual accused, and executions have not been independently verified. Follow-up open.
