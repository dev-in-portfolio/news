Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Semi-Weekly Republican, p. 2 — original page; USGS historical earthquake magnitude and death register.
Report: The paper claimed ValparaÃ­so, Chile, and many settlements along the South American Pacific coast had suffered extreme damage from the August 16 earthquake, subsequent fires, and what it described as a huge harbor wave. Telegraph failures left enormous gaps in early information; some stories alleged damage from one end of Chile to the other.
Assessment: USGS historical data lists this earthquake at approximately magnitude 8.2 and 3,882 deaths. The paper's regional rumors and sweeping claims of a devastating wave at every location should not be generalized as verified measurements. More specific Chilean surveys and ship records remain necessary.
