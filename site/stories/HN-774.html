Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; Scottish prosecutors' confirmed total.
Report: After the aircraft fell onto Lockerbie, a first-day dispatch described a probable midair breakup, 258 people aboard believed dead, destroyed homes, and an undetermined number of people killed on the ground.
Assessment: Early passenger and ground-death counts were incomplete. The subsequent confirmed figure was 259 aboard plus 11 in Lockerbie, 270 total. This first account did not yet establish the cause or perpetrator of the crash.
