Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page; U.S. Bureau of Mines historical fatality table; contemporary coroner evidence collected locally.
Report: After an April 3 explosion at Joseph Leiter's Zeigler mine in Illinois, the paper said around 50 men had been trapped, feared 40 or more dead, and counted 37 bodies recovered at one point. It confidently suggested gas accumulating during a Sunday shutdown had caused the disaster, although no completed inquiry was quoted.
Assessment: The federal historical mining table records 49 deaths at Leiter/Zeigler. The paper's Sunday-gas accumulation theory was preliminary, not a proved reconstruction; later inquest accounts must be consulted for the full mechanism.
