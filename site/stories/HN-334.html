Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page.
Report: A gang estimated at 12–15 people reportedly stopped a Chicago, Rock Island and Pacific express west of Davenport, Iowa, using a red lantern to halt the locomotive. The passenger coaches were detached and the train's engine used to move the express car before the robbers blew it open with dynamite, looted safes, and disappeared.
Assessment: Estimates of the gang's size and stolen valuables were made before a verified investigative outcome. Follow-up open.
