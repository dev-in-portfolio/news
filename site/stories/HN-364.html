Discovery narrative

Source: Nappanee Advance-News, p. 3 — original page; Library of Congress, NAACP exhibition; National Park Service study.
Report: The newspaper counted seven violent deaths after white mobs attacked Black residents, burned homes, and lynched people in Springfield, Illinois. Its coverage included troop movements and attacks on Black businesses but framed the upheaval around an alleged rape and also carried demeaning racial descriptions.
Assessment: The attacks and Black victims are confirmed. The Library of Congress records that the accusation against George Richardson was false. The original paper's allegation and prejudicial language are evidence of the news climate, not evidence of a Black suspect's guilt. The riot helped inspire the formation of the NAACP.
