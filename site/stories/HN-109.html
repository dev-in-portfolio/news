Discovery narrative

Source: Corydon Press and Anti-Masonic Democrat, p. 5, summarizing Boston papers and the New York Post — original page.
Report: Sailors from the distressed New York brig Orbit initially said Captain Samuel Woodbury had died of fever on a voyage from the West African coast. A crew member who reached the rescuing brig Mentos reportedly alleged instead that Woodbury had been murdered by an acting master, a story the paper said other crew members corroborated.
Assessment: Competing maritime accounts and reported sailor statements, with transcribed names and the precise vessel spelling needing visual checks. Not an adjudication of murder. Follow-up open.
