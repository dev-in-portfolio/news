Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 13 — original student interviews; GAO post-rollover assessment.
Report: A student-written feature described people buying bottled water or withdrawing money, alongside young interviewees who saw little reason to panic over the computer date rollover.
Assessment: Reported personal expectations, not proof that a catastrophic Y2K failure would occur. GAO later documented limited actual rollover disruption, following a large international prevention effort; the outcome neither establishes every earlier warning as reasonable nor shows that preparation was unnecessary.
