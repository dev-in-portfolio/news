Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 1 — original student front page; UN-linked human-rights year review.
Report: A feature reports the January 7 attack on the French satirical weekly Charlie Hebdo, names the Kouachi brothers, and considers how violent extremism and offense taken at cartoons intersected with freedom of expression.
Assessment: Real attack, but explanatory discussions of the attackers' beliefs are not proof that an entire religion endorsed violence. The newsroom assault killed twelve; related January Paris attacks bring the wider sequence's total to seventeen, a different casualty definition.
