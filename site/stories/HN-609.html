Report: “Plan Own Bus Service as Boycott Continuesâ€ outlined an idea for Black-owned bus or taxi relays after authorities resisted changing segregated bus operations and hiring practices.
Assessment: This article documents an organizing proposal, not proof that an independent bus system had already been licensed or started. Carpools, walking, funding, and legal challenge sustained the 381-day boycott through 1956. The proposal is unusually revealing evidence of the logistical work behind the movement, and deserves follow-up in the next decade.
