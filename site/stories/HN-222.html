Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis News, p. 1, reporting the arrival of the U.S. steamer Frolic and an interview with Captain George E. Tyson — original page; Smithsonian Institution's Polaris expedition record description.
Report: Survivors of the U.S. Arctic expedition aboard the Polaris arrived at the Washington Navy Yard. The paper said Secretary of the Navy George Robeson spoke privately with Tyson about the expedition, Captain Charles Francis Hall's death, and the condition of the ship. Tyson attributed Hall's death to apoplexy, saw no serious mutiny before his party separated, and expected the Polaris might return. The Secretary reserved any suspected mutiny question for a formal inquiry.
Assessment: A preliminary survivor interview, not the final explanation of Hall's death, the ship's fate, or crew relations. This early natural-death account should be read alongside the later allegations in HN-224. Outcome not settled by this report.
