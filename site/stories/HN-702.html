Report: “Khmer Rouge Controls Most of Cambodiaâ€ described a guerrilla movement approaching victory and asserted that it controlled roughly 80 percent of the land and 40 percent of the population, while openly saying outsiders knew relatively little about its inner workings.
Assessment: Provisional territorial estimates and a stark contemporary knowledge gap. The Khmer Rouge captured Phnom Penh April 17 and instituted mass forced displacement, persecution, and killing. This March article did not yet document the full atrocities; later established crimes should be attributed to retrospective evidence, not presented as information already verified by the reporter.
