Report: The newspaper described the recent death of Hannah Stout, daughter of postmaster John Stout near Martinsville, Indiana, after years of progressive rigidity that allegedly fixed her joints and spine and turned some soft tissues into bone-like material. It rejected rumors that her body had become extraordinarily heavy and reported that her family guarded the grave against unwanted medical curiosity.
