Recorded finding

Ludlow 1914 miners and militia blamed one another for deaths and tent-colony destruction.

Discovery narrative

Source: Marion Labor Sentinel, p. 1 — original page; National Park Service historical account.
Report: In strongly partisan, sometimes damaged OCR, a labor newspaper described Colorado National Guard troops and mine guards clashing with striking coal miners near Ludlow, tents burning, and women and children being caught in the violence. The paper printed demands for federal intervention and charged that miners' families were being deliberately slaughtered.
Assessment: The April 20 Ludlow Massacre is corroborated by the National Park Service, which records 21 killed, including 11 children. The paper offers a contemporaneous labor movement perspective. Particular battlefield decisions, intentionality of each death, and quoted accusations against named people require more than one partisan report; the historical deaths and strike context are not in doubt.
