Discovery narrative

Source: The National Intelligencer and Washington Advertiser, p. 3 — original page.
Report: A proclamation dated November 29, 1803, bearing the names Dessalines, Christophe, and Clervaux, acknowledged that some inhabitants had been killed amid the upheavals in Saint-Domingue. It expressed regret and maintained that the violence was contrary to the leaders' wishes and difficult to prevent during war.
Assessment: Primary-period publication of leaders' political defense, not independent proof of their command responsibility or lack of it. Follow-up open.
