Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; USHMM, Munich Agreement.
Report: With the Munich pact signed, Secretary of State Cordell Hull expressed broad public relief at a seeming end to an imminent European war crisis; the front page also described territorial threats around Czechoslovakia.
Assessment: The bargain ceded the Sudetenland to Nazi Germany without Czechoslovakia's participation and did not secure lasting European peace. The regime seized the Czech lands in March 1939 and invaded Poland in September. The newspaper captured a hopeful contemporary reaction, not a demonstrated long-term settlement.
