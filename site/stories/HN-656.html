Discovery narrative

Source: The Daily Banner, p. 1 — original page; NASA, Gemini VIII.
Report: “Gemini 8 Pilots Back at Florida Capeâ€ reported the return of Neil Armstrong and David Scott after their space mission ended abruptly; a March 21 issue also described the destroyer crew that recovered them in the Pacific.
Assessment: Corroborated but technically incomplete at first reading. The mission made the first docking of two spacecraft on March 16. A stuck maneuvering thruster then caused a violent roll, prompting undocking, use of reentry controls, and an emergency landing. A story about the crew's safe return alone conceals how close the technical failure came to disaster.
