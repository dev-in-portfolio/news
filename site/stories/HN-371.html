Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page; Washington State HistoryLink reconstruction.
Report: Two trains held by snow near Wellington, Washington, were swept from the rails into a canyon in the March 1 avalanche. The article initially stated 50 killed, with other passengers missing, described shattered sleeping cars, and worried that recovery might be impossible until warmer weather.
Assessment: Washington's historical record documents 96 deaths, nearly twice the first newspaper's figure. The incident took place in Washington State, despite another contemporary paper mislocating Wellington in Idaho. The first count mixed recovered bodies, missing persons, and estimates.
