Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2, international war news roundup — original page.
Report: Amid dispatches about the 1882 British-Egyptian conflict, one terse item alleged that three Arab sailors accused of mutiny aboard the Egyptian frigate Souda at Alexandria had been keelhauled and died soon afterward. The assertion appeared alongside many other fast-changing military dispatches without named witnesses or an inquiry.
Assessment: Uncorroborated contemporary allegation of a severe naval punishment, not an established finding that the ship's officers carried it out or that every detail is accurate. The vessel's log and any diplomatic or naval inquiry remain unchecked. Follow-up open.
