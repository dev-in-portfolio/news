Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis News, p. 6 — original page; Indiana historical study.
Report: A collection of reprinted editorials discusses a mob that seized five men jailed for alleged burglary at Versailles, Ripley County, Indiana, and killed them on September 14. Some editors insisted there could be no justification for killing prisoners without trial; others used the crime to advance defensive or racist arguments about Southern lynchings. A contemporaneous editorial described the men as accused of relatively minor property crimes and emphasized the absence of convictions.
Assessment: Five extrajudicial killings, not court-imposed punishment. The page comprises divergent editorial reactions rather than neutral proof of the original allegations against the men. Follow-up open on prosecutions of the mob.
