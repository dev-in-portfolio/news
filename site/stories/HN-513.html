Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; National Archives, War of the Worlds radio reaction.
Report: A local report said a radio drama portraying an invasion from Mars had unsettled some Indianapolis residents; the account described inquiries to police, radio stations, and ordinary bystanders about whether the supposed attack was real.
Assessment: Orson Welles's October 30 broadcast was fiction, and some fearful responses were genuine. But national mass panic or mass injury cannot be inferred from selected colorful anecdotes. The National Archives examines contrasting listener reactions; a later legend of universal panic overstates what the evidence proves.
