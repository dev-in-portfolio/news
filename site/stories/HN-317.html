Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Semi-Weekly Republican, p. 2 — original page.
Report: Authorities at the Hutchinson, Kansas, reformatory reportedly said Seth Rosebrook and Chad Stowell had admitted involvement in a September 1898 Missouri Pacific express-train hold-up near Leeds, Missouri. The paper noted that Jesse James Jr. was already under indictment for that same robbery, making the purported new confessions especially consequential.
Assessment: The newspaper attributes the claims to police and a deputy sheriff; an alleged confession is not independently verified guilt, and James's indictment is not proof that he committed the robbery. Follow-up open.
