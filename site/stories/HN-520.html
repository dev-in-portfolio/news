Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; Finnish National Archives, Winter War collections.
Report: A Helsinki-datelined correspondent described the Soviet invasion by land, sea, and air and bombings around the Finnish capital and other towns at the onset of the Winter War.
Assessment: The USSR began its invasion on November 30, 1939; the conflict ended in March 1940. Wartime estimates of bombing casualties and combat successes could not yet be independently verified on the day of the attack.
