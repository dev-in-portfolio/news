Discovery narrative

Source: Plymouth Weekly Democrat, p. 1, reprinting a Buffalo Courier correspondent — original page.
Report: At a well on Buchanan Farm, near Tidioute, Pennsylvania, a sudden surge of oil or gas ignited and engulfed nearby drilling works and spectators. The paper named several reported dead and badly injured people, described roughly three dozen wounded, and printed a grim account of one victim who died after trying to make a will.
Assessment: Graphic initial petroleum-industry disaster account, with an uncertain final toll and no independent determination of ignition mechanics. Follow-up open.
