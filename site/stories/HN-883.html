Discovery narrative

Source: Jewish Post, p. 19 — original first-person family account; British government's official account.
Report: Rabbi Shea Hecht wrote of trouble reaching family by telephone after the July 7 bombings and retold his brother-in-law Rabbi Aryeh Sufrin's experience on a London Underground train affected by the attacks.
Assessment: A secondhand family-survivor narrative, not a list of all four attackers, trains, or victims. British official records establish 52 victims killed, excluding the bombers; the religious interpretation of survival is the writer's own.
