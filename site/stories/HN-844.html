Recorded finding

Columbine 1999 13 murdered people (12 students and one teacher), plus two perpetrators; 15 killed INCLUDING perpetrators.

Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 3 — original student reporting.
Report: Student reporter Cassie Borges recounted the April 20 Columbine High School attack, naming student gunmen Eric Harris and Dylan Klebold, reporting 12 students and one teacher killed, and describing the fear the attack sparked among students elsewhere.
Assessment: The victim count is distinct from the deaths of the two perpetrators. The page also speculated about particular categories of intended victims and the perpetrators' thoughts; those interpretations are not established solely by a student writer's reconstruction.
