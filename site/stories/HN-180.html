Recorded finding

1857 powder-magazine catastrophe indexed with inconsistent 'Rondpore'/Persia geographic descriptions and >1,000 dead.

Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper Banner, p. 15, relaying a translated Persian letter printed in a South Asian paper — original page.
Report: Under a sensational headline claiming one thousand lives lost, the article described lightning striking a ruler's ammunition magazine at a place rendered by OCR as Loudpore, devastating a fort, nearby houses, and people inside them. The source itself was a chain of transmission from a letter translated and reprinted abroad.
Assessment: Unverified, highly amplified foreign disaster report. The headline's casualty claim is not treated as an established death count, and both place-name and event require original-language checks. Follow-up open.
