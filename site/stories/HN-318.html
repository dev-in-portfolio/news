Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Semi-Weekly Republican, p. 2 — original page.
Report: The Metropolitan National Bank in Boston lost $10,000 to a man who allegedly walked out with the money unobserved. The article said George Shea, also called Philip Lambele, fled toward New York but was arrested after descriptions were telegraphed to police; all the money was recovered. It further alleged he offered the arresting officer the stolen cash to release him.
Assessment: Arrest and recovery as reported, but the colorful bribery allegation and eventual sentence are not verified by court records. Follow-up open.
