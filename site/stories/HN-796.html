Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; U.S. Justice Department history.
Report: After state-court acquittals of officers accused in the videotaped beating of Rodney King, the paper reported violence, fires, looting, at least nine people dead, 138 injured, and deployment preparations for 2,000 National Guardsmen.
Assessment: A first-night snapshot, not the complete riot toll. The convictions and acquittals had specific legal scopes; two officers were subsequently convicted in a separate 1993 federal civil-rights trial, a result distinct from the 1992 verdicts.
