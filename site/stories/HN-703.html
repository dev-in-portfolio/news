Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 4 — original page; U.S. State Department, end of the Vietnam War.
Report: A New York Times news-service analysis printed by the Indiana paper described refugees moving from Hue toward Da Nang and considered whether the offensive might soon threaten Saigon.
Assessment: Contemporary analysis and reported displacement, not a prediction already proved when printed. North Vietnamese forces ultimately took Saigon on April 30. The subsequent outcome does not turn every March judgment about timing or the South Vietnamese government into a demonstrated fact.
