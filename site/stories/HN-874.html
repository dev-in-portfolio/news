Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 18 — original AP report; WHO history of SARS.
Report: From a medical and policy meeting in Beijing, the newspaper described cooperation against SARS and AIDS and quoted former President Bill Clinton on infections crossing borders quickly.
Assessment: Reported international health diplomacy, not proof that every policy discussed was implemented. WHO's historical summary records approximately 8,096 SARS cases and 774 deaths in the 2002–2003 outbreak; the pandemic-risk analogy and the measured epidemic are separate claims.
