Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 2 — original page.
Report: A dispatch said Gunnar Kaasen's team, led by Balto, brought 300,000 units of diphtheria antitoxin to Nome, Alaska, despite dangerous winter conditions. It celebrated Balto as leader of the 'race of mercy.'
Assessment: The emergency 1925 serum relay happened and helped address a diphtheria threat. Crucially, multiple mushers and dog teams carried the antitoxin in successive stages; Balto led the last team but did not make the entire journey alone. The first victory narrative is incomplete without the relay's other participants.
