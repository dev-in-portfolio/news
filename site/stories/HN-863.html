Source: Muncie Times, p. 28 — original page.
Report: Former network reporter and Ball State professor Phil Bremen, drawing on experience of the Iran–Iraq War, cautioned that Iraqi civilians might resist a U.S. effort to overthrow Saddam Hussein and that war could carry serious human and political costs.
Assessment: An expert's contemporary forecast, not proof of what Iraqi populations would eventually do. The United States had not yet invaded Iraq; later events can be compared with this prediction without portraying it as foreknowledge.
