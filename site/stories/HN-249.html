Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2, citing a Cincinnati dispatch to the Chicago Tribune — original page.
Report: At a wedding for Joel Hembric and Jane Dale in Roane County, Tennessee, guests became gravely ill after eating. The newspaper reported six dead, many others unconscious or endangered, and attributed the deaths to arsenic reportedly kept in the kitchen to kill crows and mistakenly used to season chicken. Several names and the groom's surname are especially prone to OCR variation.
Assessment: Grave alleged mass-poisoning accident with a specific initial cause theory, not a verified toxicology report or a final casualty total. Do not presume sabotage—or accept the accidental-mistake explanation as officially determined—without later inquiry records. Follow-up open.
