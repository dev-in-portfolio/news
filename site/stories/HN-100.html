Discovery narrative

Source: Western Sun & General Advertiser (Vincennes), p. 2 — original page.
Report: The paper printed a December 13, 1825 letter attributed to Secretary of War James Barbour, saying that complaints about a contractor named Bruce's work removing obstructions from western rivers had been supported by an officer's report. It said Major Babcock was suspended from supervising the work pending further documents, and another officer substituted.
Assessment: Published text of an attributed official letter, concerning contract performance and administrative suspension; this does not establish criminal corruption. Letter authenticity and subsequent findings have not been independently checked. Follow-up open.
