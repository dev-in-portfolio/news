Recorded finding

Loma Prieta quake: Los Angeles Times 18 Oct 1989 ran headline 273 killed; Washington Post 26 Oct reconstructed Lieutenant Governor's 271 estimate as inflated.

Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 3 — original page; USGS retrospective.
Report: The first full day of reporting listed at least 271 killed, an estimated $1 billion in damage, and a quake measured 6.9 at 5:04 p.m. on October 17.
Assessment: A major preliminary overcount of deaths. The USGS now reports 63 fatalities, 3,757 injuries, and approximately $6 billion in property damage. The modern M 6.9 magnitude agrees with one early measurement, unlike the mortality and damage estimates.
