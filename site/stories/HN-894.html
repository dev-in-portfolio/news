Recorded finding

Sago mine January 2006: announcement that 12 miners were alive was false; papers corrected to 12 dead/one survivor. LA Times printed survivor's name 'Ronald McCloy'; Washington Post gives Randal McCloy.

Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 2 — original student newspaper page; federal mine-investigation report.
Report: A student reporter revisited the January 2 Sago mine disaster in West Virginia: relatives briefly celebrated inaccurate news that twelve trapped miners had survived, before learning that only one was alive. The article emphasizes the anguish caused by communication failures.
Assessment: The false initial rescue news is part of the event's documented public history, not evidence that the eventual toll was concealed. The Mine Safety and Health Administration's investigation documents twelve deaths and one survivor; some physical measurements and timings in the student's retelling are less reliable than the official investigation.
