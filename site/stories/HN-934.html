Report: A student columnist criticizes the partisan impasse that idled many federal operations and workers during the October government shutdown and worries about future deadlines.
Assessment: Editorial attribution of political blame is not an official causal finding. GAO documented a sixteen-day partial shutdown from the lapse in appropriations; effects varied by agency, employee category, and specific appropriations authority.
