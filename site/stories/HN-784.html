Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 3 — original page; NASA Hubble timeline.
Report: The paper celebrated Space Shuttle Discovery carrying the $1.5 billion Hubble Space Telescope into orbit after delays, anticipating exceptionally sharp astronomical images.
Assessment: An accurate launch, but expectations preceded the optical test results. NASA dates the launch to April 24; a spherical aberration discovered later impaired early images, and a 1993 shuttle servicing mission installed corrective optics.
