Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; SEC on the 1987 decline.
Report: The day after the crash, the newspaper described an initial 200-point Dow gain reversing as trading intensified. It recalled Monday's approximately 508-point fall, described as a roughly $500 billion market selloff.
Assessment: An intraday market snapshot, not the final October 20 closing price or a conclusive cause analysis. The SEC gives the October 19 Dow loss as 22.6%; the article's dollar-loss estimate reflects a contemporaneous valuation rather than cash physically disappearing.
