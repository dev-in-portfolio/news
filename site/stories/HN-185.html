Discovery narrative

Source: Evansville Daily Journal, p. 2, reprinting local correspondence — original page.
Report: A man named John Winter, already imprisoned with Frank E. Riley and Adam Snyder on robbery charges, reportedly told a visiting sheriff that the group had broken into the Vincennes post office in October 1859, rifled mailbags, and discarded them in a river; he also attributed other burglaries to the group.
Assessment: Newspaper's report of a purported prison confession, not a separately documented conviction for each offense mentioned. Follow-up open.
