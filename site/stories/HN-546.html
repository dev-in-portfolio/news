Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; German Resistance Memorial Center, July 20 plot.
Report: The paper said Adolf Hitler survived an explosion at his heavily protected headquarters, relying initially on the German official news agency's description of minor injuries and injured officers.
Assessment: The attempted assassination was real; Claus von Stauffenberg's bomb exploded but did not kill Hitler, and the regime subsequently executed numerous participants and suspects. Because the initial medical details came from Nazi-controlled news, they cannot be treated as independently examined testimony; a successful nationwide anti-Nazi revolt also did not follow.
