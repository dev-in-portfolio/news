Recorded finding

Loma Prieta: Los Angeles Times 20 Oct sharply lowered Nimitz freeway deaths; Washington Post 26 Oct analyzed the erroneous early tallies.

Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; USGS final account.
Report: Two days after the October 18 headline, the newspaper explicitly acknowledged that death estimates had shrunk amid aftershocks and continuing searches and relief efforts.
Assessment: A contemporary correction within the reporting sequence, not proof that the original headline's claimed victims later disappeared. USGS's established count is 63; early collapsed-structure estimates substantially exceeded the verified death toll.
