Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2, Gallatin, Missouri telegram — original page.
Report: Frank James was acquitted in Gallatin, Missouri; the courtroom reaction was heated and several observers condemned the verdict. The report said other charges still awaited disposition, including allegations connected to conductor Westfall's killing at Winston and an earlier Gallatin bank robbery. The jury's verdict was reported alongside criticism of the prosecution evidence and claims, not established by the article, of jury interference.
Assessment: Verified contemporaneous report of an acquittal on the charge tried, not exoneration on every other accusation. Its links to the 1881 Winston robbery, HN-250, must not be collapsed into an assertion of a single definitive outcome for all charges. Other dispositions open.
