Discovery narrative

Source: Evansville Daily Journal, p. 3, contemporary battle dispatch — original page; National Park Service battle assessment.
Report: The newspaper vividly described the July 30 mine explosion under Confederate entrenchments outside Petersburg, a simultaneous Union artillery bombardment, and initial gains by Burnside's troops through the gap. The dispatch captured the attack's early promise. The National Park Service's retrospective account records that the Union assault subsequently collapsed in confusion and was repulsed.
Assessment: Period battlefield snapshot placed against corroborated later outcome, demonstrating why an early dispatch cannot be read as the final result. Outcome contextualized.
