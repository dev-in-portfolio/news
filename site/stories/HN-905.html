Discovery narrative

Source: Nappanee Advance-News, p. 6 — original local fund-raising report; NWS storm history.
Report: Woodview Elementary students turned a grade-versus-grade penny contest into local tornado relief, with the paper reporting $3,750.49 raised and recalling their earlier fundraising after Katrina.
Assessment: Reported receipts for a specific school drive, not proof of how each dollar was subsequently distributed. The beneficiary was the Nappanee Tornado Victims' Fund; expenditure records remain a separate research question.
