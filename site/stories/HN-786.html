Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 3 — original page; U.S. State Department Gulf War chronology.
Report: Troops from Iraq entered Kuwait before dawn, seized government buildings, and forced the emir into Saudi Arabia. Iraq publicly asserted that it was supporting a supposed domestic Kuwaiti revolutionary movement.
Assessment: An invasion confirmed in later diplomatic and military records; the claim of a local revolutionary invitation was Iraq's justification, not an independently established fact. The invasion triggered sanctions and a multinational response.
