Report: Under the caption “Certain death,â€ the newspaper showed a mother holding a four-year-old boy, Ali Moosa, in a Mogadishu refugee camp. It predicted he would likely die in days without medicine for a severe facial infection, amid wider famine and armed violence.
Assessment: The photograph documents severe reported need, not the child's subsequently verified fate. Without a follow-up record, the caption's prediction must remain a prediction. Nor does one photograph establish a national mortality figure or a single cause of the Somalia crisis.
