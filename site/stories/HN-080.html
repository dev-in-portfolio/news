Discovery narrative

Source: Indiana Republican, p. 1, reproducing a London dispatch citing journals from Bamberg — original page.
Report: The item alleged that a miller and his wife killed a visitor named Flor amid the influence of a religious movement associated with a traveling leader called Kloss. It reported their detention, broader official interventions, and Kloss's subsequent confinement in Meissen.
Assessment: Highly sensational, multilayered reprint with allegations against named people. No Saxon court file or independent reporting has been examined; the claims must not be treated as adjudicated facts. Follow-up open.
