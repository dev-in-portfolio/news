Recorded finding

1956 Rosa Jordan bus shooting collector title says 'shot dead'; actual report describes a woman wounded while pregnant.

Discovery narrative

Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page; National Park Service, bus integration decision.
Report: The story reported a sniper firing into a newly integrated Montgomery bus, wounding passenger Rosa Jordan and temporarily halting city bus operations.
Assessment: The 381-day boycott had formally ended following the court order, but legal desegregation did not end white-supremacist violence. The report should not be mistaken for a second beginning of the boycott; it is an early account of retaliation after a significant civil-rights victory.
