Discovery narrative

Source: Muncie Times, p. 9 — original Mathis commentary; DOJ trial outcome.
Report: The column describes the then-recent sixteen-count federal indictment of Louisiana Congressman William Jefferson and argues that his district should not be deprived of Katrina recovery assistance because of its representative's legal troubles.
Assessment: At publication, the charged conduct was alleged rather than adjudicated. A federal jury convicted Jefferson on eleven counts in 2009. Legal outcomes after that verdict need individual checking; the July 2007 indictment cannot be represented as a contemporaneous conviction.
