Source: Muncie Times, p. 1 — original front-page report; presidential statement archived by the White House.
Report: The newspaper covered President Obama's announcement that U.S. forces killed al-Qaeda leader Osama bin Laden in Abbottabad, Pakistan, almost a decade after the September 11 attacks, and reported public reactions.
Assessment: The death and operation were officially announced; some immediate operational details remained subject to revision and later disclosure. The front page is contemporaneous public reaction, not a complete special-operations record.
