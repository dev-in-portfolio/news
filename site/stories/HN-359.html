Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Semi-Weekly Republican, p. 2 — original page; Mine Safety and Health Administration historical disaster table.
Report: A tremendous blast at Monongah mines Nos. 6 and 8 in West Virginia was said to have trapped about 500 men. The article detailed the damaged entrances and rescue impediments and offered coal dust as a suspected cause without citing a finished inspection.
Assessment: MSHA's historical disaster table records 362 killed on December 6, 1907. Other federal narrative accounts use slightly different tallies; 500 reportedly entombed does not mean 500 documented fatalities. The significance of the accident is not diminished by correcting the initial count.
