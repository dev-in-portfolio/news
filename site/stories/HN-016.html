Source: New-Jersey Gazette, p. 4 — original page.
Report: In a separate piece, a Kingston report repeated a tale from France of a woman supposedly cured of palsy after sleeping beside three dogs, each of which reportedly became paralyzed and died.
Assessment: Unverified extraordinary medical tale, not credible medical evidence. The printed text says a traveler vouched for it; identity and provenance unresolved.
