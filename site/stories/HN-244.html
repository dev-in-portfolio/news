Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 3 — original page.
Report: U.S. Senator Zachariah Chandler of Michigan reportedly retired after a long speech in Chicago on October 31 and was found dead the next morning in his room at the Grand Pacific Hotel. The account first summarized doctors' view as heart disease, then quoted an examining consultation's written conclusion of natural causes, probably cerebral hemorrhage. A postmortem was reportedly not performed.
Assessment: Contemporary example of medical uncertainty during sudden-death reporting. The differing early formulations do not establish an unnatural death. The article's stated medical opinion is provisional; original coroner materials remain unchecked. Follow-up open.
