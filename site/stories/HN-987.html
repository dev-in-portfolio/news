Source: The Guardian, June 24 — original decision coverage.
Report: The U.S. Supreme Court ruled in Dobbs v. Jackson Women's Health Organization that the Constitution did not guarantee a right to abortion, overturning Roe v. Wade and Planned Parenthood v. Casey.
Assessment: A real change in federal constitutional doctrine, not an immediate nationwide ban enacted by the Court. Subsequent restrictions and exceptions varied by state and changed through further litigation.
