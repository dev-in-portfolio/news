Discovery narrative

Source: The Guardian, June 24 — original Miami-area condominium collapse report; NIST's later investigation findings.
Report: Rescuers searched the Champlain Towers South wreckage in Surfside, Florida, as officials reported one confirmed death and about 99 people then unaccounted for.
Assessment: 'Unaccounted for' included people whose status was unresolved, not a confirmed victim register. The eventual death toll was 98; engineering causation remained the subject of a longer investigation.
