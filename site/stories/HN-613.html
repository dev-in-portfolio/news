Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page; U.S. State Department, Suez Crisis history.
Report: A map titled “Trouble Erupts Near Suezâ€ showed Israeli troops advancing into Egypt and reported diplomatic and military responses.
Assessment: This page captured the opening Israeli advance, before the full Anglo-French intervention and ensuing international pressure had played out. The Suez Crisis involved Egyptian nationalization of the canal, British and French intervention, Israel's attack, and a U.S.-backed demand for a ceasefire; none is adequately explained by the map's front-line arrows alone.
