Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 1 — original page.
Report: A caption showed police gunfire damage around a women's dormitory and stated two students were killed; it also relayed officers' claim that they had been returning sniper fire during campus unrest.
Assessment: Confirmed deaths; the alleged sniper fire is explicitly a police account, not independently proved by the photograph. Photographs of bullet damage can document where shots hit but cannot, alone, demonstrate who fired first or establish a sniper's existence. The distinction is central to understanding subsequent investigations.
