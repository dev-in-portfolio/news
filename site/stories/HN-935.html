Discovery narrative

Source: Nappanee Advance-News, p. 8 — original local relief appeal; United Nations Haiyan retrospective.
Report: A short item solicited funds and essential supplies for survivors of the November 8 Philippine super-typhoon, describing displacement, destroyed homes, and urgent demands for water, food and shelter.
Assessment: An appeal is proof of fundraising outreach, not shipment or final distribution. The UN later recorded over 6,300 deaths; the paper's early needs figures and imperfect digitized text should not be used as the final humanitarian ledger.
