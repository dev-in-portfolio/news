Recorded finding

Chicago 1919 violence: Indiana 1 August seed gives 32 dead; Eugene Daily Guard 1 August reports 34.

Discovery narrative

Source: Richmond Palladium and Sun-Telegram, p. 2 — original page; Illinois State Archives coroner report.
Report: This later Associated Press account placed the toll at 32 dead—18 Black and 14 white—and more than 300 injured after troops took control. It said officials considered the violence over.
Assessment: This is a useful same-event correction to HN-415, not a second riot. The ultimate 38 deaths demonstrate that even the later official-looking figure was not final. A government declaration that order had returned does not resolve the responsibility for the attacks.
