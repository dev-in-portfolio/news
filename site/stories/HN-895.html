Source: Muncie Times, p. 21 — original Judge Greg Mathis column; GAO on displaced children and schools.
Report: Mathis's column describes a Rainbow/PUSH effort to press the federal government for more equitable help to displaced New Orleans residents, arguing that charitable donations alone could not redress barriers to return and rebuilding.
Assessment: Advocacy and interpretation, not an independent audit establishing every asserted government failure. GAO contemporaneously documented significant post-hurricane social-service and education challenges; evaluating each proposed remedy requires subsequent implementation records.
