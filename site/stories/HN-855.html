Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 6 — original page.
Report: Indianapolis workers and parents described the loss of normal safety, inability to concentrate at offices, worries about loved ones around Manhattan, and attempts to console children after televised reports of the attacks.
Assessment: First-person emotion and uncertainty, not evidence that any particular reported missing relative died. This city-level response extends the front-page article; no named person's fate should be inferred from a fear expressed that week.
