Discovery narrative

Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page; U.S. Geological Survey history.
Report: The newspaper headlined scores drowned when a dam broke, describing catastrophic floodwater racing downriver after the St. Francis Dam failed north of Los Angeles.
Assessment: The March 12–13 collapse is firmly documented. The U.S. Geological Survey cites at least 431 deaths; early vague or smaller casualty claims undercounted victims. The disaster prompted examination of dam design, geology, and construction conditions rather than supplying evidence of a deliberate attack.
