Discovery narrative

Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page; Richmond Palladium and Sun-Telegram, p. 1 — contemporaneous account; Florida State Archives survivor testimony.
Report: One first-day wire report asserted 22 Black and white people had been killed in 'race riots' at Rosewood, Florida. Another, citing reports from the nearby town of Sumner, said a mob had burned almost the entire village. Both accounts were composed while violence and flight continued.
Assessment: The Florida State Archives documents a racially motivated white-mob massacre and destruction of Rosewood's Black community. The 22 figure was an early, unverified estimate, not a verified victim list. Labeling the event a generic, symmetrical 'race riot' conceals who was targeted; later survivor accounts are essential.
