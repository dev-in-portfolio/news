Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2 — original page.
Report: A short report from Bijeljina, Bosnia, claimed residents had heard that banker Baron Albert Rothschild faced execution unless someone volunteered to take his place for one million florins. Applicants allegedly formed a pool to divide the reward and draw lots for the sacrificial participant. Crucially, the article said the local authorities were trying to convince applicants the entire rumor was false.
Assessment: Contemporary report about the spread of an implausible false rumor, not an actual death sentence or a genuine market for substitute executions. Follow-up open on origin, not on the supposed execution.
