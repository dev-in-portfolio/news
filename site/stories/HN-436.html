Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 4 — original page.
Report: An editorial juxtaposed Interior Secretary Albert B. Fall's earlier statement that he received no money connected with the oil leases against E. L. Doheny's testimony that he had advanced Fall $100,000 in 1921, supposedly as a ranch loan.
Assessment: The paper exposed a concrete inconsistency in sworn and written accounts, but an editorial juxtaposition is not itself a criminal judgment. Later Teapot Dome proceedings established serious corruption, and Fall was convicted of taking a bribe; distinguishing the 1924 testimony from those later legal findings matters. This follows the investigation first reported in HN-431.
