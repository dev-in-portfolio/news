Discovery narrative

Source: Richmond Palladium (Weekly), p. 3, collating New York eyewitness accounts and newspaper commentary — original page.
Report: The account described a large New York City fire, reporting 546 buildings destroyed and substantial financial losses. Witnesses said a tremendous explosion near stores containing saltpeter and other combustible materials knocked down surrounding buildings. Reports proposed different causes, including stored gunpowder or gas works; they did not agree on the explanation.
Assessment: Contemporary catastrophe reporting with conflicting early causal hypotheses, rather than evidence that a single explanation had been verified. Follow-up open.
