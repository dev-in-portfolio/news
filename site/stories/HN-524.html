Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; FDR Presidential Library, presidential elections.
Report: The day-after-election paper published moving state and national returns, showing Franklin Roosevelt on course for a third presidential term even as Republican Wendell Willkie led in Indiana in the partial local count.
Assessment: Roosevelt won the national election, becoming the first U.S. president elected to three terms. Early incomplete state returns, including the pictured Indiana margin, are not final certified vote totals, and Indiana's results must not be mistaken for the nationwide winner.
