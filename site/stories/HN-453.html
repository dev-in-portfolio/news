Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page; Bath School Museum historical account.
Report: Follow-up coverage said the death toll had mounted to 43, identifying school official Andrew Kehoe as responsible as families mourned and investigators inspected the site.
Assessment: The museum's 43 bombing victims matches this widely cited count; some other totals include Kehoe or his wife, whom he had killed separately, and thus use different counting rules. Those numbers should not be presented as direct contradictions without stating the population counted. See
