Recorded finding

Armenia 1988 quake Los Angeles Times 9 Dec feared losses could reach 100,000, while Washington Post said tens of thousands.

Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; USGS earthquake catalog chronology.
Report: Mikhail Gorbachev returned to command relief operations after the December 7 earthquake struck Soviet Armenia; the story described collapsed buildings, broken transport links, and people trapped in rubble, with tens of thousands feared dead.
Assessment: An emergency estimate, not a completed victim register. The USGS historical listing describes at least 25,000 killed. The day-after coverage captures severe impediments to rescue but cannot independently count all victims.
