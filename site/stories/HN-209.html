Discovery narrative

Source: Evansville Journal, p. 1, telegram relayed via Augusta — original page.
Report: A dispatch transmitted via Augusta, Georgia and attributed to Lake City, Florida, described a farm in Hamilton County suffering a sudden ground collapse: about two acres reportedly dropped fifty feet, filling with water, and the affected area was said to be expanding to four acres. The writer suggested nearby streams and groundwater had found a new outlet.
Assessment: Striking secondhand geological report, not a measured survey. The Augusta relay point is not evidence the farm was in Georgia; Hamilton County, Florida, lies near Lake City. The newspaper's rough dimensions and precise site have not been independently verified. Follow-up open.
