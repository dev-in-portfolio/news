Discovery narrative

Source: Muncie Times, p. 27 — original syndicated magazine feature; Federal Reserve crisis chronology.
Report: A Black Enterprise retirement-planning feature linked falling retirement portfolios, collapsing financial firms including Lehman Brothers, and the housing bust to a crisis in long-term financial security.
Assessment: Contemporary consumer-finance framing, not a representative survey of retirement losses. The Federal Reserve's later history distinguishes Lehman's failure from the support provided to some other firms; the economic categories must not be collapsed into 'all banks were bailed out.'
