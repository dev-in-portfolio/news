Report: An advocacy text on a local news page links the Paris attacks, Jerusalem violence, ISIS and al-Qaeda into a sweeping argument about jihadist ideology. Its explicit 'factual look' heading should not disguise its argumentative form.
Assessment: Advocacy content, not an independently corroborated newspaper investigation or proof that all episodes have identical actors or motives. The UN condemned ISIL attacks, including Paris on November 13, but that does not independently verify the advertisement's every generalization.
