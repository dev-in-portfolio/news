Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page.
Report: A collision between Southern Railway trains Royal Palm and Ponce de Leon near Rockmart, Georgia, produced a headline claiming 17 killed and 36 injured, while an official quoted in the same report initially gave 16 killed and 29 injured.
Assessment: The article itself gives conflicting early counts, a warning not to treat the most prominent figure as the finalized toll. A definitive independent casualty roster and the official cause investigation have not been established in this pass; follow-up open.
