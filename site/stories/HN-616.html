Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 1 — original page; National Park Service, Little Rock crisis timeline.
Report: Amid the desegregation crisis, the Recorder featured Little Rock Mayor Woodrow Mann's criticism of Governor Orval Faubus and cited an FBI assessment in dispute with the governor's asserted fears of violence.
Assessment: The page is important because it records opposition to state obstruction, not just images of hostile crowds. The claim that a governor was keeping order must be assessed against his National Guard's actual obstruction of nine Black students' access to Central High School.
