Discovery narrative

Source: Muncie Times, p. 29 — original economic news brief; Bureau of Labor Statistics.
Report: A news brief reports more than 17 percent seasonally adjusted unemployment among Black men in an October survey, versus lower rates for white men and women, drawing attention to unequal labor-market effects.
Assessment: A report of subgroup survey estimates, not 'one in five' for every Black adult or a direct census. Definition, reference month, seasonal adjustment, and age category must be held constant before comparing BLS figures.
