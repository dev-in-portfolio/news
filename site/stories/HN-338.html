Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page.
Report: An explosion at a United States Cartridge Company magazine in Lowell, Massachusetts, destroyed the stone structure, damaged neighboring homes, and caused fires. The article estimated about 25 dead and 30 injured and acknowledged that earlier reports had predicted as many as 75 deaths. The force was reportedly felt in distant communities, where some thought it an earthquake.
Assessment: A well-documented contemporary correction within one newspaper article: even its revised count is provisional. A full final victim roster and technical investigation have not been examined. Follow-up open.
