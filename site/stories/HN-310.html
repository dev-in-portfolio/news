Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2 — original page.
Report: At the Maule Coal Company mine in Princeton, workers were clearing debris from an earlier nonfatal explosion when a second gas blast killed six men and injured at least eight. Among the named dead was the company's superintendent Robert Maule. The report describes damaged cages, an improvised rescue bucket, and miners initially feared dead who were brought out alive. The state mine inspector was expected to investigate.
Assessment: The newspaper itself distinguished presumed fatalities from rescues. Further deaths among the wounded, and the explosion's cause, are not established here. Follow-up open.
