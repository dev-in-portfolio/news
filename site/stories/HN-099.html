Discovery narrative

Source: Indiana Intelligencer, p. 1, reprinting an account of proceedings in Madison — original page.
Report: The newspaper described murder trials concerning the killing of Indigenous people at Fall Creek. It reported guilty verdicts against Andrew Sawyer, John T. Bridge, and his son John Bridge, and said the jury recommended clemency for the younger Bridge. A June 3 execution date was reported as scheduled.
Assessment: Detailed contemporary trial account, including attributed testimony and judicial rulings. A schedule is not verification that an execution took place; official docket, clemency, and execution records are still to be checked. Follow-up open.
