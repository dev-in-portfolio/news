Discovery narrative

Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page; National Weather Service historical analysis.
Report: The front page put the latest Florida hurricane death estimate at 2,200, with an estimated 1,700 Black victims, while recovery and identification continued.
Assessment: The 2,200 was a contemporary estimate, superseded by the National Weather Service's at least 2,500 dead. The paper's specific racial breakdown has not been separately audited against a victim register; its presence in the reporting is important, but it must not be misrepresented as a finalized census.
