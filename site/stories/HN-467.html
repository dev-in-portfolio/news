Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; Equal Justice Initiative remembrance.
Report: After a white mob lynched Thomas Shipp and Abram Smith in Marion, Indiana, on August 7, the newspaper announced state efforts to punish the mob but paired that story with sensational accusations against the lynched teenagers relating to an earlier attack.
Assessment: The lynchings were extrajudicial murders. The victims' alleged involvement in another crime was not a conviction and cannot justify the mob's acts.
