Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Union, p. 2, reprinting the Brooklyn Union and Argus — 1877 original page; The Civilian and Gazette (Galveston), April 9, 1861, p. 1 — earlier original obituary.
Report: The 1877 article stated that Mary Patten, celebrated for taking command of the clipper Neptune's Car when its captain, her husband, became incapacitated in 1856, had just died aged about 41. Yet the 1861 Texas newspaper had already published a death notice for the same Mary Ann Patten, widow of Captain Joshua Patten, explicitly recalling the Neptune's Car rescue and reporting her death at roughly 23.
Assessment: A directly demonstrable chronological contradiction between two original newspaper reports. The later story repeats distinctive identifying facts about the ship and captain; it cannot be accepted as an accurate first death report. The cause of the 1877 republication error is unknown, not evidence of deception. Correction established; error mechanism open.
