Source: New-Jersey Gazette, p. 4 — original page.
Report: A dispatch from St. George's, Grenada, dated September 4 attributed an explosion at Fort Frederick's powder magazine to lightning. It mentions about 150 barrels of powder, four deaths, and force strong enough to open doors and windows in town.
Assessment: Specific contemporary disaster report; independent military or colonial records not yet consulted. Follow-up open.
