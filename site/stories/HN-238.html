Discovery narrative

Source: Indiana State Sentinel, p. 3 — original page.
Report: In a daylight robbery at the Indiana National Bank, at Washington and Meridian Streets in Indianapolis, a man reportedly positioned a wrapped orange box at the counter, climbed onto it, grabbed packages of currency, and escaped while officials and passersby reacted. The report put the initial haul at $25,000 and said two packages of $500 each were dropped inside the bank. Two other men were arrested on suspicion of diverting attention, but the thief's identity remained unsettled.
Assessment: Contemporary account with an unusually specific mechanism, but the reported amount, suggested accomplices, and police suspicions are not final findings. The article appears to reuse earlier dispatch language, so its publication date should not be read as the exact robbery date. Follow-up open.
