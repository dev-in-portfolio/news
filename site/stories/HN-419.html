Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page; Supreme Court decision, Newberry v. United States.
Report: A wire digest reported a federal grand jury indicting Michigan senator Truman H. Newberry, 14 leading campaign workers, and 120 others on alleged 1918 election-spending, conspiracy, and mail-law violations.
Assessment: This is a record of indictment, not conviction. A jury did subsequently convict Newberry (HN-420), but the Supreme Court reversed his conviction in 1921 (HN-425). Treating the 1919 accusation as his final legal status would be wrong.
