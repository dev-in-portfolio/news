Discovery narrative

Source: Semi-Weekly Journal (Indianapolis), p. 2, reprinting the Richmond Palladium — original page.
Report: The story said Joshua B. Otey, formerly a deputy postmaster, had been charged with taking money from letters, was indicted, and posted $1,000 bail for a federal-court appearance at Indianapolis. When his case was called, he reportedly did not appear, and the court ordered action against his sureties. The originating editor regarded the absence as proof of wrongdoing.
Assessment: Published report of indictment, bail, and nonappearance; the newspaper's inference of guilt is not a judicial finding. Follow-up open.
