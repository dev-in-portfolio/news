Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2 — original page.
Report: After reporting that Emma DeKoker of DeMotte had been located at a Chicago hospital and transferred to an Illinois asylum, the paper explicitly withdrew that claim. It said she disappeared November 12 while staying with relatives near Pullman after setting out to obtain medicine for a burned arm. Relatives and Chicago police had found no trace. The article speculated about her mental health and possible accident.
Assessment: The important discovery is a contemporary retraction: she remained missing as of publication. The article provides no basis to diagnose illness, declare her dead, or infer a crime. Follow-up open.
