Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2 — original page.
Report: A Chicago and Alton passenger train near Glendale, Missouri, was stopped by masked gunmen who reportedly robbed express property and passengers, including women in a sleeping car. The newspaper said two bandits each claimed to be Jesse James; one claimed to hold the pistol used to kill conductor Westfall during the earlier Winston robbery. A brakeman's account described narrowly flagging down an approaching freight train, preventing a possible collision.
Assessment: Reported second train robbery and practical near-disaster, but participants' self-identifications were claims by masked attackers, not independent identification. Reported amounts were estimates. Follow-up open.
