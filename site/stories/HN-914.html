Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, pp. 1 and 3 — original campus front page; student follow-up; CDC pandemic history.
Report: Students described classroom sanitizers, hand-washing notices, absences and concerns about the emerging H1N1 vaccine; a second article expected first vaccine supplies during October.
Assessment: Direct local coverage of school response. Vaccine distribution timing was prospective when written, and H1N1 was a real pandemic; neither panic nor a claim that all students were infected follows from precautions alone.
