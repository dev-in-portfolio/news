Discovery narrative

Source: Evansville Journal, p. 1, reprinting November 7 New York dispatch — original page.
Report: The steamship Cumberland, arriving from Puerto Cabello, reported that its first assistant engineer and stewardess had disappeared aboard ship during the night of October 31. The first report presumed they might have fallen overboard but said it did not know whether an accident or deliberate act was involved.
Assessment: Specific reported disappearance of two crew members; cause unknown in this dispatch. No name list, ship's log, inquiry, or later outcome has been checked. Follow-up open.
