Recorded finding

Pakistan earthquake October 2005: Guardian 20 Oct reports ~79,000 toll; LA Times 18 Oct distinguishes lower official tally and locally generated estimates.

Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 3 — original student report; USGS scientific event record.
Report: Student writer Amy Greer described the October 8 Kashmir earthquake, reporting an earlier estimate near 20,000 deaths had already risen to about 35,000 as buildings collapsed and survivors were rescued.
Assessment: Interim fatality counts, not a certified final register. USGS identifies the main earthquake as magnitude 7.6; exact totals differ across official reporting agencies and geographic definitions, so a final number should not be inferred from this article.
