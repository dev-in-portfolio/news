Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 3 — original page; USGS historical earthquake list.
Report: The article announced dwindling rescue hopes, damaged relief aircraft, and an official estimate of more than 50,000 dead in Armenia. It also carried allegations of clashes over detained nationalist activists.
Assessment: The estimate is not today's established toll. USGS tabulations report at least 25,000 deaths for the Spitak-area earthquake. Unrest claims are attributed contemporary reporting and do not establish the culpability or motives of individual activists.
