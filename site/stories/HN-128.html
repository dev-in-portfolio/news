Discovery narrative

Source: Richmond Palladium (Weekly), p. 1, reprinting the Cincinnati Gazette — original page.
Report: A report described the execution of John Washburn for the robbery and murder of shopkeeper William Beaver. It said a fellow suspect, Hoover, had testified against Washburn, later recanted, and triggered a reprieve; subsequent statements allegedly implicated David Davis and members of Hoover's family. The article itself observed that key aspects of the trial evidence had become disputed.
Assessment: Published retrospective account of a trial, reprieve, shifting testimony, and execution. It does not by itself establish whether any recantation was true or whether the conviction was wrongful. Follow-up open.
