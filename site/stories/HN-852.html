Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 1 — original AP report.
Report: At a Statehouse observance of Indiana's 184th statehood anniversary, officials joked about Supreme Court justices being particularly busy in Florida while attention remained fixed on that state's contested electoral votes.
Assessment: A local ceremony with contemporaneous election commentary, not a source for detailed Supreme Court holdings. Indiana's commemoration and the national litigation are separate news events brought together by the report's timing.
