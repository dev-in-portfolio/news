Discovery narrative

Source: Western Sun, p. 2, reproducing a St. John's report through the Boston Palladium — original page.
Report: The account said a February fire destroyed about 130 houses and displaced roughly 1,500 people during winter. Its author also accused some onlookers of stealing rescued property and failing to help; this criticism was expressed in conspicuously class-biased language.
Assessment: Published disaster estimates plus the correspondent's unverified allegation of pillage. Neither is a systematic count or finding against specific persons. Follow-up open.
