Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; FDA drug-approval history.
Report: The paper said FDA approval of AZT (zidovudine) brought cautious hope because it could prolong some patients' lives, while doctors warned that toxicity, particularly bone-marrow suppression, made it far from a cure.
Assessment: A historically important first antiretroviral approval, not a definitive treatment for every patient. FDA dates approval to March 1987. The contemporary article's medical uncertainty and adverse-effect reporting should remain visible.
