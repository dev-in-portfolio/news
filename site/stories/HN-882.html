Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 3 — original student report; NOAA historical database.
Report: A student feature on rebuilding after the Indian Ocean tsunami reported more than 220,000 deaths and described relief and reconstruction across several countries.
Assessment: An updated early-2005 estimate, sharply higher than the preceding December's >50,000 count, and close to NOAA's later roughly 227,899. The figure and geographic reach differ because the news was no longer a first-week snapshot; pledges of aid are not proof that funds were disbursed.
