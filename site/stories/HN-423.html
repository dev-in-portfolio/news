Recorded finding

1920 Wall Street explosion first-day Bend Bulletin: 16 killed and possible collision/accident or bomb.

Discovery narrative

Source: Richmond Palladium and Sun-Telegram, p. 1 — original page; FBI case history.
Report: A noon explosion shattered New York's financial district, damaged the J. P. Morgan building, and left many dead and wounded. The first large headline called the cause mysterious and reported a police theory that a dynamite-laden truck had collided; a federal investigator reportedly discounted a bombing theory.
Assessment: The FBI identifies this as the deliberate Wall Street bombing of 1920. The accident theory was a rapidly discarded initial hypothesis. The perpetrator was never conclusively identified; later theories do not make any named group judicially proven responsible.
