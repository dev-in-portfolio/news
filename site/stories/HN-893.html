Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 2 — original report.
Report: The newspaper quoted a philanthropy survey reporting, by December 15, approximately $3 billion in private U.S. hurricane donations, $1.8 billion for Indian Ocean tsunami relief, and $128 million for the Pakistan earthquake. Interviewees questioned whether what appeared as giving fatigue was instead households reaching their financial limits.
Assessment: As-of-date donations and respondents' opinions, not audited final receipts or disbursements. Different disasters' fundraising windows and organizational definitions affect comparison; giving volumes do not alone measure whether aid reached intended beneficiaries.
