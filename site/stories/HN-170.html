Source: Jasper Banner, p. 10, publishing the judgment and judge's remarks — original page.
Report: The trial court found David M. Stocking guilty of first-degree murder in the killing of John Rose and imposed a death sentence, with an execution date initially set for September 14. The report discussed the evidence and the judge's remarks.
Assessment: Contemporary conviction and sentence report; it must be read with HN-173 and HN-174, because his execution was first postponed and then reported as carried out in January 1856. Not evidence he was executed in September.
