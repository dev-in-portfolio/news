Report: The Black community newspaper described shock and prayer after hijacked airplanes struck the World Trade Center and Pentagon, quoting local resident Joe Atkins and reporting death counts still developing.
Assessment: Contemporaneous public reaction, not a final victim register or completed inquiry. The September 11 attacks unfolded on September 11; the Friday publication captures how local people processed information in the immediate days after them.
