Discovery narrative

Source: Terre Haute Evening Gazette, p. 1, Buffalo dispatch — original page.
Report: At Prospect Station, New York, a broken rail on a high trestle caused passenger and baggage cars to tumble roughly thirty feet while the locomotive crossed safely. The crushed cars ignited, severely hampering rescue; victims and witnesses described people trapped inside. The report separately mentioned a second, still poorly detailed Louisville-area wreck the same day.
Assessment: Contemporary investigation-stage coverage of a major railway calamity. The headline combined deaths and injuries, and neither definitive totals nor responsibility should be assumed. Follow-up open.
