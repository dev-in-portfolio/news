Discovery narrative

Source: Evansville Daily Journal, p. 1 — original page.
Report: James Reynolds, the Vincennes agent of Adams Express, had been arrested in connection with a robbery of that company's office. The paper reported that a preliminary examination acquitted him, and described public criticism of the company superintendent for initiating the arrest. This was an express-office case, distinct from HN-185's post-office robbery.
Assessment: Published favorable preliminary disposition. The word acquitted describes the paper's report of that hearing, not a reconstructed full case history. Follow-up open.
