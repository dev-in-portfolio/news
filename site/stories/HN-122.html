Discovery narrative

Source: Indiana American, p. 1, reprinting a February 2 House of Representatives debate — original page.
Report: The paper printed a proposed request for federal executive correspondence concerning the survey, location, sale, and transfer of Indigenous land reserves east of the Mississippi since 1825. The ensuing argument included concerns about possible fraud and objections to casting unsupported aspersions on entire communities.
Assessment: Documented public congressional inquiry and contentious debate, not a finding of specific fraud. Whether the records were produced and what they showed are not yet traced. Follow-up open.
