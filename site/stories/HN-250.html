Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2, quoting express messenger C. H. Murray — original page; Rensselaer Republican, August 4, p. 2, eyewitness interviews — second original page.
Report: Masked gunmen boarded a Chicago, Rock Island and Pacific train near Winston, Missouri, in July, killed conductor William Westfall and passenger McMillan, and robbed the express safe. The messenger's detailed version described being forced at gunpoint to unlock it; an interviewed passenger described the panicked rush through the train and disputed some claims about how the messenger was treated and the number of shots.
Assessment: Multiple contemporary witness-attributed accounts, showing how immediate details diverge while the killings and robbery recur across reports. Do not treat every alleged gang identification or reconstruction as settled. Connected later to the 1883 Frank James trial report, HN-259.
