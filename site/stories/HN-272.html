Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2, Sioux City, Iowa special — original page.
Report: After the shooting of minister George C. Haddock, suspicion fell on brewer John Arensdorf. A brewery driver, Henry Peters, had disappeared, prompting claims that he too had been murdered. Neighbors reported a quarrel and groans; Arensdorf said Peters had left voluntarily after a disagreement over horse care. The same story conceded that a late report placed Peters at his former home in Davenport, and rival witness accounts conflicted about the shooter.
Assessment: A record of rapidly multiplying and contested allegations, not proof that Peters was killed or that a named suspect fired the shot. Follow-up open.
