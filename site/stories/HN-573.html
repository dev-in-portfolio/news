Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page.
Report: A live dispatch from San Marino, California, described a parallel-shaft rescue for Kathy Fiscus, trapped in a narrow abandoned well casing. A rescuer thought he saw pink fabric deep underground but said he was unsure.
Assessment: The reported sighting was an uncorroborated visual clue, not confirmation the child was alive or that rescuers had reached her. This became a national live-media rescue effort, with a tragic outcome verified in the subsequent paper.
