Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2 — original page.
Report: A dynamite manufacturing plant belonging to F. A. Reynolds & Co., near White Pigeon, Michigan, exploded on September 4. The article estimated 20 tons of dynamite on site and 16 workers dead, while alleging payroll records were destroyed, delaying identification. A church spire and nearby buildings were reported damaged by the shock.
Assessment: Contemporary industrial-disaster report, not a verified final death list, precise explosive inventory, or cause determination. Follow-up open.
