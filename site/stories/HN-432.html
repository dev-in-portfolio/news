Discovery narrative

Source: Richmond Palladium and Sun-Telegram, p. 1 — original page.
Report: An Associated Press report from Herrin, Illinois, described the killing of strikebreaking mine workers during violent confrontations involving thousands of armed miners and supporters. Its headline called the killings a massacre, and its dispatch said at least 27 dead while noting no immediate formal investigation or arrests.
Assessment: The Herrin Massacre is a documented labor-violence atrocity; accounts vary on the final count and identities of participants. The reporter's figure, quoted admissions, and accusations against particular people are contemporary claims unless confirmed in subsequent proceedings. Follow-up open on the verified victim register and court outcomes.
