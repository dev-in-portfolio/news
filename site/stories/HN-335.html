Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper County Democrat, p. 2 — original page.
Report: The paper described coordinated investigations of E. J. Arnold & Co. and related turf-investment promotions, including a raid on a Chicago branch. Investigators said customers in 51 Western cities and towns had been drawn in, with losses described in the millions; company agents and managers were named in a mixture of arrests and allegations.
Assessment: Evidence of a contemporary fraud investigation and alleged geographic scope, not an audited loss figure, judicial verdict, or verified claim that any named operator escaped with a particular amount. Follow-up open.
