Discovery narrative

Source: Crawfordsville Review, p. 2, local Montgomery County reporting — original page.
Report: Nehemiah T. Ham had vanished after leaving a gathering in Alamo in late April. After an unsuccessful search, men driving cattle noticed buzzards gathering near a fallen tree, where Ham's remains were found concealed under wood and leaves. The newspaper named several people jailed on suspicion of murder and others held over an earlier alleged robbery.
Assessment: Documented discovery and arrest report, not proof any named suspect committed murder. The reported corpse's discovery resolves the physical disappearance but not the full criminal case. Court outcome open.
