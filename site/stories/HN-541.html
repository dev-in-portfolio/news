Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; U.S. Navy, Bari armed-guard account.
Report: A delayed dispatch reported 17 Allied vessels sunk and approximately 1,000 casualties after the December 2 Luftwaffe raid at Bari, Italy, where ammunition ships exploded.
Assessment: The air attack was real; the wartime ship and casualty figures were preliminary. Crucially, the U.S. vessel John Harvey carried a secret cargo of mustard-gas bombs, and its destruction caused chemical exposures not explained in the first public reports. The U.S. Navy history supplies that later context; the initial newspaper article did not disclose it.
