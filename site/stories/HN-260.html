Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2, local/regional account — original page.
Report: Opponents of a mill dam on the Monon River near Monon, Indiana, reportedly blew a large hole in it at night after efforts to negotiate its removal failed. Following repairs and the hiring of guards, another blast opened a wider breach and broke the mill's windows; the newspaper described the proprietor being pushed into the river during an earlier watch. No responsible party was conclusively identified.
Assessment: Reported series of infrastructure-sabotage incidents arising from a local property and environmental dispute, but motive and perpetrator identities were inferred by the writer, not determined in court. The paper's comic language about those involved is not itself evidence. Follow-up open.
