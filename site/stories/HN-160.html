Discovery narrative

Source: Wabash Express, p. 1, reprinting reports from Chile via the National Intelligencer — original page.
Report: The account recounted the December 1851 revolt at the Fort Bulnes penal colony, the reported killing of governor Muñoz Gamero, seizures of vessels including the American Florida and British Eliza Cornish, and a violent struggle over their cargo. Importantly, the article also reported a follow-up: leader Cambiaso had been captured, chained at Chiloé, and some treasure recovered.
Assessment: Serialized/reprinted trans-Pacific revolt narrative containing its own later development. Details of killings, cargo totals, and legal outcome need Chilean and maritime records. Follow-up partially present; later judicial outcome open.
