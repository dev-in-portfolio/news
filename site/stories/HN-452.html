Discovery narrative

Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page; Bath School Museum historical account.
Report: The newspaper announced that a school building had been dynamited in Bath Township, Michigan, as rescue crews searched wreckage and details about the attack emerged.
Assessment: This was a deliberate bombing carried out on May 18 by Andrew Kehoe, not an accidental explosion. The breaking story precedes more complete casualty reporting; the museum documents 38 pupils, four school employees, and one bystander killed in the bombing, a total of 43 victims apart from deaths counted differently in wider descriptions of Kehoe's crimes.
