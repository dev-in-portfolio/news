Discovery narrative

Source: Indiana American, p. 3, relaying a March 26 Philadelphia report attributed to a service officer — original page.
Report: An officer was cited as saying pensioners at the Naval Asylum on the Schuylkill confined asylum officers, seriously injured the commander, and were subdued after naval-yard personnel were summoned. Alleged leaders were reportedly taken into custody aboard the Sea Gull.
Assessment: Secondhand service-officer report of a disturbance and detentions. Proceedings against the alleged participants were not traced. Follow-up open.
