Discovery narrative

Source: Wabash Herald, p. 4, reprinting the Indiana Democrat — original page.
Report: The Indiana state treasurer reportedly mailed a $100 note believed possibly counterfeit, but it never reached its recipient. A later advertisement led investigators to a stage driver between Shelbyville and Greensburg who had passed the note; he was sent before a federal judge on a mail-robbery accusation. The report mentioned contradictory statements and suspicion of accomplices.
Assessment: Contemporary investigation and arrest report, with no trial outcome; its speculation about accomplices should not be adopted as fact. Follow-up open.
