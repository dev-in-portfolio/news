Washington Post 25 Oct 2005 obituary of Rosa Parks carries a published correction about the name of an honor.

Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 5 — original memorial essay; Library of Congress Parks timeline.
Report: A remembrance considered the consequences had Rosa Parks complied with Montgomery bus segregation in 1955, treating her refusal and her life of civil-rights activism as a legacy of moral courage.
Assessment: A counterfactual tribute, not proof that the movement depended on only one person or moment. Parks died October 24, 2005; the Library of Congress holds records documenting her earlier activism, community organizing, and subsequent work beyond the famous bus protest.
