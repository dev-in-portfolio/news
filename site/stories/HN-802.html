Discovery narrative

Source: Jewish Post, p. 11 — original opinion page; FBI investigation history.
Report: A commentary compared the February World Trade Center bombing to threats confronting Israel and cited statements by diplomats, reflecting early public argument over its meaning.
Assessment: Commentary is not the perpetrators' proven identity or a finding about a whole religious or ethnic population. The FBI confirms a truck bomb was detonated February 26, killing six people. The eventual investigation and trials, not the editorial's generalizations, establish individual criminal responsibility.
