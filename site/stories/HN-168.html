Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper Banner, p. 14, Indianapolis correspondent's letter dated January 22 — original page.
Report: The writer said Frank May, cashier of the Farmers' and Mechanics' Bank of Indianapolis, had disappeared and the bank's vault appeared emptied. The article offered two competing possibilities: robbers might have kidnapped or killed him and taken his keys, or he might have taken the money himself. It openly admitted the affair was unexplained.
Assessment: Documented contemporary uncertainty, not evidence May stole funds. Attempts to search by name and bank for a later explanation met archival HTTP errors. Follow-up open.
