Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 1 — original page; National Park Service, Freedom Riders.
Report: The Black weekly printed an image of an Anniston, Alabama, bus burned by a white mob, described federal official John Seigenthaler's injuries during attacks in Montgomery, and reported riders continuing despite arrests.
Assessment: The attacks on the buses began May 14, before this May 27 issue; the issue documents subsequent resistance and consequences, not one simultaneous event. The Freedom Rides challenged segregation in interstate travel, and violence against the riders should not be relabeled as mutual disorder.
