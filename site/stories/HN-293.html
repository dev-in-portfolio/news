Report: In Pittsburgh, Hugh Dempsey, a Knights of Labor district leader, was found guilty by a jury of a charge related to the poisoning of non-union workers at Homestead's mills. The article said the jury had deliberated about three hours and noted that Dempsey was released under a substantial bond pending further steps.
