Discovery narrative

Source: People's Pilot, p. 2, Louisville dispatch — original page.
Report: During gatherings connected with Grand Army of the Republic veterans, an artillery caisson of the Louisville Legion exploded before a planned salute. The headline said four killed, but the OCR of the immediate victim list names only three, including a Black soldier, William Adams, while other soldiers were injured. Speculation that someone had been smoking was directly disputed by the commanding captain.
Assessment: Confirmed contemporary report of a fatal military accident with an internally incomplete victim roster; do not fix the figure or assign a smoking-related cause from this page alone. Follow-up open.
