Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2 — original page; HistoryLink investigation and victim list.
Report: The newspaper placed the May 10 Roslyn, Washington, mine fatalities at at least 42, with other estimates of 45 to 50, while one manager said the exact ignition source might never be known. Later historical research based on mining reports and the coroner's jury confirms 45 workers died. The inquiry found an explosion involving gas and inadequate ventilation; company and inspector theories differed over the exact ignition mechanism.
Assessment: Later fatality tally corroborated (45) and a documented investigative finding narrows the early managerial uncertainty. Details of the precise ignition sequence remain contested in the historical record. Follow-up partly resolved.
