Discovery narrative

Source: Indiana Palladium, p. 3 — original page.
Report: A single-line foreign-news item asserted that the supposed assassin of Caspar/Kaspar Hauser had been arrested and would probably be convicted and confess. It named no suspect and offered no supporting details.
Assessment: Unsubstantiated and apparently premature news claim, not evidence that a perpetrator was identified. Subsequent scholarship treats the circumstances of Hauser's fatal wound as unresolved; see the medicolegal review. Do not record the alleged arrest as a verified event.
