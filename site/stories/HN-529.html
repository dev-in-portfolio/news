Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; U.S. House of Representatives, declarations of war.
Report: A special edition described Germany and Italy declaring war first, followed by rapid congressional declarations and Roosevelt's appeal for a full-scale response.
Assessment: These December 11 declarations expanded America's war against Japan into direct war with the European Axis. The paper also carried battlefield claims that had not yet been independently corroborated; formal legal war declarations and purported tactical victories are different kinds of evidence.
