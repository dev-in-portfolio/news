Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 1 — original page; Detroit Historical Society, Uprising of 1967.
Report: The banner headline said the Justice Department and FBI were investigating charges against Detroit police after the July uprising, foregrounding accusations of abusive policing rather than describing only burning buildings or looting.
Assessment: An investigation into allegations, not proof of every charge or a verdict. The Detroit uprising began in July after a police raid and escalated amid long-standing racial and economic tensions. The Black press's choice of headline is itself evidence of divergent contemporary priorities; investigation and established misconduct remain different claims.
