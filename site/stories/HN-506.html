Recorded finding

New London 1937 school blast: Texas Daily News-Telegram 19 March and NZ Northern Advocate 20 March both printed 500 deaths.

Discovery narrative

Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page; Texas State Historical Association, New London school explosion.
Report: A headline said the school death toll was still mounting following the catastrophic March 18 explosion at the London School in Rusk County, Texas.
Assessment: The disaster was traced to leaked natural gas, not a proven deliberate bomb attack. The Texas State Historical Association gives approximately 298 deaths. Because identification and victim counts varied, the contemporary headline is not a final census.
