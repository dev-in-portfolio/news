Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2 — original page; Society for American Baseball Research biography.
Report: Investor Erastus Wiman was convicted in New York of second-degree forgery, after a jury deliberated roughly two hours and recommended mercy. The newspaper said sentence had not yet been imposed. A later biographical account records that the conviction was reversed on appeal, a fact omitted by accounts stopping at this June verdict.
Assessment: First-instance guilty verdict with verified subsequent appellate reversal; it would be false to present the 1894 result as a standing final conviction. Later outcome partly corroborated.
