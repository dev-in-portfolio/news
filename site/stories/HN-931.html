Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 4 — original campus news report; DOJ account of victims.
Report: A high-school writer describes the December 14, 2012 shooting at Sandy Hook Elementary in Newtown, Connecticut, identifying twenty children and six adult staff as the school victims and noting the killing of the attacker's mother separately.
Assessment: The school victim count is corroborated but exact terminology about the attack and the perpetrator still requires precision. The 26 were killed at the school; the perpetrator's mother was a separate victim elsewhere.
