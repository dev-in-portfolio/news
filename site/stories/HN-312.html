Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Semi-Weekly Republican, p. 2 — original page.
Report: George H. Brown, formerly of Jasper County and later a dealer in Knox, Indiana, had vanished while traveling on business. His attorney C. H. Peters asked Chicago police to help, noting that Brown was not known to be financially desperate and had possibly intended to collect a debt near Urbana. The article reprinted a report from the Chicago Inter Ocean and noted that Brown's family lacked an explanation.
Assessment: A missing-person investigation only; the story does not establish murder, fraud, voluntary flight, or his eventual fate. Follow-up open.
