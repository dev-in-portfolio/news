Report: “Presidential Aide Says Nixon to Announce Resignationâ€ described Vice President Gerald Ford being called to the White House and the expectation that Nixon would resign.
Assessment: A forecast borne out by subsequent events, not a claim resignation had already legally taken effect. Nixon announced his intention on the evening of August 8 and left office August 9; Ford then became president. The original page records anticipation and preparation during the constitutional transition.
