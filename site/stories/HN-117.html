Discovery narrative

Source: Indiana Palladium, p. 4, crediting a London paper — original page; a similar item appeared in the Rising Sun Times — original page.
Report: An item said a dying man near Ellesmere, England, admitted participating in the murders of Francis Bruce and Ann Taylor roughly twenty years earlier. A blacksmith named Preston had reportedly been convicted and executed. The new claim was that Preston was involved in the robbery but did not personally kill the victims; two other men allegedly committed the murders.
Assessment: Secondhand report of a purported confession; the underlying case and identity of the dying speaker have not been verified. The report does not establish legal innocence of every offense or conclusively prove a wrongful conviction. Follow-up open.
