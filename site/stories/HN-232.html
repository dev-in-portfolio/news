Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper Republican, p. 2, reprinting New York newspaper reports — original page.
Report: A locomotive identified as Centralia on the Easton & Amboy Railroad exploded while waiting on a siding near South Somerville, New Jersey. The blast crushed a car holding railroad workers. The paper reported five killed, thirty-three scalded or injured, and suggestions that a weakened boiler and lack of water might have contributed.
Assessment: Specific early railway-disaster account, but the stated mechanical causes are allegations, not an official technical verdict. Follow-up open.
