Discovery narrative

Source: New-York Gazette and the Weekly Mercury, p. 2 — original page.
Report: A London item dated June 17 asserted that someone once employed as a secretary to Benjamin Franklin was arrested and jailed in the Bastille after allegedly obtaining goods from Bordeaux merchants with forged bills. The person was not named.
Assessment: Uncorroborated wartime allegation republished in a Loyalist newspaper; do not attribute guilt to any individual. Follow-up open.
