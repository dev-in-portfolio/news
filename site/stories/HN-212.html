Discovery narrative

Source: Evansville Journal, p. 1, Antwerp dispatch dated June 22 — original page; U.S. diplomatic report establishing the Ticonderoga was at Antwerp that month.
Report: An American clipper named Neru (OCR spelling uncertain) reportedly experienced a violent crew mutiny while docked at Antwerp. Officers were assaulted, local police boarded, and sailors from the nearby USS Ticonderoga reportedly assisted in suppressing the disturbance.
Assessment: Contemporary transatlantic dispatch, with independent confirmation of the Ticonderoga's presence in Antwerp that month—not independent confirmation of its alleged intervention. The clipper's name needs page-image confirmation, and later prosecution or disciplinary records have not been located. Follow-up open.
