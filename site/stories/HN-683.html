Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 1 — original page; New York State Archives, Attica chronology.
Report: The headline “Shots, not knives killed N.Y. prison hostagesâ€ publicized the autopsy-based correction to early claims that inmates had slit hostages' throats during the retaking of Attica prison.
Assessment: Major correction to the initial official narrative. The New York archives document 43 deaths overall: four attributable to inmates, with 39 killed during the state's armed retaking on September 13. Autopsies showed the hostages killed in that assault were shot, not stabbed. This distinction does not excuse killings or hostage-taking elsewhere during the uprising; it identifies what the particular correction establishes.
