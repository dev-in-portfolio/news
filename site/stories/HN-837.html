Source: Jewish Post, p. 8 — original page; Justice Department retrospective.
Report: As Matthew Shepard's funeral took place, leaders of the Central Conference of American Rabbis called for stronger federal and state laws against hate violence.
Assessment: A documented policy demand, not proof that the requested law had already been enacted. The federal Matthew Shepard and James Byrd Jr. Hate Crimes Prevention Act came in 2009; the 1998 murder convictions and later national legislation are different legal events.
