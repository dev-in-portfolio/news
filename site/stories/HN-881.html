Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 6 — original commentary; NOAA historical tsunami figures.
Report: A reflection on the recent December 26 Indian Ocean earthquake and tsunami said more than 50,000 had died and warned that contaminated water could complicate relief.
Assessment: An extremely early partial toll, not the full catastrophe. NOAA's later global historical dataset attributes approximately 227,899 deaths to the earthquake and tsunami, a differently dated and much more complete estimate. The source is evidence of how incomplete the picture still was that week.
