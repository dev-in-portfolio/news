Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 3 — original security discussion; DOJ date and casualty count.
Report: An article describes safety notices sent to parents and debate over school security after the Newtown massacre, but places the attack on December 17, rather than December 14, 2012.
Assessment: A demonstrable date error within real aftermath reporting. The massacre occurred December 14. This kind of misdating is evidence of imperfect editing/OCR and reporting, not of a second school attack on December 17.
