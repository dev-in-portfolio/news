Discovery narrative

Source: Farmers & Mechanics Journal, p. 1 — original page.
Report: A polemical election essay argued against calling a state constitutional convention, warning that a new convention would have broad authority and might change Indiana's existing restriction on slavery. It urged voters to consider candidates' positions before supporting a convention.
Assessment: Documented contemporary political advocacy and concern, not evidence that a specific covert plan or a constitutional change succeeded. Follow-up open.
