Recorded finding

Frontenac coal mine 1888 original wire supposedly asserted 90 bodies recovered; later accounting cites 44 dead.

Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Republican, p. 2 — original page; Kansas Historical Society account.
Report: The first dispatch on the Frontenac, Kansas, Mine No. 2 explosion imagined 160 miners dead, effectively all but two men thought to be underground. Later on the same original page, the newspaper revised the fatality count to 37. The Kansas Historical Society records 44 men and boys killed in the November 9 disaster and notes subsequent safety reforms.
