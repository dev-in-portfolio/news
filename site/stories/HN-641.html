Discovery narrative

Source: Decatur Daily Democrat, p. 1 — original page; U.S. State Department, crisis settlement.
Report: The paper quoted Pravda's favorable account of Khrushchev's decision to dismantle missile installations, alongside the continued naval interception and other crisis news.
Assessment: The public removal announcement was a turning point, not immediate proof every missile had already been shipped away. The settlement also entailed a U.S. pledge not to invade Cuba and a privately communicated plan to remove Jupiter missiles from Turkey—the latter absent from much immediate public reporting.
