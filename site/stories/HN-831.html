Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 2 — original page.
Report: Student reporter Christine Schroeder summarized the Paris car crash, rescue efforts, Diana's death at age 36, and the outpouring of public mourning. The report appeared weeks after her August 31 death and described the September 6 funeral-week mourning period, with somewhat loose dating in the narrative.
Assessment: A secondary contemporary student account of a well-documented crash, not accident reconstruction or official inquest findings. Small chronology details in the newspaper should not be used to assign legal or moral responsibility for the collision.
