Discovery narrative

Source: Jasper Banner, p. 13, reprinting an unidentified earlier account — original page.
Report: A report concerning the death of Justus Matthews near New Haven, Connecticut, said Samuel Sly had been arrested and confessed to a brutal attack. It linked his stated motivations to a spiritualist sect called the Wakemanites, associated with his sister, Mrs. Wakeman, and quoted extraordinary claims she reportedly made about a prior journey to the spirit world.
Assessment: Reprinted report of an alleged confession and religious beliefs, not verified trial testimony or proof of supernatural explanations. The court outcome has not been traced. Follow-up open.
