Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 2 — original paired student opinions; Indiana Legislature's 2015 RFRA documents.
Report: The school paper debates Indiana's recently enacted Religious Freedom Restoration Act amid concern over LGBT discrimination, private-business practices, boycotts and conscience protections.
Assessment: Competing legal and ethical arguments, not court holdings. Indiana enacted SEA 101 in March, then adopted SEA 50's clarifications in April; characterizations of what businesses could lawfully do must account for the amendment and other applicable laws.
