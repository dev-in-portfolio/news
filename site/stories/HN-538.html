Discovery narrative

Source: Jewish Post, p. 3 — original page; USHMM, Warsaw Ghetto Uprising.
Report: The Jewish Telegraphic Agency reproduced a resistance account passed via Jewish organizations, describing Jews fighting SS and German forces as flames engulfed the Warsaw Ghetto.
Assessment: The uprising lasted April 19–May 16, 1943. The text is a transmitted resistance account, with timing and casualties not fully reconciled by the date it appeared in Indiana; it is not proof that organized resistance was still sustaining the same battle on June 4. The uprising and German destruction of the ghetto are confirmed by postwar research.
