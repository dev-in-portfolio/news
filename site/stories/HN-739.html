Report: A health column described concern about transmitting what it called the “A.I.D.S germâ€ through donated blood and shared injection needles, at a time when the disease's causative virus and reliable diagnostic testing were still being worked out.
Assessment: Public-health communication during uncertain scientific development. The bloodborne transmission concern was substantive, but a 1983 columnist's terms cannot be read as later virology or proof that a specific blood unit carried HIV. The CDC's first recognized report of the syndrome's early cases was in June 1981.
