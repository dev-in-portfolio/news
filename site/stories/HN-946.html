Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 3 — original student analysis; UNHCR's July 2015 refugee count.
Report: A student article tracks the Syrian civil war, neighboring countries taking refugees, and the difficult choices facing the United States and Europe while ISIS terrorism dominated debate.
Assessment: Dated policy interpretation, not a vetting study or evidence that refugees as a group were terrorists. UNHCR reported more than four million registered Syrian refugees in neighboring states by July 2015; refugee registration, asylum applications and internal displacement are distinct categories.
