Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Semi-Weekly Republican, p. 2 — original page; Heritage Johnstown mining history.
Report: Following a July 10 blast at the Cambria Steel Company's Rolling Mill mine in Johnstown, Pennsylvania, initial estimates ranged from 125 to 200 dead, while a rumor placed the toll even higher. The report explained that afterdamp obstructed rescuers and that miners who escaped through another entrance had only fragmentary knowledge of colleagues still underground.
Assessment: Later local research documents 112 deaths, mostly from the ensuing deadly gases. The panic-era range and the 300-death rumor must not be converted to final counts.
