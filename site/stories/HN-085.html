Discovery narrative

Sources: Indiana Centinel, p. 3 — February notice and June commentary.
Report: A signed February notice summoned shareholders to investigate bank assets, circulation, loans, director authorizations, the suspension of payments in specie, and the possibility of surrendering the charter. A June discussion complained that results of the investigation had not been fully communicated to shareholders and feared damage from continued uncertainty or piecemeal debt collection.
Assessment: A documented public financial dispute with at least one subsequent newspaper discussion; irregularities were questioned, not established as criminal fraud. Final resolutions and bank records remain unreviewed. Follow-up open.
