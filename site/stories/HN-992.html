Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 5 — original Lafayette student report; NTSB final findings.
Report: The paper reported 38 derailed railcars, 11 carrying hazardous materials, concern about vinyl chloride and a suspected bearing-overheat failure as investigations continued.
Assessment: An unusually technical student report that substantially anticipated the NTSB's later bearing-failure finding. Its chemical-hazard descriptions are not individual residents' exposure measurements, and the timing matters: the inquiry was not final in March 2023.
