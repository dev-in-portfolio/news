Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2 — original page.
Report: An ammunition caisson exploded during a U.S. Army drill procession in Chicago, scattering shrapnel, killing four soldiers and reportedly nine horses, and injuring at least 17 other people. The report suggested that a loose or defective shell cap might have caused the accident, but admitted no mechanism had been established.
Assessment: Reported military accident with an unconfirmed mechanical hypothesis, not sabotage or an identified negligent person. Follow-up open.
