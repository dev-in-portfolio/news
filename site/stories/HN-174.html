Report: The extensive eyewitness-style account described Abram Rice, Timothy Driskill, and David M. Stocking being hanged in connection with the murders of Cephas Farenbaugh and John Rose. It recounted their last conversations, their statements disputing or qualifying responsibility, and the execution ceremony. The report supersedes the earlier uncertainty over whether their death sentences were carried out, but does not by itself validate the trial evidence or fix the precise execution date.
