Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; Britannica, Spanish Civil War overview.
Report: A dispatch mapped fighting in Spanish Morocco and Spanish towns following a military rebellion, describing loyal government forces and rebel positions around the Strait of Gibraltar.
Assessment: The July 17–18 uprising developed into the 1936–1939 Spanish Civil War. This early geographic account does not establish that either faction already controlled the whole country, and the newspaper's first reports did not know the war's eventual outcome.
