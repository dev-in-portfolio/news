Discovery narrative

Source: Jewish Post, p. 1 — original page; USHMM, killing centers overview.
Report: The Post relayed a Netherlands government-in-exile charge, based on underground reports, that Jews deported from occupied Holland to unknown destinations were being murdered using poison gas.
Assessment: Mass murder in Nazi gas chambers is historically established, but the particular underground dispatch was attributed intelligence, not independent verification of each named deportation route or victim. The story demonstrates that the genocide's mechanisms were being publicly reported in Jewish newspapers during the war, not first revealed after 1945.
