Discovery narrative

Source: Western Eagle, p. 2 — original page.
Report: The newspaper reproduced documents about the killing of an Indigenous man near Grosse Isle in the Detroit River area, including correspondence involving Michigan territorial governor Lewis Cass and British authorities across the border. The material describes a $500 reward and disagreements related to responsibility and jurisdiction.
Assessment: Published purported official correspondence, with severe multi-column OCR mixing and no investigation file examined. It establishes that this dispute appeared in print, not who committed the killing. Follow-up open.
