Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Semi-Weekly Republican, p. 2 — original page; Stanford History Department's review; historical reassessment in Stanford Magazine.
Report: The paper described university cofounder Jane Leland Stanford dying in convulsions at a Honolulu hotel after drinking bicarbonate of soda, reportedly exclaiming that she had been poisoned. A previous suspected poisoning at her California residence made the story still more alarming. The dispatch treated motive and perpetrator as unknown.
Assessment: Unlike many speculative poison stories, a Honolulu coroner's jury found strychnine poisoning, as later Stanford historical scholarship documents. The killer's identity remains unproved; allegations against particular associates and competing official explanations must not be made into a judicial verdict.
