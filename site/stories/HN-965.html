Recorded finding

Camp Fire on 9 November 2018 San Francisco Chronicle 'at least five dead' vs LA Times same day 'at least nine dead'.

Discovery narrative

Source: The Guardian, November 9 — firsthand account from Paradise, California; CAL FIRE's final incident record.
Report: Evacuees described a town burned almost beyond recognition; local authorities had confirmed at least nine deaths and thousands of destroyed structures while crews searched.
Assessment: A preliminary count during an active fire. California's eventual official Camp Fire death toll was 85; the scale of the disaster was not captured by initial reports.
