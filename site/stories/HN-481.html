Discovery narrative

Source: The Daily Banner (Greencastle), p. 1 — original page; U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum explanation.
Report: In coverage of Nazi Germany's actions against opponents and Jews, a dispatch discussed Marinus van der Lubbe and opponents' accusation that Nazis themselves had set the Reichstag fire to blame Communists. It also reproduced claims of confession and harsh Nazi responses.
Assessment: The parliament building burned on February 27, not this March publication date. Responsibility for the fire itself remains a separate historical question from the established fact that Nazis exploited it to restrict rights, persecute opponents, and consolidate power.
