Discovery narrative

Source: Banner-Graphic, p. 2 — original page; USGS lessons from the Armero catastrophe.
Report: A Colombian town near Nevado del Ruiz was reportedly evacuated for a practice drill so convincing that its mayor said he believed there was a real emergency; thousands remained on surrounding hills awaiting permission to return.
Assessment: A separate post-disaster response story, not another eruption or a verified advance warning before Armero was destroyed. The report illustrates the urgency and confusion of preparedness after catastrophe, but cannot establish whether earlier warnings in the disaster area were properly communicated.
