Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2 — original page.
Report: A Flint & Pere Marquette train ran into the rear of a Lake Shore express near a tunnel close to the Toledo Union Depot. The first report identified six people killed and 16 seriously injured, many suffering from escaping steam rather than fire. The paper described how delays placed both trains close together and a flagman's warning reportedly came too late.
Assessment: Specific early reconstruction of the collision, but assigning final operational fault requires the railroad and coroner investigations. Follow-up open.
