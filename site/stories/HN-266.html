Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Journal, p. 1, January 24 London telegrams — original page.
Report: Successive blasts damaged areas of Westminster Palace and the Tower of London, injuring people including visitors and children and alarming the city. The same continuously updated news report initially described hurried arrests after witnesses saw a suspicious cab and people fleeing, then explicitly stated that all people arrested on suspicion that afternoon had been released for lack of adequate evidence. It also corrected a first rumor that the White Tower was destroyed or badly damaged.
Assessment: Contemporary terror-attack reporting with corrections already embedded in its first edition. The initial detentions were not convictions, and initial rumors about destruction should not survive their corrections. The actual perpetrators and later trials need separate documentation. Follow-up open.
