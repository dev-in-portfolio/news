Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 1 — original page; National Park Service, Emmett Till history.
Report: The Recorder printed a photograph of Emmett Till's severely battered face as his body lay in state in Chicago, alongside portraits of his mother and two accused white men before the murder trial.
Assessment: Fourteen-year-old Till was abducted and murdered in Mississippi in August 1955. The photograph became vital evidence for the broader public, made possible by Mamie Till-Mobley's insistence that the public see the violence. Period labels about the accusation against Till are not evidence that he provoked or deserved the crime.
