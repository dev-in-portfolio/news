Discovery narrative

Source: Daily State Sentinel, p. 3, reproducing an attributed shipboard letter and an explicitly political editorial introduction — original page.
Report: The letter described Chinese people allegedly abducted, confined on vessels, sold to labor shippers, and guarded by armed crews to prevent escape. It claimed the writer's vessel was carrying hundreds of such passengers, who were being sent into harsh bonded work. The editor used the material polemically in a comparison with American slavery.
Assessment: First-person-attributed accusation of forced labor and human trafficking, reproduced through a partisan newspaper; its particulars, ship identity, and numbers require independent evidence. Follow-up open.
