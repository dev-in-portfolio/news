Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Recorder, p. 6 — original page; USHMM on genocide and displacement.
Report: Women in Tanzania's Benaco camp spoke of shortages of clothing and privacy and worked with a UNHCR organizer to represent different camp sections and press for sanitation and daily needs.
Assessment: A specific humanitarian and women's-agency account, not a complete resolution of camp conditions. Refugees were not an undifferentiated population: some individuals were victims of the genocide, while other reporting raised concerns about alleged perpetrators among those who fled. Individual responsibility must be established separately.
