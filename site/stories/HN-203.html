Discovery narrative

Source: Evansville Journal, p. 2, political letter citing an 1861 legislative committee report — original page.
Report: A newspaper contributor accused Indiana politician William H. Talbott of securing a Michigan City state-prison building contract through improper dealings. The article quoted a legislative committee as finding that competitors' bids had been suppressed, that $1,000 was paid in connection with withdrawing a rival bid, and that the state had lost $56,093.33 compared with an alternative proposal. The author also said the contract was surrendered after the inquiry.
Assessment: Partisan newspaper argument citing identifiable official material, not a newly adjudicated 1866 fraud. The committee's complete record and Talbott's response have not been independently read. Source-context follow-up open.
