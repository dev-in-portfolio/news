Recorded finding

Ferguson witness words and 'hands up' account disputed by competing witnesses and subsequent evidence.

Discovery narrative

Source: Jefferson Booster, p. 7 — original student column; DOJ federal shooting investigation.
Report: An opinion article about protests after Michael Brown's August 9 death opens with the widely circulated 'Hands up, don't shoot' account but labels it rumored and notes conflicting eyewitness statements.
Assessment: Do not convert the disputed slogan into a verified last quotation. The later DOJ investigation concluded the evidence did not support federal charges against officer Darren Wilson and found key surrender accounts inconsistent with credible evidence. That finding is distinct from DOJ's separate finding of systemic unconstitutional policing practices in Ferguson.
