Discovery narrative

Source: Muncie Times, p. 2 — original editorial; UN Security Council Resolution 1701.
Report: An editorial condemned civilian deaths and damage during the Israel–Hezbollah fighting and called for urgent international action to end attacks. It blamed multiple actors but clearly advocated a political position.
Assessment: Editorial assessment, not a final civilian-casualty investigation. The Security Council adopted Resolution 1701 on August 11, calling for cessation of hostilities and an expanded UN role; the newspaper's publication followed the resolution and cannot stand as a full cease-fire compliance assessment.
