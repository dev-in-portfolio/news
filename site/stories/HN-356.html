Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Semi-Weekly Republican, p. 2 — original page; Library of Congress historical context; Georgia National Guard original records.
Report: The paper described armed white mobs attacking Black Atlantans on streets and streetcars, with deaths, injuries, and the deployment of state troops during September 22–25. It framed the violence around widespread allegations that Black men assaulted white women, even as the article conceded that those killed had no shown connection to those accusations.
Assessment: The Atlanta race massacre is documented independently. The Library of Congress links it to inflammatory political and newspaper campaigns; the underlying sexual-assault allegations must not be adopted as proven causes or justifications for murder. Fatality totals varied in the press, and the victims were attacked by mobs, not convicted in court.
