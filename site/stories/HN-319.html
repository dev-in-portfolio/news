Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Journal, p. 1 — original page.
Report: Front-page dispatches from San Juan, St. Thomas, and London describe a hurricane that devastated Puerto Rico, damaged barracks holding American troops, and struck Montserrat and other Leeward Islands. The paper reported 64 recovered bodies at Humacao, with considerably more deaths feared elsewhere, and separately reproduced the Leeward governor's evolving account of damage on Montserrat.
Assessment: Distinct territories and separate dispatches are not one verified casualty total. The paper's numbers are urgent incomplete tallies. Follow-up open on each island's official records.
