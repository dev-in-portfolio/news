Recorded finding

Winecoff fire Medford Mail Tribune 8 December 1946 says 122 deaths; later standard figure is 119.

Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis Times, p. 1 — original page; U.S. Fire Administration, hotel-fire history.
Report: “122 Killed in Fire at Atlanta Hotel; Toll Mounts Hourlyâ€ described trapped occupants at the Winecoff, fatal jumps from windows, and an emergency response still in progress.
Assessment: Federal fire-history material records 119 deaths, not the paper's immediate 122. The building lacked sprinklers and had only one exit stairway. A shifting body count is a documented emergency-reporting error, not evidence that an unidentified additional three victims must have existed.
