Discovery narrative

Source: People's Pilot, p. 2, August 18 Nebraska dispatch — original page.
Report: A Rock Island accommodation train fell from a trestle near Lincoln, Nebraska, and burning wreckage trapped passengers; the article reported 24 bodies recovered. Officers arrested George Davis, describing circumstantial sightings of a crowbar near the track, and suggested that sabotage had caused the crash. In the same article, an unrelated Santa Fe head-on collision was reported separately with two deaths.
Assessment: Two different accidents on one page; no assumption that the first was deliberate or Davis guilty without subsequent proceedings. Initial casualty counts still need reconciliation. Follow-up open.
