Discovery narrative

Source: Jewish Post, p. 1 — original page; NTSB final accident finding.
Report: The paper stated that at least 13 of the 230 people aboard TWA Flight 800 were Jewish, possibly more as passenger information developed, and described rabbis traveling to assist bereaved relatives.
Assessment: The subgroup identification is explicitly provisional; 230 is the established total of people killed. The NTSB found that the aircraft broke up after a center-wing fuel-tank explosion; the precise ignition source was uncertain. This victims' community profile is not an accident-cause investigation.
