Discovery narrative

Source: Democratic Sentinel, p. 2, dispatch from Gunnison, Colorado — original page.
Report: An explosion at the Colorado Coal and Iron Company workings near Crested Butte wrecked the mine entrance and ventilation apparatus while a morning shift was underground. The article referred to 57 men trapped, six additional people killed, and other estimates of the total, while residents and rescue parties tried to reopen the workings. Mine inspector John McNeil was quoted as fearing all the trapped men had died. A firedamp ignition was suspected; officials said ventilation and inspection had been in place.
Assessment: First disaster account, not a final casualty roster or conclusive cause report. Its figures are not internally clean; do not add them into a purported final total. Official inspection and recovery lists require further research. Follow-up open.
