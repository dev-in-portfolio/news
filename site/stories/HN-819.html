Discovery narrative

Source: Jewish Post, p. 7 — original page.
Report: A short community profile introduced Lisa Silberstein, a 21-year-old U.S. Army computer and supply-communications specialist scheduled to deploy to Bosnia from Germany. The story connected her deployment with her family's earlier military service and religious observance abroad.
Assessment: A named individual's anticipated assignment, not a confirmation of every subsequent movement or a representative account of the NATO-led peace mission. The Bosnian agreement did not itself guarantee that reconciliation and enforcement were complete.
