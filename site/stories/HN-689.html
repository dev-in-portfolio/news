Discovery narrative

Source: Nappanee Advance-News, p. 1 — original page; National Archives, Watergate chronology.
Report: Reporting on a congressional election speech quoted John Brademas attacking the Nixon administration over the Watergate bugging affair and other disputed issues, including ITT and the wheat deal.
Assessment: Documented campaign criticism, not proof every accusation made in the speech was correct. The Watergate arrests occurred June 17, but the broader conspiracy and obstruction were established through later investigations, recordings, and proceedings. This small-town report shows the issue circulating outside Washington before those findings.
