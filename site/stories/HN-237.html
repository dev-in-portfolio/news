Discovery narrative

Source: Rensselaer Union, p. 2, reproducing and comparing Chicago newspaper accounts — original page.
Report: Burglars seeking the vault of McCoy & Thompson's bank in Rensselaer, Indiana, had allegedly worked for months on a concealed tunnel. Detectives led by William Turtle and Captain Bull watched the preparations, recovered elaborate tools, and arrested men identified as James Filkins and Martin Davis. The newspaper also described the suspected involvement and flight after bail of local saloon proprietor M. V. B. Warner; reported tunnel duration varies between versions.
Assessment: Detailed account of a thwarted heist, not a successful loss of the vault's money. Much of the narrative relies on the participating detectives' version of events; final dispositions and Warner's fate were not checked. Follow-up open.
