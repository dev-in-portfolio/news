Recorded finding

1880 Morey/Garfield letter was printed as genuine by some outlets and later retracted as forged.

Discovery narrative

Source: Indianapolis News, p. 1, reporting Judge Davis's ruling in New York — original page.
Report: In preliminary criminal-libel proceedings concerning the infamous Morey letter—a purported statement by James A. Garfield about Chinese immigration—Judge Davis stated that the letter was not written by Garfield and that evidence for the supposed addressee Henry L. Morey was lacking. He sharply criticized the decision to publish it and ordered accused journalist Kenward Philp held to answer.
Assessment: A contemporaneous judicial finding that the document was forged, not a verdict proving Philp personally produced it or assigning criminal guilt to every political figure who circulated it. The article explicitly left Philp's guilt to a jury. Document finding established; individual culpability open.
