Discovery narrative

Source: Jewish Post, p. 1 — original page; USHMM, Berlin Olympics.
Report: Under a headline about the Nazis' failure to benefit as hoped from the Games, the Jewish Post discussed Owens's four medals, Nazi racial ideology, and Jewish residents' fear that visible persecution would intensify after foreign Olympic visitors departed.
Assessment: This is especially valuable as contemporary Jewish community concern, not proof that every predicted measure took place immediately. The later intensification of Nazi antisemitism is historically established, while the paper's account of Hitler personally snubbing Owens should be distinguished from a fully reconstructed record of individual Olympic greetings.
